# Yankee Swap

For French-Canadian Celebrity Versions, Yankee Swap was combined with the OTEV competitions.

The players will compete in a series of rounds where they must try to score the highest number. The player with the lowest score each round will be eliminated from the game. If two or more players tie for the lowest score, they will play a tiebreaker round. Once they are eliminated, they must claim a prize which may contain money, vacations, advantages in the game, punishments, the power of veto, or the HOH key. The players that are eliminated in later rounds will have the option of keeping the prize that they have received or trading it for other prizes that have already been revealed. The player with the power of veto (or HOH key) in their possession at the end of the final round will win POV or HOH.

Big Brother 11 (US) is the only season where this competition was played as a Head of Household competition. This competition was originally going to be a Veto competition, however, because of Chima Simone's expulsion, it was later played as an HOH competition instead.
Big Brother 8 (US) is the only season where none of the prizes and punishments were traded.
Big Brother 8 (US) is also the only season where the Power of Veto was awarded last. In all of the subsequent seasons that featured this competition the veto was awarded first.
Danick Martineau is the only person to win this competition as a Power of Veto and go onto win the game.
Ryan Quicksall, Libra Thompson, Judd Daugherty, Caleb Reynolds, Elena Davies, Tyler Crispen, Matt Klotz, Sarah Miller, Marie-Christine Lavoie and Jean-Thomas Jobin won the competition in their respective seasons, however, they choose not to take the Power of Veto and took the prizes instead.
Angela Rummans from Big Brother 20 (US) was eliminated the earliest from the competition to still win the Power of Veto, being eliminated in the 2nd round.
Of the Canadian Players, Allison White was eliminated the earliest and won the POV. She was eliminated in the 3rd round in Big Brother Canada 2.
Of the French-Canadian players, Fabiola Aladin was eliminated in the 1st round and still won the POV.
Jeff Schroeder, Jordan Lloyd, James Huling, Paul Abrahamian, and Frédérique Turgeon have played this competition twice. Dani Briones and Nicole Franzel have played this competition three times.
Jeff and James made it to the final round twice.
Paul was eliminated in the first round and received a punishment both times they played.
Dani won once and lost the other two times.
Nicole lost this competition all three times and received a Unitard from it twice.
The one time she did not receive a Unitard, she was already wearing one as a different punishment, and her showmance Corey Brooks received the Unitard instead.
Dan Gheesling, Brendon Villegas, and Coco Belliveau were present for this competition twice but never played. Britney Haynes and Liliane Blanco-Binette played this competition on her first season but didn't play it on their second season, while Rachel Reilly played this competition on their first seasons but didn't play it on their second or third seasons. Jean-Thomas Jobin and Mona de Grenoble didn't play on their first season but played on their second.
The earliest week that this competition was played was in Week 2 during Big Brother 8 (US), while the latest week that this competition was played was in Week 8 during Big Brother 18 (US), Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 3, and Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 4.
Jessica Milagros, Britini D'Angelo, Eddy King, Kyle Capener, and Katherine Woodman are the only people that won both the competition and the HOH or POV and have never made it to the final five.
Big Brother 28 (US) is the first US civilian season that this competition was not played since its introduction.
