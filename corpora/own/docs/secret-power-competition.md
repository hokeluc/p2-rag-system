# Secret Power Competition

The

In Big Brother 22 (US), all houseguests in the game during Week 5 were given the opportunity to compete for one of three special powers: The Disruptor, The Replay, and The Blocker. They would be won by David Alexander, Dani Briones and Christmas Abbott respectively.

In Big Brother 25 (US), America voted for four houseguests to compete for the BB Power of Invincibility in Week 4. Cirie Fields, Cory Wurtenberger, Jag Bains and Matt Klotz were chosen by America, and Matt would go on to win the power.

In Big Brother 26 (US), any houseguest who voted "yes" to AINSLEY's morality test during the premiere was given the opportunity to compete for in a special "Upgrade Competition". The houseguests were split into two different premiere groups, with one chance to vote in AINSLEY's test for each group. In the first group, Angela Murray, Joseph Rodriguez, Makensy Manbeck and Rubina Bernabe passed the test, while only Leah Peters and Quinn Martin did so in the second group. Makensy and Quinn would go onto win their respective competitions, earning themselves America's Veto and the Deepfake HOH powers respectively.

In Big Brother 27 (US), the houseguests had to split up during the premiere into two different groups. Each group had the opportunity to compete for a game changing power. Zach Cornell was in the first group that had to rescue Julie from The Mastermind. He won $10,000 publicly, and it was later privately revealed to him that he has the ability to use this money to buy their way off the block as a final nominee by bribing the HOH. Jimmy Heagerty was in the second group that had to reclaim the HOH Relic, and he won power was to publicly choose which four houseguests would be eligible to play in the first HOH. Unlike the other powers, his had no added secret benefit.

The competition would appear once again in BB27, during Week 2 in a similar format to BB22. Within The Black Box competition, one houseguest would win HOH while other powers would be up for grabs. Keanu Soto won a Veto Advantage, Kelley Jorgensen won the chance to earn a second POV, and Mickey Lee won an HOH Takeover.
