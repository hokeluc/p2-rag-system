The Bond is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

In the first week of summer, Adrian Rocha, Ava Pearl, Will Williams, and Zach Cornell began talks of forming an alliance. Zach proposed expanding their numbers to include Lauren Domingue and Vince Panaro. This new alliance officially came together on Day 9, after a meeting in the Head of Household room. Ava is credited with naming the group "The Bond," a nod to James Bond. The group anticipated being divided on the first eviction vote due to their late formation. However, they committed to working together moving forward.
