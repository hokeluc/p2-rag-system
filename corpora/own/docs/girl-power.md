Girl Power, also known as The Angels, was an alliance on Big Brother 4 (US).

Girl Power was created very late into the season, after Erika Landin and Jack Owens, Jr. were nominated by Jee Choe, Alison Irwin and Jun Song, who were already in an alliance, were the swing votes. The remaining two members of the Three Stooges, were targeting Erika. Alison and Jun decided to keep Erika in the house, and create Girl Power, going against the Three Stooges plan, and evicted Jack.

After Jun won Head of Household, the alliance was successfully able to keep Jee and Robert Roman on the block, and they evicted Jee, seeing him as a greater overall threat. The worst-case scenario happened after Robert won HoH. This meant one of the girls would be evicted that week. Robert wanted to keep Erika safe. However, Alison and Jun had a previously made final two deal and planned on evicting Erika if they won the POV. Robert nominated Alison and Jun. After Alison won the POV, Alison cast the sole vote to evict on Erika. After Alison won part one, and Jun won part two, the girls were guaranteed to make it to the final two. This led to an infamous moment where the competition came down to a tie-breaker and both girls tried to throw it. Jun ended up throwing it better and Alison became the final HoH and cast the sole vote to evict on Robert. On Finale Night, Jun became the winner of Big Brother 4 (US), winning by a vote of 6-1.

All women in the alliances hold the highest-placing positions among all women in the house, as Erika got fourth place, Alison landed on the runner-up position and Jun won the season.
