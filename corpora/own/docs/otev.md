# OTEV

For French-Canadian Celebrity Versions, OTEV was combined with the Yankee Swap competitions.

OTEV is a musical chairs-style competition played in rounds, with one person being eliminated each round until there is only one person left.

1. The playing area features the OTEV statue with five (to begin with) kneeling spots in front of it at the top of a platform only accessible by a slippery ramp. Surrounding this are several different zones filled with the item of the challenge.
2. The six (or however many players there are left) kneel before the OTEV at the beginning of each round. There is one less kneeling spot than there are players left.
3. OTEV will sing a song or recite a rhyme describing, depending on the season:
  1. A previously evicted houseguest.
  2. Two previously evicted houseguests.
  3. A previously played competition.
  4. The day for a specific event in the house.
  5. A specific event.
4. When OTEV says "Go", the players must rush down the ramp and look for the item displaying, depending on the season:
  1. The name of the houseguest OTEV described.
  2. A mashup of the names of the two houseguests OTEV described.
  3. The name of the competition OTEV described.
  4. Numbers for the day of the event.
  5. The number of people who participated in the event.
5. Once the player has found the correct item, they head back up the ramp and kneel on one of the kneeling spots. One player will be left without a spot to kneel on. If the player grabbed the wrong item, they are allowed to leave the kneeling spot to go back and try to find the right one, as long as they're not the last person up the ramp.
6. If all of the players on the kneeling spots brought back the correct item, the last person to return will be eliminated.
7. If one of the players brought back the wrong item and the person not on a kneeling spot brought back the right item, the person who brought back the wrong item will be eliminated.
8. Once a player has been eliminated, a new round begins.
9. The last person standing wins the Power of Veto.

The character of OTEV changes his form every season to match with the theme of the season or competition, but is always the "host" of the competition. In recent years, he has often decided to spray the houseguests with whatever substance he has available to kick off the competition.

In Quebec, the character of OTEV remains the same every season but with a new outfit. He also takes on The Zingbot's role of roasting the houseguests.

OTEV is a play on the word "veto", as "OTEV" is "veto" backwards.
The voice of OTEV has occasionally been provided by a special guest.
During Big Brother 11, formerly evicted houseguest Casey Turner was the voice of OTEV.
During Big Brother 16, comedian Gilbert Gottfried voiced OTEV.
Jordan Lloyd, Frank Eudy, Kevin Campbell, Enzo Palumbo, Nicole Franzel, Tyler Crispen, Emmanuel Auger, Maxime Landry, Kevin Lapierre, Richardson Zéphir, Jean-Thomas Jobin, Frédérique Turgeon, Rachel Reilly, and Angela Murray are the only houseguests to compete in this competition multiple times. Enzo, Tyler, and Richardson all won the first time they competed.
Nicole is the only person to compete in this competition three times, but she has never won it.
Both times that Frank competed, he had been on the block and was evicted that week.
Christmas Abbott, Cody Calafiore, Daniele Briones, James Huling, Camille Felton, Kim Clavel, Liliane Blanco-Binette, Coco Belliveau, and Mona de Grenoble were present for this competition twice. Dani and Liliane played the first time, while Cody, James, Kim, and Mona played on the second time. Camille and Coco never played.
Christmas was selected to play during her first season, but because of her injury was forced to sit out, and she was not selected to play on her second.
François Lambert was present for this competition three times. He won the first time but was not selected for his second or third.
Emmanuel Auger, Maxime Landry, Kevin Lapierre, and Richardson Zéphir are the only houseguests to compete in OTEV twice on one season.
Tommy Bracco is the first openly LGBT houseguest to win this competition. He was followed by Michael Bruner and Mona de Grenoble.
Zach Rance won OTEV prior. However, he did not come out as bisexual until later.
The Big Brother 26 edition of OTEV was the shortest ever, as every contestant except for the winner, Makensy Manbeck, was eliminated after getting the first question wrong.
Big Brother 10 is the only season since Big Brother 7 to not feature an OTEV competition.
Dan Gheesling is the only contestant from Big Brother 10 to compete in OTEV. Memphis Garrett was present for this competition on Big Brother 22 but was not selected to compete.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 1 is the only season to feature two OTEV competitions.
Big Brother 18 and Big Brother 24 are tied for the earliest appearance of OTEV in the United States, occurring during week 5 of both seasons.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 1 has the earliest appearance of OTEV worldwide, occurring in week 2.
Currently, Cody Calafiore, Jag Bains, and Danick Martineau are the only people who have won OTEV and then gone on to win the game. Throughout the fandom this was known as the OTEV Curse.
Ryan Quicksall, Dan Gheesling, Liz Nolan, Tyler Crispen, François Lambert, and Makensy Manbeck are the next closest, as they all got 2nd place on the season they won OTEV.
Janelle Pierzina, Jeff Schroeder, Tyler Crispen, Enzo Palumbo, and François Lambert all won OTEV and lost the game on one season, and later lost all subsequent seasons they played in.
Dan is the only winner prior to Cody to have also won OTEV, but he did not play OTEV on the season he won.
