Fatal Five was an alliance on Big Brother 18 (US).

The Fatal Five was created as they were the girls inside the 8-Pack alliance, serving as an extra side alliance to benefit the members.

The Fatal Five seemed to be in a good position until conflict between Da'Vonne Rogers and Tiffany Rousso created cracks in the alliance. After Da’Vonne began to worry about Tiffany being too emotional of a player, she relayed her concerns to Nicole Franzel and Zakiyah Everette. They agreed and began to distance themselves from Tiffany.

After a fight between Tiffany and Frank Eudy occurred, Da'Vonne began to attempt to pit the two against each other. After Da'Vonne and Michelle Meyer helped save Tiffany and evict Bronte D'Acquisto, their tension continued to grow. However, after Frank and Tiffany talked and realized Da'Vonne was playing them, Frank and Tiffany began to expose Da'Vonne’s game to the house and target her.

This caused Nicole to defect from the alliance in favor of aligning with Corey Brooks and Paulie Calafiore. After Tiffany won the Roadkill Competition, Tiffany targeted Da'Vonne and nominated her. Despite campaigning from Tiffany and Frank, Tiffany was evicted by a vote of 8-0-0. After Paul Abrahamian won HOH, Nicole helped in targeting Da'Vonne. Despite campaigns from the other Fatal Five members, Da'Vonne was evicted by a vote of 6-2. The alliance continued to crumble after Victor Arroyo nominated Michelle Meyer and Zakiyah. After the house realized that Paulie was controlling the house, James Huling used his power from America's Care Package to evict Zakiyah.

After Zakiyah was evicted and Michelle attempted to call out Nicole during her eviction speech, the alliance was officially over.

Nicole was the last member to be nominated.
Nicole was the only member to win an HOH competition. (America gifted Michelle the Co-HOH prize.)
