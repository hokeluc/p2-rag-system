The Minutemen is the alliance between Jag Bains and Matt Klotz on Big Brother 25 (US) that would form the core of the dominant Mafia alliance, alongside third member Bowie Jane Ball. With help from Bowie Jane's three Head of Household wins, The Minutemen would make up the final 2 after evicting her the round prior.

On Day 38, Matt Klotz and Jag Bains solidified their loyalties to each other and named their alliance The Minutemen. They were inspired by The Hitmen, a dominant alliance in Big Brother 16 (US).

Once the game hit the jury phase, the alliance began their domination over the competitions, and would hold control over the house by winning the POV every week. Additionally, the only HOHs they would lose were won by affiliate (and Mafia member) Bowie Jane Ball, who was easily persuaded to act in their best interests, instead of her own.

When Jag won the final HOH, he decided to stay loyal to The Minutemen and take Matt to the final 2. After a strong performance in his speech and jury questioning, Jag won in a 5-2 vote over Matt.
