# Safety Suite Competition

Similar to the Immunity Competition, the winner of the competition would get safety for the week but he or she must pick a "Plus One", making two people immune. The Plus One would receive a punishment in exchange for being safe. The current HOH is ineligible to enter the Safety Suite, and a houseguest can only enter the Safety Suite once before being ineligible for future rounds. If only one houseguest enters the Safety Suite, they will not be guaranteed safety - they will still have to complete the competition before their time runs out in order to win safety.

It is unknown what happens if no one chooses to compete in the safety suite.

This twist lasted for the first 3 weeks of that season.

Kaysar Ridha and Janelle Pierzina played in the first Safety Suite Competition. The competition was Don't Miss A Beat and that competition was won by Kaysar Ridha. He would then save Janelle Pierzina. As a +1, Janelle's punishment was to wear the All Star "Mascot" costume for the rest of the week.

Bayleigh Dayton, Christmas Abbott, Cody Calafiore, Da'Vonne Rogers, David Alexander, Ian Terry and Kevin Campbell played in the second Safety Suite Competition. The competition was Gettin' Tipsy and that competition was won by Christmas Abbott. She would then save Ian Terry. As a +1, Ian's punishment was to wear the BB’s All-Star Wars costume for the rest of the week.

Daniele Briones, Enzo Palumbo, Memphis Garrett and Nicole Franzel played in the third Safety Suite Competition. The competition was Stealing Safety and that competition was won by Enzo Palumbo. He would then save Christmas Abbott. As a +1, Christmas's punishment was the All Star is Born punishment for the rest of the week.
