Jackpot was an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created in Week 1. The alliance consists of Claire Rehfuss, Sarah Beth Steagall and Tiffany Mitchell.

In Week 1, Sarah Beth, Claire, and Tiffany bonded well and formed an alliance called Jackpot. Soon after its creation, Sarah Beth told her other closest ally, Kyland Young, about the alliance. When Kyland revealed to Tiffany that Sarah Beth had disclosed the alliance, both Tiffany and Claire began to distrust Sarah Beth. In Week 4, Sarah Beth made several attempts to convince the house to target Hannah Chaddha, whom Tiffany was growing closer to at the time. This created an even larger rift in the alliance and led Tiffany and Claire to quietly distance themselves from Sarah Beth. In Week 5, when Derek Xiao nominated Christian Birkenberger and Sarah Beth, Tiffany strongly considered flipping the vote to target Sarah Beth instead of Christian; Claire agreed that she would not mind flipping as well. However, both Tiffany and Claire ultimately voted to evict Christian instead.

In Week 7, Sarah Beth won Head of Household. Unaware that Tiffany was no longer loyal to her, Sarah Beth told Tiffany that she wanted Jackpot to go far in the game and that her true loyalties remained with them. While Sarah Beth was in power, Tiffany and Claire pretended they were still loyal to Jackpot. Sarah Beth nominated Claire as a pawn and successfully evicted Derek X. In Week 8, Tiffany won HOH and officially severed ties with the alliance by nominating Sarah Beth alongside Kyland. When Claire won the Secret HOH power (dethroning Tiffany), she chose to stay loyal to her pair alliance with Tiffany rather than Jackpot, keeping Sarah Beth and Kyland on the block and effectively ending the alliance. Sarah Beth considered revealing the existence of Jackpot to the rest of the house before her eviction to sabotage Claire and Tiffany's games, but she ultimately kept quiet.
