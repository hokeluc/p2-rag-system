The Judges was the dominant alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

Originally formed on shaky ground in Week 6 as part of a plan to save Morgan Pope, The Judges dominated the post-jury competitions despite members Rachel Reilly and Will Williams being evicted shortly after. In the endgame, the alliance operated with Morgan gluing Vince Panaro and Ashley Hollis together until they comprised the final three. After Morgan and Vince won the previous four HOH competitions, Ashley won the final HOH, evicted Morgan, and defeated Vince in the jury vote, securing the alliance's overall victory.

After Rachel Reilly became the Head of Household for Week 6, she nominated Mickey Lee, Morgan Pope, and Vince Panaro for eviction, perceiving them to directly be the three largest threats to her game, although Ashley Hollis, her closest ally, pushed for another notable social threat in Rylie Jeffries to be nominated. With Ashley's assistance, Vince convinced Lauren Domingue to use the Power of Veto on him, and Rachel subsequently replaced him with Rylie.

Despite the fact that Morgan was nominated by Rachel and the two had a feud the week before, Ashley suggested that she and Rachel should bring Morgan over to their side in order to have both Morgan and Vince as numbers. By adding Will Williams into the mix, the five formed "The Judges" alliance – a name coined by Morgan based on the idea that they would decide each eviction. Although the state of the alliance looked bleak after Mickey won the BB Block Buster that week, The Judges were able to stick together and sway Will's closest ally Ava Pearl to fulfill Ashley’s plan and evict Rylie over Morgan in a narrow 5-4 vote.

Despite their massive success in evicting Rylie, Ava soon soured on Rachel due to the pressure of voting to evict him and became a looser number. Vince expressed reluctance towards evicting Rylie that week and naming Katherine Woodman over Rachel as his replacement nominee during his HOH reign the following week, but the outsiders' influence on Vince proved unsuccessful, and Katherine was evicted by a 7-1 vote in Week 7. Keanu Soto, one of these outsiders, won HOH for Week 8 and nominated Morgan, Vince, and Ashley at different points in the week, but it was Mickey who was evicted by a 6-1 vote.

The White Locust twist at the start of Week 9 was a near-catastrophe for the five. Ava won safety, and chose Vince to compete in the Mastermind's Maniacal Maze competition. Vince chose Lauren, Lauren chose Morgan, and Morgan chose Rachel to compete next after all three survived their attempts. Rachel was unable to complete the competition within the allotted time frame and was immediately eliminated from the game, in a colossal blow to The Judges.

By virtue of finishing the White Locust competition in the fastest time, Lauren became the Week 9 HOH and nominated Morgan, Ashley, and Will, with Keanu as her backdoor target. Lauren infamously threw the BB Comics POV to Morgan, and Keanu's Block Buster win sent Will home in a 3-2 vote after a hard push by Ashley to be saved by Morgan and Vince, meaning the alliance now operated as a trio.

With two of their members evicted in a row, Vince won HOH during Week 10 and nominated Ava, Keanu, and Kelley Jorgensen. After winning the POV and viewing Lauren as a major threat to her game, Morgan attempted to have Vince name Lauren as the replacement nominee. She went back and forth on deciding whether to use the POV on Ava, in order to form another ally, or Keanu, who picked her to play in the POV competition. Morgan subsequently used it on Ava and forced Vince's hand into nominating his closest ally, Lauren, instead of Ashley. Nonetheless, Lauren won the Block Buster and Kelley was evicted by a 3-2 vote with Vince casting the tiebreaker vote due to The Judges' needing to split up the Flower Power trio of Kelley, Ava, and Lauren.

Morgan won HOH during the Double Eviction round and nominated Keanu and Lauren. Keanu won the POV, leading Morgan to nominate Ava in his place. Lauren was evicted by a 2-1 vote, with Ashley and Keanu voting to evict Lauren and Vince voting to evict Ava. Keanu was evicted in a 2-0 vote by Ashley and Morgan during Vince's fourth HOH, and finally, Morgan won both the HOH and POV that same week, with Vince evicting Ava over Ashley at the final 4.

With Ava's eviction, the three remaining Judges had managed to make it to finale night and the alliance officially dissolved. Morgan won Part 1 of the Final HOH competition, and Ashley won Part 2 following a fatal error by Vince. Ashley convinced Morgan to backstabbed her number one ally Vince had she won Part 3, but Ashley won instead and evicted Morgan as the sole vote due to her expansive physical résumé. Ashley's strategic and social gameplay was able to convince a jury against Vince's untidy gameplay and she won the game in a 6-1 vote.
