The Final Four was an alliance in Big Brother 18 (US), consisting of Corey Brooks, Nicole Franzel, Paul Abrahamian, & Victor Arroyo. When Nicole won HOH in week 10, her and Corey wondered who their target should be. Wanting to be in the middle of the clashing duos and keep their promise to James and Natalie, Nicole and Corey decided to work with Paul and Victor and target Michelle, who had been actively gunning for Nicole for weeks. Though Jatalie was oblivious to this, after Nicole won the veto and kept the nominations the same, they were still convinced that Michelle would stay and were blindsided when she was evicted.

Following Michelle's eviction, James and Natalie felt betrayed by Nicole and Corey and wanted to target them. However, in a close race, Victor won HOH, solidifying his allowance with Nicorey. Although Paul and Victor wanted James gone initially, Nicole convinced them that Natalie was the bigger threat and ultimately targeted her. Corey won the following HOH and at final five, him and Nicole decided that they had to break the Final Four alliance in order to advance themselves further. This was a done deal when Nicole won the veto and caused Victor's third eviction.
