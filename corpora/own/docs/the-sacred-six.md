The Sacred Six, aka the BB-luminati, was an alliance on Big Brother 20 that consisted of Angie Lantry (aka Rockstar), Bayleigh Dayton, Chris Williams (aka Swaggy C), Faysal Shafaat, Haleigh Broucher, and Kaitlyn Herman, which was an expansion of the FOUTTE alliance.

The Sacred Six was formed in Week 1.

Bayleigh is the only member of this alliance to win a Power App from the BB App Store while the twist was in play.
