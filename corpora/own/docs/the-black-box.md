# The Black Box

The competition is named "Black Box" due to the complete darkness inside the room, which significantly increases the difficulty as houseguests must navigate without being able to see.

A similar variation of this competition is called The Haunting, where houseguests are usually provided with headlamps to help guide them through the dark environment.

In this competition, houseguests enter a completely dark “Black Box.” They must locate specific items in the darkness and return them to designated spots. The houseguest (or team) that secures the highest number of items in the shortest time wins.

Big Brother 18 (US): Each houseguest had to keep one hand on a button while searching. Failure to return in time forced that player to leave the Black Box.
Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US): Players crawled through a mock restaurant and raced to escape in the fastest time.
Big Brother 20 (US): Houseguests hunted for folders—one fewer than the number of competitors. The player left without a folder received a punishment. One special folder granted its finder automatic advancement to the next round; any other folder simply avoided the punishment.
Big Brother 21 (US): Players searched the darkness for the true exit while facing multiple decoy exits. The last to find the correct exit was evicted on Day 1.

Big Brother 15 (US): Debuted as a Have/Have-Not Competition. Andy Herren, McCrae Olson, and Spencer Clawson won, becoming Haves for the week.
Big Brother 16 (US): Returned as a Battle of the Block competition. Christine Brecht and Donny Thompson won, dethroning HoH Derrick Levasseur—despite Christine’s attempt to throw the comp.
Big Brother 18 (US): Served as the Head of Household competition. Natalie Negrotti defeated Corey Brooks by four seconds to claim her first HoH.
Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US): Used as the Power of Veto competition; James Maslow secured his first win of the season.
Big Brother 20 (US): Featured on opening night. Half the cast competed; Angela Rummans won and advanced to the Immunity Competition, while last-place Kaycee Clark received a Unitard punishment.
Big Brother 21 (US): After Jackson Michie became Camp Director, he banished four houseguests—Cliff Hogg III, David Alexander, Jessica Milagros, and Kemi Fakunle—to a Hit the Road version of the Black Box. Cliff and Kemi escaped; Jessica edged out David, causing his Day 1 eviction.
Big Brother 22 (US): Returned as the “BB Basement” twist. All houseguests searched for three Power Icons and placed them on matching podiums:
David Alexander — Disruptor power
Dani Briones — Replay power
Christmas Abbott — Blocker power
