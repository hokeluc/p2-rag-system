The Love Triangle is an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

Early in the game, Lyric and Rome were attached due to a budding showmance. Jason was also very friendly with Lyric and had a strong relationship with Rome, leading to the creation of the alliance.

After Devens' HoH win in week 2, Rome discussed with Devens that he would not be comfortable with him or his closest allies Lyric or Jason being nominated. Earlier in the week, Jason was plotting against both Angela and Devens with Drew, who relayed this information back to the Crossovers alliance. Angela pushed for them to be initial nominees with Rome being a replacement option should the veto be used. Devens agreed and nominated Lyric and Jason alongside pawn Melody for eviction and explicitly recalled his 1-on-1 conversation with Rome to be a part of the reason why he made this decision. During the nomination ceremony, La Trice began to cry as she was hurt that Rome excluded her from his deal with Devens. Angela (in her egg costume) additionally and exposed Jason's trickery at the nomination ceremony, causing a large fight. After the nomination ceremony, Lyric and Rome agreed to pretend to "break up", hoping this would reduce the target on both of them.

At the veto competition, Devens was narrowly able to outpace Jason to win the Power of Veto. Lyric campaigned to have the veto used on her, and was successful in her endeavors, as Devens believed Lyric and Rome's break up ploy. At the ceremony, Devens used the veto on Lyric and nominated Rome as the replacement and target. The majority of the house wanted Rome gone should he not win the Blockbuster; Rome lost the Blockbuster against Jason and is swiftly evicted in a vote of 12-1, with Taylor being the sole vote to save him against Melody.

After Rome's eviction, Kamu won the HoH competition and was set on targeting players not in the Toolshed alliance, specifically Mallory and Jason due to competition strength. However, he is convinced by Drew to not initially nominate Jason and instead use him as a replacement to prevent him from playing in the veto competition. Kamu agreed and nominated Lyric, La Trice, and Mallory for eviction.

With Lyric winning the Power of Veto, Kamu crafted an idea to "expose" Jason's position in the house during Mallory's tea party. He used the excuse that Jason promised Kamu that he would not use the veto if he were picked to play and would keep Kamu's nominations the same, but went to each of the nominees and told them he would use the veto on them. During the tea party, Kamu and Jason argued in front of the other houseguests, with Jason objecting that he did try to work with the Red Corner and that Kamu would not name who told him information about Jason. After the confrontation, Jason retrieved a tea kettle and spoon from the kitchen, vowing to get revenge against Kamu.

The following day, Jason bangs the tea kettle and spoon to whenever he sees players "strategizing" to alert Kamu. He does this throughout the day and then is named the replacement nominee at the Veto Ceremony after Lyric removes herself from the block. Jason failed to win the BB Blockbuster competition against Mallory and is evicted unanimously in a vote of 12-0 against La Trice. After being evicted, he is greeted by Julie with her own kettle and spoon.

After Jason's eviction, Lyric made a deal with Haley during the HoH competition that they would not nominate each other in the event either of them won. After Haley's win, she initially honored the deal by nominated Drew, Taylor, and Melody. Lyric tried to woo Chuk and Kamu to stay off the block in case the veto was used, knowing her allies were being evicted and jumping ship from her Giggle Girls alliance. While this initially seemed to be effective, Taylor won the veto and was set to use it, prompting Haley to consider using Lyric as the replacement nominee.

After being warned by multiple houseguests that she was a consideration, Lyric revealed to Chuk and Kamu that Mallory would nominate them, hoping that would convince Haley to nominate Mallory instead. This had the opposite effect, as after campaigning to Haley directly, Haley chastised Lyric for throwing her "best friends" in the house under the bus. Lyric was named the replacement nominee after Taylor used the veto but was promised by the Red Corner that she was the pawn and would be safe regardless of who won the BB Blockbuster.

Throughout the week, Lyric seemed to be the most safe of the three nominees. However, the day of the eviction, Dee and Devens discussed the possibility of evicting Lyric regardless of who she was next to on the block. They believed Lyric would be more loyal to Haley, Chuk and Kamu as opposed to Melody and Drew who would target the trio instead. They were able to get Angela on board, as she believed that Drew, the initial target, could be repeatedly targeted in future weeks. Drew and Barrett were easily convinced to join the plan and Melody was able to convince Mallory to vote for her against Lyric. Taylor and La Trice were on board to vote "with the house".

Drew won the BB Blockbuster, and he along with Devens, Dee, Angela, Barrett, Mallory, Taylor, and La Trice voted to evict Lyric, blindsiding Haley, Chuk, Kamu, and Yash in an 8-3 vote, leading to the collapse of the alliance as one of the worst alliances in Big Brother history, with all three members evicted back to back to back with an average placement of 15th.
