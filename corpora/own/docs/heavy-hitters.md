Heavy Hitters is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The "Heavy Hitters" alliance formed during the season's first week. Jimmy Heagerty and Zach Cornell initiated discussions to create a large alliance with Morgan Pope, Rachel Reilly, Rylie Jeffries, and Vince Panaro. They invited each potential member to a meeting in the boxing room, which led to the alliance's official formation. They believed uniting strong competitors would be a strategic move, as no one would suspect their collaboration, hence the alliance name.

In the Diary Room, Rachel Reilly privately revealed her true intentions with the alliance and to evict fellow members "one by one." She stated her real loyalty lay with Amy Bingham, with whom she had previously formed The Mom Squad.
