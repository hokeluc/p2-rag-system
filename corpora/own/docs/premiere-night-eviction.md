# Premiere Night Eviction

The twist requires houseguests to compete for safety during the premiere episode(s). Throughout the night, several houseguests will secure safety, leaving a few vulnerable. In most versions, either a group of houseguests or a single individual must choose who gets evicted, resulting in at least one houseguest leaving the house on the very first night.

On night one, the houseguests were divided into eight pairs who would play together for the season. The pairs competed in the inaugural Power Couple competition, where the winning couple immediately evicted another pair from the game. Jen Diturno and Parker Somerville won the competition and evicted Jacob Heald and Sharon Obermueller on Day 3.

On premiere night, twelve new houseguests and four returning coaches competed in the first challenge, "Hit The Road". The three top-scoring teams earned immunity, while the losing team was unprotected. Team Dan finished last, leaving Dan Gheesling to choose which of his three players to evict. He decided to evict Jodi Rollins on opening night.

On night one, houseguests were divided into teams chosen by four returning players. The next day, the four teams competed in another "Hit The Road" competition. As the challenge progressed, three teams won safety, leaving the fourth to fight among themselves for survival. Glenn Garcia lost the final challenge and was evicted from the game on Day 2.

On night one, it was revealed that a Big Brother Swap twist would send one of the new houseguests home, with Paul Abrahamian (a veteran) taking their place. Paul was allowed to grant safety to eight players, forcing the other eight to compete in a "Hit The Road" challenge. At the end, three houseguests remained unsaved and the house voted among them to evict Cameron Heard on Night 1.

Houseguests voted for Jackson Michie to become Camp Director on the first night, granting him safety for the week. He then had to banish four fellow houseguests, who faced off in the "Hit The Road" competition. David Alexander lost the competition and was evicted on the first night, although he later became eligible for Camp Comeback.

On launch night, the public voted for five housemates, each to stand behind a podium assigned either a prize or a punishment. After three "timebombs" went off, two housemates remained. One of the final two bombs contained an eviction notice, while the other was empty. Simon Gross drew the eviction bomb and was evicted on opening night, but later was allowed to return.

Jodi Rollins and Cameron Heard have the shortest stays in Big Brother US history. Jodi spent about six hours in the house, while Cameron lasted approximately eleven hours.
Glenn Garcia's eviction was the only one to take place on Day 2 instead of opening night.
Big Brother 19 (US) and Big Brother 21 (US) are the only US seasons with this twist that did not divide houseguests into pairs or teams on opening night.
Big Brother 9 (US) was the first season to feature this twist without returning players.
Frank Eudy and Paul Abrahamian are the only houseguests to have been present for two premiere night evictions in Big Brother US.
The premiere night eviction twist is highly controversial among fans and former houseguests, as many consider it unfair for someone to go through months of casting and sequester only to be eliminated in the first few days. Jodi's eviction is the most infamous case, and is often—incorrectly—thought to be the first instance of the twist.
From the US version, Jodi is the only person evicted on premiere night who did not get a guaranteed chance to return.
No US houseguest evicted on premiere night has ever returned to the game via a twist.
Jacob and Sharon were both sequestered for a potential re-entry twist via house vote, but due to Neil's voluntary exit, Joshuah Welch picked Sharon as his new partner, allowing her to return while Jacob lost the public vote for re-entry.
It was stated that if all four coaches had declined entering the main game in Big Brother 14 (US), there would have been a pre-jury re-entry twist; however, Jodi was not sequestered and, therefore, had no chance to return.
Glenn and Cameron both competed in a Battle Back Competition on their original seasons but failed to re-enter the house.
After eviction, David Alexander was allowed to live in the house as part of Camp Comeback (Days 15–30) for a chance to battle back in. He ultimately did not return to the game.
David has spent the most time in the house, during a single season, of any US premiere night evictee (excluding Sharon, who returned under different circumstances).
David is the first, and so far only, US premiere night evictee to return for a future season; he later competed on Big Brother 22 (US) and finished in 8th place.
