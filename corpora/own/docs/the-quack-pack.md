The Quack Pack was an alliance on Big Brother 14 (US).

The Quack Pack was formed week four, during Danielle Murphree’s HOH, when Britney Haynes decided to bring Ian Terry as an addition to the Team Touché alliance.

The alliance started off strong, successfully backdooring power player Janelle Pierzina whilst making a side alliance with the other side of the house to ensure their safety. After Frank Eudy won HOH, the alliance was kept safe due and Wil Heuser was evicted. After Shane Meaney won HOH, the alliance decided to turn on and target Frank and Mike to make the first move against the other side of the house. Shane decided to nominate them alongside each other. This angered the duo as they fought to stay in the house. However to no avail as Mike was evicted. During the Double Eviction the alliance gained power once again with Ian Terry winning HOH. After Frank won the POV, the alliance decided to evict his best ally, Ashley Iocco.

Following Ashley’s eviction, outsider Frank won HOH. He originally targeted Dan Gheesling whom he blamed for his allies' eviction. He decided to nominate Dan and Danielle Murphree. Due to Pandora's Box, Ian gained an additional POV. With this in mind the alliance made a plan to throw the veto to Britney Haynes or Shane to force Frank to nominate a houseguest outside of the alliance. However in the POV competition after Dan went against the plan and other outsider Jenn Arroyo won the POV, the alliance was angered. It didn’t help that Dan had to do a 24-hour solitary confinement punishment. However, Dan had one last trick. After finishing his punishment Dan decided to host a house meeting which he called his “funeral”. In which he threw Britney and Ian under the bus to Frank and Jenn. After giving a huge pitch to the both of them, he was successful as Jenn saved Dan and Frank targeted Britney. Ultimately, Britney was evicted by a vote of 4-1, being the first member of the alliance to be evicted.

Following Britney’s eviction, Ian won HOH. Despite his best ally being evicted last week. He still decided he wanted to keep the quack pack and he nominated Frank and Jenn. Despite Dan and Danielle making a deal with the duo, they went back on it and finally evicted Frank. However it was announced that it was a double eviction. Dan went in to win HOH. Originally secretly targeting Ian for being a big threat. His plan was foiled as Ian won the POV. Instead he opted to evict Joe Arvin. Following Joe’s eviction, Ian won HOH. He decided to continue to remain loyal to The Quack Pack. He nominated Shane and Jenn. Although Dan had other plans to evict Shane. Once again his plans were foiled after Shane won the POV. Dan seated to evict Jenn.

After Jenn was evicted, it was official The Quack Pack had reached the end and one of them was going to win. After Shane and Danielle’s eviction, Ian won by a final jury vote of 6-1, being crowned the winner of Big Brother 14 (US).

Every member made it to the jury portion of the game.
Britney Haynes is the only official member of the alliance that did not make it to the Final Four of the season.
