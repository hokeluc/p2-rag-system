# Laser Maze

Players must move a large object through a maze of lasers to a finish line. Each time the object or the player come in contact with the laser, the player receives a deduction in time. The player to complete the maze in the fastest time will emerge the winner.

The competition was slightly altered in its Big Brother 26 (US) iteration: each player had to move a "power core" through a maze of bamboo (rather than lasers). Each time the "core" came into contact with the bamboo, the battery of the core is drained by 5%. The player with the highest remaining batter is the winner. If there is a tie, the player who completed the course in the fastest time is the winner. This format returned for Big Brother 28 (US).

The competition first appeared as the final Safety Suite Competition in Week 3 of Big Brother 22 (US). Enzo Palumbo emerged victorious, winning safety for the week. He selected Christmas Abbott as his +1, earning safety but also receiving a punishment.

The competition re-appeared in Big Brother 26 (US) as the Week 3 Power of Veto competition. Tucker Des Lauriers won the competition, winning his first POV of the summer. He would go on to use the POV on Angela Murray instead of himself in an attempt to evict Quinn Martin.

This competition was featured again as the Week 3 POV competition in Big Brother 27 (US). Keanu Soto completed the maze in the fastest time, making this his first POV competition win of the summer.

The competition returned in Big Brother 28 (US) as the Egyptian-themed Final 5 POV competition. Drew Campbell completed the course with the fewest strikes, winning his second POV competition.
