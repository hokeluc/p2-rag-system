# Living Art

In this competition, houseguests stand at a podium and on go, they race to find discrepancies in artwork or find out which houseguest appears the most in a series of videos. The first houseguest to find the correct answer in the fastest time wins.

History[]

United States[]

The competition first appeared on Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US), during the Week 3 Double Eviction episode. Ross Mathews won the competition, winning the Double Eviction Power of Veto, after correctly finding the artwork that is an exact duplicate of the ones on the memory wall.

The competition reappeared on Celebrity Big Brother 2 (US), during Week 3 Double Eviction once again. This time as two separate competitions, Safety and Head of Household. Tamar Braxton won the Safety Competition after having the closest number to how many living statues were in the house. Lolo Jones won the Head of Household competition after finding the closest number to the living statues that remained in the house.

The competition reappeared on Celebrity Big Brother 3 (US), during the Week 4 Head of Household competition. This time, players had to find the differences in paintings and run back to their podium with the correct answer in the fastest time. The slowest or incorrect houseguests would be eliminated. Todrick Hall won the Head of Household competition.

The competition reappeared once again in Big Brother 26 (US), this time as the first AI Arena Competition. Kimo Apaka won the competition after correctly identifying which houseguest appeared the most in a series of videos.
