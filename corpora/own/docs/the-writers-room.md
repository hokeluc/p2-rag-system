The Writers Room is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Writers Room was created on Day 41 by Cory Wurtenberger and Mecole Hayes. The two were in the Have-Not room discussing the vote flip to evict Izzy Gleicher when Cory brought up the idea of working together and calling themselves The Writers Room. Mecole instantly said she thought the name was cute and she liked it.

After going through with the plan to evict Izzy, a final four deal was created between Cory and Mecole and their closest allies, America Lopez and Felicia Cannon. This deal managed to keep America safe when she was on the block that week and led to Cory protecting the four during his Double Eviction Head of Household reign.

After Cameron Hardin returned to the house and won Head of Household, he set his sights on Mecole and Felicia, placing them on the block alongside each other. After Cameron won the Power of Veto and discarded it, Cory decided to turn on Mecole, as Mecole had done very little to reestablish the trust between them which led to Cory’s mistrust of her. America and Cory also leaked the final four to Jag Bains and Matt Klotz. Cory’s plan went through as come eviction night, Mecole was evicted by a vote of 7-0, which led to Mecole attempting to call out and paint a larger target on Cory’s back as she was leaving the house.

Cory Wurtenberger is the only member to win a competition.
