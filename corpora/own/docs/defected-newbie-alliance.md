Defected Newbie Alliance, also known as Team Darkside, was an alliance formed during Big Brother 13 (US).

The Defected Newbie Alliance was the outcome of failure of The Regulators after having two of their alliance members evicted (Keith Henderson and Cassi Colvin) in a row. In Week 4, only 11 houseguests remained, and Dominic Briones, with his back in a corner, had to find his way back with majority by persuading a veteran, Daniele Donato, to go against the Veteran Alliance and pull in the 6 remaining newbies in with them. Unfortunately the veterans found out and evicted Dominic later that week.

Following Dominic’s eviction, Daniele and Kalia Booker won the next 3 HOHs. While this should’ve spelled good news for the alliance it didn’t. As across three weeks they only managed to evict one other member of the opposing alliance in Brendon Villegas. Due to him returning so he had to be evicted twice and Lawon Exum volunteering to be nominated and evicted.

Due to Adam Poch and Shelly Moore never really being loyal to the alliance and with Dominic and Lawon evicted. The alliance only had 3 members left in Daniele, Kalia and Porsche Briggs.

The following week, Jeff Schroeder won HOH and Daniele and Kalia were the final nominations. Daniele was able to convince Shelly to turn on Jeff and Jordan Lloyd. This caused her to defect from her alliance with them. Shelly tried to campaign for Daniele to stay but was unsuccessful. However Kalia, Porsche and Shelly re-formed the alliance and agreed to come after Jeff next week. Following Daniele’s eviction, it was a Double Eviction and Kalia won HOH. Through Shelly officially defecting from her alliance with Jeff. Kalia was able to cast the tie-breaking vote to evict on Jeff.

The alliance looked to be in good shape as they got Adam to re-align with them. Leaving them having numbers 4 to 2 against veterans Jordan and Rachel Reilly. Things seemed to look even better after Porsche won HOH. Unfortunately Porsche opened Pandora's Box and unleashed the duos twist. This gave outsiders Jordan and Rachel a chance to save both of them if one won the veto. Rachel ended up winning the POV and saving her and Jordan. This forced Porsche to nominate Shelly and Adam. Where Shelly was evicted. Following Shelly’s eviction, Rachel won HOH. She nominated Kalia and Porsche. Despite Adam winning the POV and having a chance to force Rachel to nominate Jordan. He once again betrayed his alliance and didn’t use it. Later Kalia was evicted. Following this Adam won HOH and Porsche won POV and she cast the sole vote to evict on Jordan. Later Rachel won the final HOH and evicted Adam. In a close vote of 4-3, Porsche lost to Rachel.

Alliance members Daniele Donato and Dominic Briones got married on January 17th, 2013.
Kalia and Porsche both accidentally nominated and evicted one of their alliance members (Lawon Exum and Shelly Moore)
