Four Prime was an alliance on Big Brother 22 (US).

Four Prime was created due to Nicole Franzel trying to protect Ian Terry by bringing her allies into an alliance with him.

After Daniele Briones won Head of Household in Week 6. The alliance seemed to be in good shapes. However after David Alexander used the disruptor power and Da'Vonne Rogers used the POV. Dani was left in a tough spot either to nominate from her The Committee alliance or to nominate Ian. Ultimately, she saw The Committee as a more beneficial alliance then Ian and she nominated him as a replacement nominee. Ultimately, Cody Calafiore and Nicole voted Ian out. Thus, officially ending the alliance although Cody, Dani and Nicole would remain closely aligned.

Ian Terry is the only member of this alliance to not win any competition.
