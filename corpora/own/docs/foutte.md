FOUTTE, which stands for Five Of Us Till The End, was an alliance on Big Brother 20 (US) consisting of Rockstar Lantry, Haleigh Broucher, Chris Williams (aka Swaggy C), Faysal Shafaat, and Kaitlyn Herman. The later inclusion of affiliates Bayleigh Dayton and Scottie Salton would later expand the group into two more alliances that remain closely associated with FOUTTE's history and legacy: The Sacred Six and The Hive.

Despite initially being in the majority, the alliance soon found itself in the minority after Kaitlyn flipped her vote at the first eviction. Following Kaitlyn's defection, the alliance was blindsided week after week—even during weeks when a member or affiliate was HoH, which happened a total of 5 times. The alliance was officially eliminated for good at the final 7 when the last member, Haleigh Broucher, was evicted. Due to being on the wrong side of almost every vote, never knowing what was going on, and being consistently manipulated by the other side, this alliance is considered one of the worst in Big Brother history.

FOUTTE was formed in Week 1, prior to the first HoH competition, and got its name after the first nominations but before the first App Store. With half the house immune from eviction, Haleigh and Chris began to form an alliance of five, bringing in Angie, Faysal, and Kaitlyn, with Chris later bringing Bayleigh in as an affiliate (although he said she was not a true member). After Tyler won HoH, Haleigh began flirting with him and tried to pull him to their side. Tyler nominated Sam and Steve. The alliance decided to target Sam for eviction and worked with Steve to keep him safe, using Scottie as a spy to gather information from the opposing alliance. After Faysal won the Power of Veto, the alliance believed they were in control for the week.

Faysal did not use the veto, leaving the nominations the same. At the very first live eviction, however, Kaitlyn, unhappy with Faysal and Haleigh's friendship, flipped her vote to evict Steve, blindsiding him 7–6. This shocked the alliance and put them in the minority. Kaitlyn then won the following HoH, which satisfied the rest of the alliance, though they were unaware that Kaitlyn was the one who flipped. Kaitlyn denied flipping and pretended she was still with them. At the nomination ceremony, she nominated Scottie and Winston from Level Six. The alliance believed Winston was the target, though Kaitlyn's real intention was to backdoor Swaggy. After Tyler won PoV, the alliance assumed Tyler would save Scottie and Kaitlyn would nominate Angela, but they were still unaware that Swaggy was her true target. At the PoV ceremony, Tyler used the veto on Scottie, and Kaitlyn put up Swaggy, making it clear she was the traitor. At the live eviction, Scottie ended up betraying Swaggy by voting to evict him, and Swaggy was evicted by a vote of 8–4.

After Swaggy's eviction, Scottie became HoH, putting the alliance in power for the first time. Realizing Brett's charisma and the strength of Brett and Winston as a duo, Scottie nominated both. He also won the PoV. Both Brett and Winston tried to convince Scottie to nominate Kaitlyn to please the house and even pitched a final 3 deal. Scottie told Kaitlyn about their conversations, causing her to argue with both. At the PoV ceremony, Scottie did not use the veto, leaving the nominations the same. While FOUTTE and Kaitlyn targeted Brett, Level Six targeted Winston. Having the numerical advantage, Level Six succeeded in evicting Winston in a 6–5 vote, blindsiding FOUTTE.

In week 4, Sam became HoH and nominated Kaitlyn and Haleigh. Faysal promised both that he would save them if he won PoV, and then did so. At the PoV ceremony, he saved Haleigh, upsetting Kaitlyn, and Sam nominated Rockstar. At the eviction, Kaitlyn was evicted 9–1, with JC voting against Rockstar. However, Sam's power app was automatically activated, giving Kaitlyn a chance to come back. She had to complete a puzzle to return, but failed to do so and was permanently evicted.

Bayleigh became HoH in week 5, putting the alliance in power for a second time. Her goal was to get Brett out, initially planning to nominate him alongside JC. Faysal tried to talk her out of nominating JC, causing a minor argument. Bayleigh considered nominating Brett with Rachel, and even thought about backdooring Scottie, as she didn't trust him after the "hinky votes." Before nominations, Bayleigh told Rachel about her power app that could change nominations at a ceremony, hoping to gain Rachel's trust. At the ceremony, Bayleigh nominated Brett and Rachel, with Brett as the main target and Scottie as a possible backdoor. She wanted nominations to remain the same so Brett would be evicted. Tyler won PoV, and despite initially considering using it on Brett to backdoor Scottie, Bayleigh wanted nominations to remain. Tyler feared his ally Angela could be the replacement, so he did not use the PoV. FOUTTE thought Brett would go but was unaware Level Six targeted Rachel. Level Six used their numbers to blindside Rachel in a 5–4 vote.

After the blindside, Angela became HoH and nominated Scottie and Rockstar. FOUTTE was unaware Level Six's real plan was to backdoor Bayleigh. Haleigh won the first hacker competition, letting her remove a nominee, replace them, and cancel a vote. She secretly removed Scottie and nominated Tyler, viewing him as the biggest threat. Angela won PoV, enabling the plan to backdoor Bayleigh, which FOUTTE was still unaware of. That night, Bayleigh tried to convince Angela to use the PoV on Rockstar and nominate Scottie; Angela pretended to consider it. At the PoV ceremony, Angela used the veto on Tyler and nominated Bayleigh, setting up the backdoor. Bayleigh was evicted by a 6–1 vote, becoming the first member of the jury.

After Bayleigh's eviction, Haleigh became HoH, putting FOUTTE in power for the third time. She nominated Angela and Kaycee, hoping to backdoor Tyler. However, Kaycee won the hacker competition, removed herself, and nominated Rockstar. Haleigh's hope of backdooring Tyler was shattered when Tyler won PoV. Tyler used it on Angela, and Haleigh put Kaycee back up. FOUTTE thought Kaycee would go, but Level Six still controlled the vote and targeted Rockstar. Rockstar was evicted by a 5–1 vote, with Scottie being the lone vote to keep her.

Shortly after Rockstar's eviction, Faysal became HoH. Although the alliance should have been in power, Faysal was convinced by JC that Scottie voted against Rockstar, had feelings for Haleigh, and was targeting him. Unaware JC was lying, Faysal targeted Scottie despite Haleigh's protests. At the nomination ceremony, Faysal nominated Scottie and Brett, with Scottie as the target. The PoV competition was Scottie's only hope, but Brett won, sealing Scottie's fate. Making matters worse, Sam convinced Scottie to propose that Haleigh go up instead, which upset Haleigh since she'd stood up for him. At the ceremony, Brett used the veto on himself, and Faysal replaced him with Kaycee. Scottie was evicted in a unanimous 6–0 vote after alienating Haleigh.

After Scottie's eviction, Angela became HoH. Faysal believed he and Haleigh were safe, but Haleigh was worried. At the nomination ceremony, Angela nominated both. Afterward, Faysal regretted sending Scottie out during his HoH. Faysal's only hope was the PoV, as he was the target, but Kaycee won and did not use it, sealing Faysal's fate. He was evicted by a 4–1 vote, with Sam voting against Haleigh.

After Faysal's eviction, he, Scottie, Bayleigh, and Rockstar competed in a "battle back" competition; Scottie won, returning to the house. Tyler became HoH and nominated Scottie and Haleigh, with Scottie the target. Kaycee won PoV and did not use it, so Scottie was evicted again by a unanimous 5–0 vote, becoming the fourth jury member. With Scottie gone, Haleigh was the last member remaining in the alliance.

After Scottie's eviction, Kaycee became HoH and nominated Haleigh and Sam, targeting Haleigh. The PoV was a must-win for Haleigh, but Kaycee won, ruining Haleigh's chance to stay. Kaycee did not use it, and Haleigh was evicted in a unanimous 4–0 vote, making her the fifth jury member and ending the alliance for good.

Swaggy C and Kaitlyn are the only members not to reach the jury stage.
Faysal is the only male member to reach jury.
Haleigh is the highest-ranking member.
