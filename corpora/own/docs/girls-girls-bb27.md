Girls Girls is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The "Girls Girls" alliance, consisting of Amy Bingham, Ashley Hollis, and Rachel Reilly, formed in Week 1. Vince was HoH and nominated Amy and Ashley for eviction. Due to Amy and Ashley being on the block they developed a close bond that grew into a friendship. Prior to being nominated, shower-gate rumors spread through the house of Ashley taking a lengthy shower. Amy was one of the people that defended Ashley against the rumors and begged Vince not to nominate her. This ended up backfiring, Amy and Ashley were both nominated alongside Zae Frederich. Amy and Rachel had already became a final two duo called The Mom Squad. Since Amy was close to both Ashley and Rachel, they formed a trio and called themselves the Girls Girls. Amy was the Mastermind's accomplice and reveled her secret to both Ashley and Rachel to help solidify her trust with both women.

After Ashley won POV, the Girls Girls went to work to make sure Amy had the votes to survive the first eviction in the event she was sitting on the block after the Block Buster. Rachel did a good job of rallying all of the women in the house to make sure Amy had the votes to stay. However, Rachel wanted to target the alpha males and made Zae her primary target. Amy and Ashley were unsure if going after Zae was a good move for their game as they also developed a close bond with him being on the block. Due to Rachel's massive influence and making sure Zae was the house target. Amy survived the first eviction and Zae was evicted by a 9-5 vote.

Realizing Rachel could be a huge house target. Amy and Ashley formed a separate final two in an attempt to distance themselves from Rachel and to lower their threat level. Jimmy Heagerty became the Week 2 HoH, there was a lot of pressure from the entire house to nominate Rachel. Jimmy did not think that was a good move for his game and nominated Adrian Rocha, Keanu Soto, and Kelley Jorgensen instead. Keanu and Kelley both won special veto powers and removed themselves from the block. Jimmy put Amy and Will Williams on the block to replace them. Adrian went on to win the Block Buster and Amy was evicted by a 13-0 vote.
