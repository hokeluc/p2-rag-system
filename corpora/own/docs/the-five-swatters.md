The Five Swatters was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

As a countermeasure to the quick dwindling number of their allies, Jasmine Davis and Alyssa Snider formed The Five Swatters, along with Joseph Abdin and Kyle Capener. Unbeknownst to the female members of the alliance, Joseph and Kyle's true loyalties were with The Leftovers and both acted as double agents to get more inside information. This easily toppled the alliance as Indy and Jasmine became the first two people evicted during the jury phase.

After Indy Santos was nominated during Week 6, Indy and her allies Alyssa Snider and Jasmine Davis realized they needed more numbers for their alliance. They decided to bring in Alyssa’s showmance Kyle Capener and Joseph Abdin to create The Five Swatters.

Despite the alliance being formed, Joseph and Kyle both agreed that The Leftovers would always be a priority for them. With The Leftovers decided on evicting Indy, Joseph and Kyle joined them and Indy was evicted by a vote of 7-1. This caused The Five Swatters to break. During the split house double eviction week, Jasmine was evicted on the inside while Alyssa and Kyle turned on Joseph and got him evicted. After the houses reunited, Kyle was later evicted by a vote of 5-0; thus, leaving Alyssa as the only alliance member left.

All members voted for Taylor Hale to win on finale night.
Each member of the alliance was on the jury.
