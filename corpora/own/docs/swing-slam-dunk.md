# Swing, Slam, Dunk

Similar to Dizzy Discs, players are on a disc but they are allowed to sit. However, players have less control as they are swung back & forth between two soft walls they are slammed into with increasing speed. In some seasons, they are also dunked into water to increase difficulty. Slime, rain or other liquids may be splatted onto the houseguests, similar to The Wall. After a while, the competition may be made more difficult by forcing the houseguests sit on whatever is below their disc.

Due to the size and set up of the competition and the need for less players, this is often used as a end of season Part 1 Final Head of Household competition.
