# Double Eviction

Often, the HouseGuests will compete for HOH following the previous eviction and then the new HOH will immediately nominate two people for eviction during the live show. The next day, they will compete in the POV and have the next eviction within the next two or three days. This was first seen in Big Brother 5.

Fast Forward evictions are more fast paced. In the North American format, the HouseGuests are informed of the Fast Forward immediately before or after an eviction. Someone is crowned the new HOH, and he or she only has the ad break to name his or her nominees. The POV competition is usually short due to being performed live. The Veto ceremony is also held live, and then another eviction occurs all within one hour. In Big Brother US, this is the most typical variation. This eviction first occurred in Big Brother 7 (US). This format is used and enhanced for a Triple Eviction (first seen on Big Brother Canada 3), where two people leave during the second eviction of the night.

In the International Format, Double Evictions are done in one night. The double eviction typically determines their evictees through who among the nominees received the two lowest save votes or in some seasons the top two highest evict votes. The announcement may vary, both nominees can be announced as evicted at the same time but in most seasons, the announcement is usually done by evicting the nominee with the lowest vote first and then interrupted only by a commercial gap before evictng the second nominee with the second lowest viewer votes.

In Big Brother 9 (US), there were 16 HouseGuests who competed in pairs. For the first two weeks, they were evicted in said pairs. The twist returned in the first week of Big Brother Brazil 23.

On Big Brother Canada, there are instant evictions. This is a variation of the "two evictions in one week" but houseguests are given a few minutes warning before the upcoming eviction. In addition, the PoV may not normally be up for grabs during this eviction cycle, meaning the initial nominees will face the vote. There is no studio audience as instant evictions are pre-recorded and the live feeds are cut until the episode airs to keep the results as secret as possible.

Introduced on Big Brother Canada 3, the Double Eviction Vote sees the HoH nominate 3 houseguest for eviction with 2 being evicted. The voting process for this round in that houseguest vote in to save a houseguest. Originally, this method was used for then second round of "Two Evictions in one night", making it a triple eviction night.

Introduced on Big Brother UK 2004, the UK sent home two people. Michelle and Emma went to a secret room and stayed in that room and observed the other housemates. After Emma was expelled, Michelle went back into the house to compete once again, getting a second chance.
Similar to Big Brother UK 2004, on Big Brother Canada 4 the houseguests competed in a normal double eviction and evicted Kelsey and Loveita, thinking they were gone for good. However, the two went to stay in a Secret Suite and were spying on the houseguests for a week. At the end of the week, the houseguests currently in the house voted to bring back one of the two, and they chose Kelsey, giving her a second chance, officially evicting Loveita alongside Dallas, who was evicted normally that same week.
5 years later, the Fake Double Eviction returned on Big Brother Canada 9, where they evicted Jedson and Tera. After they were evicted, they were both ordered to go to the HOH bedroom and put on some noise cancelling headphones. Once the double was over, they faced off in a competition on who would come back into the game, which Tera won, and re-entered the game.
Though not officially named after it, the Fake Double Eviction was informally introduced to the American version on Big Brother 25 (US) in its "Scary Week", beginning with the double eviction where the house evicted Cameron and Jared. After they were evicted, they were told that they became "Big Brother Zombies" and moved back into the house as Zombies, and the week played out with no HOH or POV competitions. At the end of that week, one of the two will return to the game by a "Do or Die Competition", while the other is out of the game for good, where Cameron won, and re-entered the game, while Jared was evicted for good.

Introduced in Big Brother 18 (UK), the UK sent two people home via the backdoor. The remaining 7 housemates were grouped together and took turns to enter the judgement room, where it'd be revealed whether or not they'd go to Big Brother Heaven or Hell (Heaven meaning a placement in the finale and Hell meaning eviction). The first group of Raph and Isabelle both got through to the finale, before the sisters came in and it was discovered that Hannah Agboola had been evicted after the door to hell opened for her. Next up were Kieran and Tom, with Kieran discovering he'd been evicted as well due to hell opening for him.

Introduced in Big Brother Canada 10, the houseguests would compete in a Safety Competition, where the winner would start off the Chain of Safety. They would pick one person they wanted to be safe, and that person would pick someone else, and so on until only three players remained. Those three players would compete in a second Safety Competition, where the winner would be safe and the remaining two would instantly be the final nominees for the week. All eligible houseguests then cast their votes to evict.
When the twist returned in Big Brother Canada 11, there was already an HOH from the night prior, so they started the Chain of Safety and only one Safety Competition took place that night between the three unchosen players. Since there was an HOH, they were not allowed to vote, all remaining eligible houseguests were. This event was dubbed The Fatal Feast.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 5 used this twist in Week 2.
During Big Brother 27 (US), as part of the "White Locust Resort" twist, the houseguests would first compete for safety. The winner would then call someone out, and that person would need to complete a new competition in a set amount of time or immediately be evicted. If that houseguest successfully completes their round, they would call out the next person to compete, and that person will have less time to do so, with the same rules, until someone fails and is evicted.

Introduced in Big Brother 24 (US), the houseguests were split into two groups for the entire week, isolated from each other with no interaction between groups. They would each play their own games of Big Brother culminating in their own individual evictions, for two total players being evicted that night.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 5 used this twist in Week 5.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 6 used this twist in Week 3.

Introduced in Big Brother Canada 12, the houseguests were made to compete for safety and to determine the order of the chain. Every person in the chain would choose a different position in the game to place someone else in, between the Head of Household, two nominee spots, three Veto Player spots, a Slop Pass and being unable to cast an eviction vote.
Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 6 featured a variation of this twist in week 5. It was known as Horror at the Ball.
