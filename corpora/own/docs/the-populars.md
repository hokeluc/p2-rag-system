The Populars were one of the four cliques apart of the clique twist introduced on Big Brother 11. The three members were Braden Bacha, Jordan Lloyd, and Laura Stein.

This clique is the only clique to have no members on the jury, as Braden and Laura were evicted before the jury stage and Jordan was in the final two.
Jordan was the only houseguest from this clique to win any competitions.
This clique has the lowest-placing and highest-placing houseguests of the season.
This later happened to Team Freakazoids in Big Brother 18 (US).
Jordan is the only member to return for a second season.
