Tangela was an alliance and showmance on Big Brother 20 (US).

Angela Rummans and Tyler Crispen quickly grew close in house, joining Level Six together and later becoming a Showmance.

Main article: Level Six

Angela and Tyler along with the other members of Level Six and their affiliates, created an alliance early in the game to combat the FOUTTE alliance that formed. Angela and Tyler helped lead the early blindsides of Steve Arienta and Chris Williams, leaving the opposing alliance distraught.

Working alongside each other inside the game, Angela and Tyler grew extremely close and made a Final 2 deal. While Tyler played both sides of the house to ensure his safety in case the other side of the house won Head of Household, Angela was clearly on the opposing side, causing her to become a target for the opposing side and caused her name to be floated around as a potential target for many early weeks. However, Angela was able to avoid nomination. Despite both of them avoiding nomination, their allies in Level Six were targeted. When Scottie Salton won Head of Household in Week 3, he targeted Brett Robinson and nominated him alongside Winston Hines. After Angela and Tyler realized keeping Brett would be keeping a shield for them, they flipped the votes and kept him. After Bayleigh Dayton won Head of Household during Week 5, she targeted Brett again. Bayleigh nominated him alongside Rachel Swindler, one of Angela’s closer allies. However, after Rachel started talking about targeting Tyler, Angela and Tyler saved Brett once again, evicting Rachel.

Following, Rachel’s eviction, Angela won Head of Household. Angela decided to nominate Rockstar Lantry and Scottie Salton. After Haleigh Broucher won the Hacker Competition, she attempted to target Tyler and saved Scottie and nominated him. After Angela won the Power of Veto, she vetoed Tyler and nominated Bayleigh as a replacement nomination, believing she was the Hacker. After Haleigh called a house meeting to confess she was the Hacker, Angela and Tyler got into a fight with Bayleigh, resulting in screaming matches between the trio. Later Bayleigh was evicted. After Haleigh won Head of Household, she quickly targeted Angela and Tyler, nominating Angela alongside Kaycee Clark, whom Angela and Tyler had become a trio with. After Kaycee won the Hacker Competition, she saved herself and nominated Rockstar. Tyler proceeded to win the Power of Veto, after Rockstar infamously messed up. Tyler vetoed Angela and Rockstar was later evicted.

After Rockstar’s eviction, Angela and Tyler and their affiliates quickly evicted the remaining members of the FOUTTE alliance, with Haleigh, the last member standing being evicted at the Final 7. Brett started to target Angela and Kaycee within the weeks leading up to FOUTTE’s downfall. Brett even told Tyler about his potential plans. This caused Angela, Kaycee, and Tyler to target Brett during the Double Eviction. After Tyler won ahead of Household and Angela won the Power of Veto, they were successful and Brett was evicted. Angela and Kaycee were able to win Head of Household and the Power of Veto and they evicted Sam Bledsoe

After outsider, JC Mounduix won Head of Household and Kaycee won the Power of Veto. Angela and Tyler were left nominated alongside each other. Kaycee decided to cast the sole vote to evict on Angela. On Finale Night. Kaycee became the final Head of Household and she brought Tyler to the Final 2 with her. Ultimately, Tyler lost the game to Kaycee by a close vote of 5-4.

Angela and Tyler dated for 4 years after the show, however, they broke up towards the end of 2022.
