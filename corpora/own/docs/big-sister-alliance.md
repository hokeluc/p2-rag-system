The Big Sister Alliance was an alliance formed between Paul Abrahamian, Zakiyah Everette, and Jozea Flores on the first day of the game. The three of them secretly made a pact to target the veteran players shortly after those players entered the house.

The alliance's pact quickly fell apart as Jozea's side of the house was blindsided by his eviction in the first week, after the veterans managed to get some of the new players on their side (including Zakiyah).

Paul is the only member of this alliance that has won a competition during Big Brother 18.
