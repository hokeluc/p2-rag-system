The Afterparty (also known as The Dyre Fest alliance) was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

As a result of a split house double eviction twist that resulted to the consensus target Joseph Abdin's eviction, The Afterparty alliance was formed, with the intention to decimate the remaining houseguests from the other half of the house. While seemingly set up for success, things quickly crumbled when Kyle Capener was exposed for his controversial comments, which eventually led to his eviction and the demise of the alliance.

The Afterparty was formed in Week 7, after Kyle Capener exposed The Leftovers to HOH Terrance Higgins to save him and his showmance Alyssa Snider. They then pulled in Matt Turner to form The Afterparty.

The Afterparty looked to be set up for success, successfully evicting Joseph Abdin and Turner went on to win the next HOH. Originally, the alliance planned to backdoor Michael Bruner. Their plans were foiled however after Michael won the POV. As it was common knowledge that Michael was going to use the POV to save his ally Brittany Hoopes, Turner searched for a replacement nominee. Later within the week, Kyle got exposed for making racist comments, which led to Turner in defecting from the alliance and nominating Kyle. Without the numbers, Alyssa and Terrance went with the house and Kyle was evicted by a vote of 5-0. Despite Turner betraying the alliance, Alyssa and Terrance remained closely aligned with each other and still tried to work with Turner. However, after Michael won HOH and nominated Alyssa and Terrance, Terrance was targeted and evicted. In Week 10, Alyssa found herself nominated alongside Taylor Hale. With Alyssa knowing she had Brittany’s vote to stay, Alyssa sought out Turner’s vote. However, Turner valued his relationship with Monte Taylor more and voted to evict Alyssa instead. Monte, being the Head of Household, cast the tie-breaking vote, Alyssa was evicted, which officially ended the alliance.

All members except for Matt Turner voted for Taylor Hale to win on finale night.
Every member of the alliance made it to the jury stage.
