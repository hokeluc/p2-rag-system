# Perfect Game

A "Perfect Game" is a feat that has been accomplished by only six houseguests: Dan Gheesling, Kevin Martin, Dane Rupert, Cody Calafiore, Jean-Thomas Jobin, and Chelsie Baham.

Initially, a Perfect Game was a fanmade term adopted from Survivor, with no official definition from the Big Brother producers or houseguests, instead being mostly defined by this wiki's interpretation. However, after Big Brother 26 (US), our definition was canonized by production via the show's official Instagram account.

The elements are as follows:

The houseguest never has an eviction vote cast against them.
The houseguest is announced the winner of Big Brother.
The houseguest wins the game in a unanimous Jury vote.
Some added stipulation that enhances a Perfect Game but is not specifically needed to achieve one:
The houseguest never faced eviction as a final nominee due to coming off the block with the Veto or another twist.
The houseguest was never nominated at all, including by default at the Final Three, and thus never faced eviction period.
The houseguest always voted in the majority for evictions, and potentially for other voting twists such as re-entry votes.
This includes tiebreaker eviction votes and votes to save, but excludes sole eviction votes.

The concept of a Perfect Game originated in Survivor (US), and has only been achieved twice as of its 50th season. Read more about it here.
Due to Cody never being nominated at all, including at Final 3 due to winning the Final HOH, and never voting in the minority during Big Brother 22 (US), he has the most statistically Perfect Game of all six winners.
Tychon Carter-Newman is the first person to avoid nomination all season, but not win with a Perfect Game.
Chelsie Baham is the first woman and Person of Color to win with a Perfect Game.
Tamar Braxton is the first person to win the game with a unanimous vote but not a Perfect Game. She was followed by Xavier Prather. They both had exactly one eviction vote cast against them throughout the season.
She's also the first woman to win with a unanimous vote.
Had Ian received all of the jury votes to win during Big Brother 14 (US), Dan would have been the first houseguest to play a Perfect Game and lose to a Perfect Game.
Had Dan won the season with all seven votes by the Jury, he would have been the only person to win with a Perfect Game twice.
Dan, Cody, and Anthony are the only people who were eligible to win with a Perfect Game twice.
With the exception of Big Brother Canada 10 and Big Brother Canada 12, all the winners of Big Brother Canada avoided receiving any eviction votes, although Kevin M and Dane are the only ones to win with a Perfect Game.
Ironically, the first 6 runners-up of Big Brother Canada were unable to win with a Perfect Game, as they all had received at least 1 eviction vote before reaching the Final 2. Anthony Douglas was the first Canadian runner-up who had the opportunity to achieve a Perfect Game, followed by Breydon White, Claudia Campbell and himself again on Season 12.
Although they never received eviction votes, Sarah Hanlon and Kevin M both received nomination votes during the weeks where nominations where determined by a house vote. Sarah received 2 nominations during Week 1 of Big Brother Canada 3, while Kevin M received 1 nomination during Week 4 of Big Brother Canada 5.
Jag Bains has played the most "Imperfect Game" of all Big Brother (US) winners who faced a jury. He had 14 votes against him, receiving 12 eviction votes and losing 2 jury votes. He is also the only winner to have been evicted and returned in the same season.
Jillian MacLaughlin and Kevin Jacobs have played the most "Imperfect Games" of all Big Brother Canada winners. They both have 3 votes against them: Jillian received 0 eviction votes and she lost 3 jury votes while Kevin J received 2 eviction votes and lost 1 jury vote.
For Big Brother 1 (US) and Big Brother: Over The Top, the concept of a Perfect Game is different.
For Big Brother 1 (US), a housemate would have had to never receive any nomination votes, or a percentage of America's vote to banish and have had to receive 100% of America's vote to win.
For Big Brother: Over The Top, a houseguest would have had to never receive an eviction vote and have had to receive 100% of America's vote to win.
Because of the stipulation of having to receive 100% of America's vote to win, a Perfect Game in Big Brother 1 (US) or Big Brother: Over The Top is virtually impossible.
Big Brother: Reindeer Games did not feature voting at all, so a Perfect Game is not possible to achieve since a major stipulation requires avoiding getting votes against you. However, it is worth noting that Taylor Hale made it to the finale without being nominated.
