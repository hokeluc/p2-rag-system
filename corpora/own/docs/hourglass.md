# Hourglass

This competition involves players either stacking objects or building a puzzle while needing to refill their timer.

In Big Brother 23 (US), this competition was played in teams; each team assembling a puzzle while swapping teammates to reset their timer to 25 seconds, and the winning teammate who placed the last piece of the puzzle became HOH.

In Big Brother: Reindeer Games, this competition was the third stage of the final competition, and since it was a head to head match between the Final Two, there was no timer element required.

In Big Brother 26 (US), the rules were simplified and the houseguests were given one hour to stack as many objects as they wanted to. Within the hour, they had to hit a button lock in their score once they were satisfied, and whoever had the most pieces stacked in the fastest time would win the competition.
