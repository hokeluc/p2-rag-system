The Mastermind is the main antagonist of Big Brother 27 (US), where he appeared to introduce various twists throughout the season.

During the season finale, his identity was revealed to be Big Brother 2 (US) winner, Dr. Will Kirby. However, it was later revealed that this was a set-up to frame Dr. Will, and that there was not one, but instead three Masterminds: Eric Stein, Jessie Godderz and Frankie Grande, all seeking out revenge for twists that cost them the wins in their respective seasons (America's Player from Big Brother 8 (US), the Coup d'Etat from Big Brother 11 (US) and the Rewind Button from Big Brother 16 (US)). Each Mastermind had a different mask, and clues around their identity were hidden throughout the house.

The mastermind captured Julie and stole the HOH Relic. Zach Cornell saved Julie, while Jimmy Heagerty recovered the HOH Relic.

At the first HOH Competition of the season, Vince Panaro successfully was able to destroy the mastermind's lair after winning.

The Mastermind recruited Amy Bingham to be the Secret Accomplice in order to set the events of the premiere in motion, culminating in allowing Rachel Reilly into the cast, and then giving Amy an opportunity to become the true 17th houseguest herself.

He also was responsible for introducing various powers into the game within the first two weeks.

In Week 9, the Mastermind kicked off his Month of Mayhem, which would start with a surprise elimination at the White Locust Resort, with one houseguest not returning to the Big Brother House. The houseguests were also told that there are more twists yet to come. The elimination would be orchestrated and done by his concierge, Season 20 runner-up/fan favorite and Season 22 All-Star Tyler Crispen.

The Mastermind confronted The Zingbot after he broke into Hotel Mystere to zing the houseguests. After a face-off with many failed attempts and plenty of zings, The Mastermind successfully destroyed Zingbot, setting up the next HOH competition.

On Day 73, the Mastermind introduced the final BB Blockbuster, and following Kelley Jorgensen's eviction, he announced that it was double eviction night and that it was, "all about him." The following Head of Household and Power of Veto competitions were both based on him.

In Week 11, the Mastermind orchestrated an eviction all on his own, without the help of Julie. The Head of Household competition, nomination ceremony, and veto meeting all took place inside of "the Sanctum", which was a dark room dimly lit with several ritualistic candles. During the nomination ceremony, the Head of Household, Vince Panaro, made his nominations by placing voodoo dolls resembling each houseguest on a table in the middle of the Sanctum. Instead of casting their votes to evict secretly in the Diary Room, the houseguests casted their votes to evict in front of the nominees by stabbing the voodoo doll of the nominee they wished to evict.

Upon Amy's eviction in Week 2, The Mastermind surprised her with a traditional Big Brother goodbye message, bidding farewell to his Secret Accomplice.
The Mastermind made a surprise takeover during the second episode of Big Brother: Unlocked that aired on August 8, 2025, to help feed into the narrative that his true identity is "Intern Andrew".
