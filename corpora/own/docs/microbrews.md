# Microbrews

Houseguests are given a set of large tweezers and must vertically stack minuscule objects on top of each other. The first houseguest to place all of their objects and have their tower remain stable for the following few seconds wins.

This competition has two variations: from its debut in Big Brother 22 (US) to Big Brother 25 (US), the players had to stack miniature cans, bottles, or glasses into a pyramid-shaped tower formation. From Big Brother 26 (US) onward, however, the players had to instead solve a vertical puzzle by stacking the pieces in the same manner as before.
