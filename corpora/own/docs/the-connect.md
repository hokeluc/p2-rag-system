The Connect was an alliance on Big Brother 22 (US).

The alliance was formed early in the game, and they would come up with the strategy that they would each have an connection outside of the alliance to additionally use.

While Bayleigh Dayton and Da'Vonne Rogers considered this alliance real, Cody Calafiore and Enzo Palumbo didn’t see it as a real alliance, and instead favored working with The Committee. The alliance hardly talked again after the first few weeks, and soon Cody and Enzo had both been able to evict Bayleigh and Da'Vonne.
