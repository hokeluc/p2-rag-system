Golden Girlies is an alliance on Big Brother 26 (US).

Angela and Brooklyn initially bonded over being married mothers. Eventually, due to personal and strategic differences, they were at odds. This was heightened when Brooklyn ate some of Angela's charcuterie without permission.

Both Angela and Brooklyn appeared on Dr. Phil prior to their appearances on Big Brother.
