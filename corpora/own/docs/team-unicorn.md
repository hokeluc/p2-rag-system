Team Unicorn was one of the four teams apart of the team twist introduced on Big Brother 18. The four members were James Huling, Natalie Negrotti, Bronte D'Acquisto, and Victor Arroyo. After winning the Ride the Rocket competition on Day 1, all four members of the team were given immunity from the first two evictions of the season.

This is the only team to have a female as its lowest ranking member, which is Bronte.
In addition, she is the only team member to not make jury.
She is also the only team member not to win an individual competition.
This team, along with Category Four, has the most members on the jury with three.
