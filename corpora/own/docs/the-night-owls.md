The Night Owls, commonly referred to as Vorgan, was an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

Officially formed around Week 6 following the eviction of their close ally Zach Cornell, the duo of Morgan Pope and Vince Panaro survived numerous close calls during the rest of the pre-jury, managing to scrape by while in the house's spotlight. With Morgan's six consecutive HOH and/or POV wins and Vince's three additional HOH reigns, the pair dominated the jury phase as the new core of the larger Judges alliance. However, their Judges collaborator Ashley Hollis shutout both in the Final HOH competition, evicted Morgan, and won over the jury against Vince, resulting in their eleventh hour defeat.

The centrifuge for post-jury discourse among the fanbase, The Night Owls were heavily controversial for their volatile and seemingly flirtatious relationship in spite of Vince having a girlfriend at home. In addition to being the root of Morgan's strategic control over Vince, the pair were known for their constant sleep deprivation, which led to their alliance name and partially caused their failure to win the Final HOH.

Morgan Pope and Vince Panaro began to find themselves on the same side of the house circa Week 4, when the former recognized his usefulness to her game and pitched to her ally and then-Head of Household Mickey Lee to name Jimmy Heagerty as the replacement nominee instead of Vince. Morgan, Vince, Mickey, and their mutual ally Zach Cornell then formed The Melting Pot alliance that ran the house dynamics for the brief period thereafter. However, after Ava Pearl sent Zach home on her HOH reign and Mickey's tyrannical gameplay became clear to the house, The Night Owls increasingly solidified as a duo.

Rachel Reilly won the Week 6 HOH as one of the house's biggest targets, and gunned hard for those at the top of the power structure by nominating Mickey and the duo. After Vince manipulated his confidant Lauren Domingue into using the Power of Veto on him despite being in the least amount of danger out of the nominees, Rachel sought revenge by nominating a well-liked player in Rylie Jeffries as per her warnings towards Lauren. However, Rachel's closest ally Ashley Hollis saw the value in protecting Morgan from eviction due to it seeming unlikely that Vince would leave her side and that they could thus use both as numbers. Ashley, Morgan, Vince, Rachel, and Will Williams promptly formed The Judges alliance, and were able to sway Ava to their side following Mickey's Block Buster victory, resulting in Rylie's blindside over Morgan in a 5-4 vote.

Vince won the following HOH, and at the request of Morgan, he nominated their Kelley Jorgensen, Ava, and their now ostracized former ally Mickey as the safest route. Mickey won the POV and vetoed herself, and after an extreme push by Morgan and Ashley, Vince named outsider Katherine Woodman as the replacement instead of the more threatening Rachel, and Katherine was subsequently evicted by a 7-1 vote. Week 8 HOH Keanu Soto, briefly angry at Vince for not following his suggestion of betraying Rachel, nominated him and Morgan. Keanu and Vince tried and failed to trick Morgan into using the POV on Vince following her victory, with Vince then winning the Block Buster and Mickey leaving over Ashley in a 6-1 vote.

Both were called out during the White Locust twist, but survived eviction at Rachel's expense, and from the previous week onward, Morgan won safety via competitions for six consecutive rounds. However, a wedge was often formed between the pair due to Vince's previous allyship with Lauren as displayed by him choosing her first during the twist, and Morgan continuously sought full control over him by targeting her, which led to frequent cycles of bickering amongst them. Though this landed her on the block during Lauren's Week 9 HOH, Morgan remained steadfast on evicting her once she won her second POV in a row, oftentimes using Vince's controversially risqué connection with her despite having a relationship outside the house to her advantage.

Vince won his third HOH at the start of Week 10, and through Morgan's insistence of keeping Ashley safe, he nominated Keanu, Kelley, and Ava. However, with her third consecutive veto, Morgan went the risky route by duping him into naming Lauren as the replacement, a plan made successful once Ava was vetoed off of the block. Though Lauren won the Block Buster and Kelley was sent home in order to break up the Flower Power trio, Morgan finished the job with her HOH during the Double Eviction, as Ashley and Keanu sent Lauren home in a 2-1 vote, with Vince in dissent. Vince and Morgan won the next two HOHs and evicted Keanu and Ava as per Morgan's wishes while bringing Ashley with them to the final 3.

Arriving at the final 3 alongside their Judges co-member Ashley, Ashley successfully attempted to flip Morgan against Vince to secure her spot in the final 2, and Vince appeared to be considering evicting Morgan as well. However, as Vince and Morgan made glaring errors in Parts 2 and 3 of the Final HOH competition as a culmination of their tendency to get limited sleep around each other, allowing for Ashley to win and evict Morgan for her unassailable physical game.

With the jury largely displeased by Vince's betrayal of each of them and prioritizing Morgan over his own game while receptive towards Ashley's own strategic and social merits, Ashley defeated Vince and The Night Owls as a whole in a 6-1 vote, with Morgan being the sole vote for him to win.
