Six Shooters was an alliance on Big Brother 21 (US).

The Six Shooters were formed as an alliance inside of Gr8tful to take out Isabella Wang and Nick Maccarone.

The Six Shooters created a plan to keep Nick’s target Nicole Anthony in the house so she could go after him. They successfully did so blindsiding Nick and Isabella. After Cliff Hogg III won HOH in week 4. Christie Murphy used her Diamond POV as leverage to have Cliff backdoor Isabella. They were successful as Isabella was nominated and evicted. Following Isabella’s eviction, Holly Allen won HOH. The Six Shooters planned on targeting Nick. However after Nick won the POV, they changed their target to Sam Smith a former ally of theirs.

On Day 44, an argument broke out between all members of the alliance due to Jackson Michie telling Jack Matthews that Holly and Kathryn Dunn knew each other outside of the house and Jackson trying to plant seeds that they should turn on Christie Murphy. This argument led to Jackson and Holly defecting from the alliance.

Despite Holly’s target, Sam being evicted. Jackson and Holly left the alliance. Despite the other remainders of the alliance still aligning with each other and working with Nick. Jackson and Holly aligned with the other side of the house and evicted the remaining members of the alliance.

All members of the alliance in jury voted for Jackson Michie to win.
