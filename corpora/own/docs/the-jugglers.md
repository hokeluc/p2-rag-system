The Jugglers is an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

Barrett and Drew were members of the The Crossovers, an alliance with The Icons, which later merged with the The Red Corner alliance in week 2 in order to blindside Rome Seymour. Recognizing they were at the bottom of both alliances, the pair formed a duo that they named the Jugglers.

Angela Murray was suspicious of Drew, despite being in both alliances, due to his friendships with people not aligned with them and his close bond with Melody Morris. She convinced Dee Valladares to target him, although they were prevented from doing so until week 4 when Haley Thogmartin was HOH and put Drew up as a pawn. Dee informed Barrett of this blindside plan, and he chose not to inform Drew, instead reassuring him that he was not the target all throughout the week.

However, on day 30, Dee recognized that Drew was not a threat to them and instead flipped the votes to target Melody instead. The votes later flipped again to target Lyric Medeiros over Melody on day 31. When Drew won the Blockbuster, both members blindsided the Red Corner by evicting Lyric.

When LaTrice was HoH she initially wanted to put up both Jugglers as pawns, but ultimately just nominated Barrett. However, due to chaos and fights between other housemates, he was comfortably in no danger. The pair officially cemented their alliance with Melody and Mallory Aurichio, named F.A.P., with their main goal being to evict Kamu Kirk.

When Yash won HoH in week 6, he considered putting up Barrett as a pawn, but Drew spent several hours in his HoH room with him until he changed his mind. Drew later warned Barrett about this, as he was still likely to be the replacement nomination. Before the week 6 veto ceremony, F.A.P. officially merged with the Crossovers to create the Mosh Pit alliance. Devens used the DPoV on Dee and replaced her with Kamu,

During the Mosh Pit creation, Angela mentioned that she had previously been working with Barrett and Drew, worrying Mallory and Melody that they were not loyal to F.A.P.. Devens and Dee worried that Drew was still not loyal to them and told Barrett, who told them that Drew encouraged LaTrice to view Devens as a threat during her HoH. Devens planned to make a speech if Kamu was not evicted revealing both the Mosh Pit and the Crossovers. He first threatened Barrett with this, but Barrett encouraged him to do so, promising loyalty to the icons. Devens then told Drew of this a few hours before the live episodes, threatening to expose his entire game and get him evicted if Melody or Mallory do not vote with them. Kamu was evicted after Angela won the blockbuster.

Dee won the next HoH and decided to target Drew, but decided to confront him so she would have support for turning on their alliances. She gathered the Mosh Pit and confronted Drew, but he did not admit to betraying the icons, as he did not. After the others had left, Barrett claimed that Drew never warned him about being the replacement nomination, and encouraged Dee to target him. Prior to nominations, Barrett continued to lie about knowing Dee's nominations to Drew. Drew won the Time Capsule and the Never-Not Pass, worrying Barrett as he was now the only person in the Mosh Pit to not be voted by America.

Drew was nominated by Dee at the ceremony alongside Taylor and LaTrice, however during the ceremony Drew successfully appealed to the whole house and changed both Barrett and the Icon's minds, switching the target to Taylor. LaTrice won the veto, and the Jugglers believed that Yash would be nominated and targeted. Drew confided in Barrett his new anti-icon thoughts, and believed they should target Dee and Devens in the upcoming weeks, which Barrett expressed reluctant support for.

Dee told Drew that she wanted to nominate Mallory as a pawn, as she had a good relationship with Haley and used her bribe power on Yash. She later told Barrett as well, but claimed that Drew did not know, and he was frustrated by this line of thought due to his closeness with Mallory. Drew and Barrett discussed Drew's feelings of expendability to the icons, as well as continuing discussions of nominating them, unaware Devens was nearby. Devens pretended to overhear them, prompting Drew to explain his feelings to the icons and causing the Jugglers to feel they could not turn on them. Drew began to suspect that Mallory was the target and partially confronted Devens over it.

Drew was talking to Dee, Devens, and Haley when Haley told Drew that Mallory was the target. Drew partially pushed back on this, but ultimately could not due to his own precarious positioning. He insisted that Devens tell Barrett, and when Barrett was informed he reacted negatively. Barrett decided to flip on Drew and ensure he was evicted even over Taylor, who the pair had been targeting. He told Mallory and Melody of the Red Corner, the Crossovers, and the Toolshed whilst continuing to tell Drew they must remain with the Crossovers and abort their previous flip discussions. He formed a new majority with Mallory and Melody alongside Yash and Taylor with the purpose of ensuring Drew left. However, Drew won the Blockbuster and Mallory was evicted instead with only Melody and Barrett voting to keep her.

For the Medieval twist, Dee chose Haley and Drew to go into the challenge to protect them both, and after Drew won he chose Barrett to protect him as well. When Barrett won, he wanted to ensure Haley's eviction as she had pivoted to allying with the Icons, and so, against Drew's wishes, chose Yash to go into the final challenge. Barrett's scheme was successful as Yash won and subsequently evicted Haley.

Drew won the next HoH and targeted the trio of Yash, Taylor, and LaTrice, as they were targeting him, annoying Barrett who had hoped he would target the Icons. When Yash won the veto, and Drew made it known he would not be renominating Melody, either Barrett or the Icons would be the renom. Devens volunteered, believing he would be the safest due to the Finger Guns Alliance with Drew and Barrett as well as the Crossovers, whilst Drew wanted to renominate Dee as she was not communicating with him and had nominated him the prior week. However, as nobody including Barrett supported this plan, Drew ultimately went through with nominating Devens.

Barrett planned to officially betray the Crossovers by evicting Devens, and continuously lied to Drew about this, despite Drew's suspicions of a plan going on without his knowledge. In the situation that Devens won blockbuster, Yash wanted Taylor evicted whilst Drew wanted LaTrice evicted. Barrett initially agreed with Yash, but later changed his mind and decided to evict LaTrice. This came to fruition when Devens won the blockbuster, and Barrett evicted LaTrice.

In week 9, Barrett won the HoH and immediately promised Drew, alongside Melody, complete safety for the week. He made plans to nominate all three Icons, and hinted to Drew about this although he did not explicitly confirm anything. Barrett told Drew about the Icons targeting Drew since week 2, about him being the initial target in week 4, about Barrett intending to vote Drew out no matter what, and about intending to evict Devens on Drew's HoH. Drew was stunned by this information, but Barrett pledged loyalty to Drew and agreed to pick Drew if he was chosen in the veto picks, so they could take down Angela and renominate Yash.

Barrett was chosen for the veto picks, but he betrayed Drew and chose Yash instead. Drew was upset by this, feeling completely betrayed by Barrett, and confronted him about this in the backyard, asking why Barrett did not just tell him he wanted the nominations to stay the same, Barrett stated he believed Drew would choose the Icons over him and Melody, frustrating Drew further. In his HoH room, Barrett continued to antagonise Drew by cuddling with Melody on his bed whilst Drew awkwardly watched from the chair. This happened on consecutive nights until Melody stopped on Drew's request.

After Yash won the veto, Barrett hoped to ensure either Dee or Devens left by starting a new alliance among the remaining new players called The Ceasefire. However, Barrett and Melody were the only ones who believed in it, as Drew, Taylor, and Yash immediately realized this was a fake alliance. Ultimately, Yash and Taylor betrayed the new formed Ceasefire and evicted Angela instead of Dee.

In week 10, Yash won HoH and he and Taylor fabricated a story to blame Drew for their betrayal. Despite Drew evicting Dee, Barrett believed Yash over him and planned to evict Drew if he remained on the block. Yash also lied about the terms of the BB Bribe, with Drew believing the Icons correct version whilst Barrett was more conflicted. Yash nominated the three members of F.A.P. with Drew as the house target. Barrett blamed Drew for this, and made a pact with Melody to never vote for Drew in the end, even if they believed the person he sat next to played a worse game. Whilst Melody quickly rescinded this, Barrett never did and it is unknown if he would have followed through.

After Barrett dropped out of the pressure cooker veto, only Drew and Yash remained. Drew convinced Yash to throw him the veto in exchange for Drew evicting Barrett if he remained on the block and not nominating Yash on his next HoH. When Melody won the veto, Drew kept to his end of the deal and evicted Barrett, ending the Jugglers alliance.

In exit interviews, Barrett spoke negatively about Drew and rated his game significantly lower then everyone else remaining in the house. When Drew was evicted at final 3, Barrett mocked him live on stage for failing to make final 2. However, the pair reconciled and are friends outside of the house.
