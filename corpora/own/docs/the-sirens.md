The Sirens is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Sirens was created in week seven by America Lopez and Blue Kim as they had been building trust with each other and decided to establish a final two deal.

Despite making this final two deal, neither America or Blue were loyal to this as they had other final two partners. After Cory Wurtenberger, America’s showmance won Head of Household in the Double Eviction, he nominated Blue as a pawn to send her showmance, Jared Fields home. Cory was successful in his plan as Jared was evicted in a 6-1 vote. Infuriated by her nomination, Blue turned on America and Cory and began actively targeting them. Later, Blue exposed the alliance to Jag Bains, in an attempt to convince him to target the showmance. After America was told about Blue’s plan to target her and Cory, America also turned on Blue, actively targeting her alongside Cory. Ultimately, neither America or Blue were able to win power to take each other out but were evicted back-to-back by The Minutemen in the second double eviction.

Blue is the only member to win a competition.
