# BB Power of Invincibility

During week four, America was informed that the BB Power of Invincibility would enter the game, however, only 4 houseguests decided by America would be eligible to play. Later that week, America voted for Cirie Fields, Cory Wurtenberger, Jag Bains, and Matt Klotz to play in the competition. Matt went on to win the competition and earn the BB Power of Invincibility. Matt would end up using the power later that week to save Jag from eviction.
