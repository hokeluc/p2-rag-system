The No Names was a short-lived alliance formed between Bridgette, James, Michelle, Natalie, Paul, and Victor in Big Brother 18.

For weeks, Paulie Calafiore was running the house as most of what he wanted to happen has happened. During week 7, Victor nominated Michelle and Zakiyah Everette for eviction. Paulie wanted to send Michelle home, while Paul and Victor wanted to send Zakiyah home. It caused tension between former allies Paul and Paulie. After realizing that Paulie was running the house, Natalie and Bridgette tried to save Michelle from eviction and send Zakiyah home instead. Paulie than realized what Natalie and Bridgette were doing and later got into an argument with them which upset James and Paul. Natalie and Bridgette were able to convince James, who at first was hesitant about going against Paulie, to vote out Zakiyah. Natalie, Michelle, and Bridgette later convinced Paulie's former allies Paul and Victor to join their side, forming an alliance between them which was nameless (thus the No Names alliance).

During the same week, James received the Care Package and got the power to eliminate two eviction votes. James excluded Paul and Corey's eviction votes. Zakiyah was than evicted by a 3-2 vote. Before the Double Eviction, the No Names alliance wanted to target Paulie, Corey, and Nicole for eviction. However, during the Double Eviction, Corey became the HoH and nominated Bridgette and Michelle for eviction. A 5-1 vote evicted Bridgette.

After Bridgette's eviction, Victor won HOH. At first, Victor wanted to nominate Michelle and Natalie for eviction and was hesitant about turning on Paulie. However, Paul later convinced Victor to turn on Paulie and to nominate Corey and Paulie for eviction. The members of the No Names alliance then called Paulie out on his gameplay and his behavior the previous week. Victor later won the Power of Veto and Paulie was evicted later in the week by a unanimous 5-0 vote.

After Paulie's eviction, Natalie began to question the alliance as she felt that Paul would be a huge threat in the game. Natalie later became the HoH, and after talking with Nicole about Paul being a threat, Natalie and James decided to target Paul for eviction. Michelle received the Co-HOH Care Package, and initially, she wanted to target Nicole for eviction, but after being convinced by Natalie about how much of a threat Paul was, Natalie and Michelle nominated Victor and Paul respectively for eviction. Paul later won the Power of Veto and Victor was evicted for a second time by a vote of 2-1, he later returned to the game for the second time. After his re-entry, Paul and Victor turned on Natalie, James, and Michelle ending their alliance.

Natalie was the only alliance member not to vote for Paul to win.
