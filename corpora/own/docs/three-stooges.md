The Three Stooges was an alliance on Big Brother 4 (US).

During Week 1, The Three Stooges aligned together as they were the guy “exes” in the house and quickly bonded with each other.

Despite Jee being nominated in Week 1. He was able to survive in favor of evicting Amanda Craig. The Three Stooges started to take power once Jee won Head of Household in week two. The alliance decided to target Erika Landin, Robert Roman’s ex. Jee made a fatal mistake when he chose to nominate his ally Michelle Maradie as a pawn. The Three Stooges were blindsided when the opposing alliance the Elite Eight, kept Erika and Michelle was evicted by a vote of 6-2.

Following Michelle’s eviction, Dana Varela won HOH. Figuring that she was at the bottom of her alliance, Dana decided to flip on her old alliance and re-aligned with The Three Stooges and her showmance Justin Giovinco. Dana nominated Alison Irwin and Jack Owens, Jr., targeting Alison. Jun decided to flip with Dana over to the Three Stooges, since they were aligned together and Jun was also at the bottom of that alliance. After Nathan Marlow won the veto and saved Alison. Dana decided to nominate David Lane and he was later evicted by a vote of 5-2.

Following David’s eviction, Alison won HOH. Seeking revenge for being nominated last week, Alison set out to target Dana. In order to gain the votes to evict Dana, Alison made a deal with The Three Stooges that in exchange for safety, they would vote her out. Later Alison nominated Dana and Jun Song for eviction. Later Dana was evicted by a vote of 6-0. Following Dana’s eviction, Justin Giovinco won HOH. Things started to be looking up for The Three Stooges as they thought they had both Alison and Jun working with them. Which just left them to dismantle the remaining member of the Elite Eight. Later Nathan was evicted by a vote of 5-0.

Following Nathan’s eviction, Erika win HOH. She sought out to target The Three Stooges. She did so by nominating Justin and Robert for eviction. After none of them won the veto. Justin was later evicted by a vote of 3-1. Following Justin’s eviction, Jee won HOH. He decided to target the remaining members of the opposing alliance. He did so by nominating Erika and Jack for eviction. What Jee and Robert weren’t aware off was that Alison and Jun had formed an alliance. Later they along with Erika formed an alliance and blindsided Jack and The Three Stooges at the eviction.

Main article: Girl Power

Following Jack’s eviction, Jun won HOH. Jun decided to target the remaining members of The Three Stooges. Jun nominated Alison and Robert for eviction, attempting to hide her alliance with Alison. Later after Alison vetoed herself. Jee was named the replacement nominee and was evicted by a vote of 2-0.

Robert was able to win his first HOH at the final four and Erika was evicted. After Robert failed to win the final head of household competition, Alison cast the sole vote to evict on him, officially ending the alliance in defeat.

All members of the alliance voted for Jun Song to win.
