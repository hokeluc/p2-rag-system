# AINSLEY

The Big Brother "Artificial Intelligence Network Self Learning Entity", aka AINSLEY is the main antagonist of Big Brother 26 (US), where she appeared to introduce various twists throughout the season, before being deleted by Makensy Manbeck after her victory in Part 2 of the Final Head of Household competition.

She was first presented as a woman, portrayed by Cosette Rinab, vying for a chance to be the 17th houseguest of the season, but was denied entry after not receiving a majority of the votes from either move-in group. Following this, her true status was revealed, taking the form of a blue CGI avatar that is voiced by Elise Dubois.

The first six houseguests evicted on Big Brother 26 voted "NO" to Ainsley entering the house.

In December 2024, her actress, Cosette Rinab, competed on the first season Amazon Prime's Beast Games alongside her sister Talia, who was eliminated in Episode 0. They also competed alongside future Big Brother 28 (US) houseguest Drew Campbell. She was eliminated in Episode 2.
