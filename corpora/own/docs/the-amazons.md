The Amazons was an alliance on Big Brother 20 (US)

Angela Rummans and Bayleigh Dayton formed “The Amazons.”

In week 6, Angela won HOH. Angela was secretly targeting Bayleigh due to her having a power app. Angela’s reasoning grew when suspicions that Bayleigh was the BB Hacker. After Angela won the POV, she decided to backdoor Bayleigh. This led to an explosive argument and confrontation between Angela, Bayleigh and Tyler Crispen. Bayleigh was then evicted by a vote of 6-1, thus officially ending the alliance.
