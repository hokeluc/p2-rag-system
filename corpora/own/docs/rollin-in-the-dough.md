# Rollin' in the Dough

The houseguests must crawl through a pool of sticky goo and slime back and forth, retrieving a certain number of items. The first team or houseguest to bring back the required number would win the competition.

The competition's most memorable appearance was on Big Brother 12 (US), where Kathy Hillis infamously got stuck in the caramel, ultimately costing her team the competition and making them the Have-Nots.
