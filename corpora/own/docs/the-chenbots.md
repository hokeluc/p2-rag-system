The Chenbots was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

Brittany Hoopes and Michael Bruner quickly formed a bond whilst inside the house. The two began working together on a game level early on and formed an alliance however, they didn’t name their alliance till week 5.

Brittany and Michael started off rocky. Brittany was placed backstage and Michael was nominated as the target. Michael won the POV however and saved himself. Due to Paloma Aguilar’s walk, Brittany was also safe. Brittany and Michael each joined a seperate alliance with Brittany joining the Girls Girls and Michael joining the Po’s Pack. Brittany and Michael formed a strong bond with Taylor Hale which led to a final three deal between the three. After the Festie Bestie twist was unleashed upon the house. Michael and Brittany became Festie Besties with each other. Originally, current HOH Matt Turner targeted Brittany as he felt she was untrustworthy. He then nominated the Festie Bestie duo of Brittany and Michael. In the POV competition, Turner and Jasmine Davis had to be disqualified due to Jasmine not feeling able to compete. Nicole Layog was also throwing the POV competition, in hopes her and Taylor would be the replacement nominees. Brittany and Michael then both went on to win the POV.

Main article: The Leftovers

After Daniel Durston publicly berated and attacked Taylor. The Leftovers alliance was created after the outsiders of the house realized the girls weren’t loyal to them.

Brittany and Michael continued to stay safe under the dominance of The Leftovers. Brittany and Michael used this time to grow closer and form close game relationship with Kyle Capener and Taylor. This led to them being in a comfortable position in the house. However, Michael’s competition wins started to turn the duo into a major target.

After the Split House Twist ended and the house reunited, Matt Turner won HOH. Turner and Kyle decided now was the time to turn on The Leftovers. Since they had formed The Afterparty with the two remaining outsiders. Turner targeted Michael viewing him as the biggest threat on the opposing side of the house. Turner decided the best plan of action was to backdoor Michael. Turner nominated Brittany and Taylor as they were his two closest allies. Michael got drawn for the POV luckily and was able to win. Brittany and Michael realized that Kyle had turned against them and used information from prior weeks to convince Turner to nominate Kyle after Michael vetoed Brittany. They were successful as Turner nominated him as the replacement nominee. Brittany and Michael were successfully able to save Taylor and Kyle was evicted by a vote of 5-0.

Michael continued his competition dominance by winning the next HOH. He targeted Terrance Higgins as he was open that he was targeting the duo and Taylor. Michael nominated him alongside Alyssa Snider. Michael ended up winning the POV and leaving the nominations the same. Brittany and Michael became targets for many in the house. Alyssa and Taylor wanted to target Michael as he was the biggest competition threat and they were closer to Brittany. Monte Taylor and Turner began to target Brittany, thinking there was an all-girls alliance. Taylor was able to convince them to target Michael however. Terrance was evicted by a vote of 4-0, however, it was a Double Eviction. After Turner won HOH, he nominated Alyssa and Brittany. After Monte won the POV he saved Alyssa and Michael was nominated and evicted.

Despite Brittany winning the POV the following week. In week 11, Brittany failed to win both HOH or POV. Ultimately, Monte cast the sole vote to evict on Brittany.

Both members voted for Taylor Hale to win.
