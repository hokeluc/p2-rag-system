The Unreliables is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Unreliables was created in week three, between America Lopez, Cory Wurtenberger, and Jag Bains as the trio had bonded through out their time in the house and were already in Family Style together.

Although the trio was initially on the bottom of the house, after Cameron Hardin won Head of Household in week six and nominated Izzy Gleicher and Felicia Cannon, America and Cory revealed to Jag the information they had that Izzy had leaked. This caused the trio to rally the votes to evict Izzy, going against what the previous power players, Cirie Fields and Jared Fields wanted. Their plan was successful as Izzy was later evicted. Despite Jared winning HOH, he had set his target on Cameron and successfully backdoored him. During this time, The Unreliables aligned with Blue Kim, Bowie Jane Ball, and Matt Klotz to run the game for the next few weeks, despite the cracks in the alliance, created by Blue and Cory.

After Jag won the secret Head of Household in week eleven, he quickly told America and Cory this information and his plan to target Blue in order to protect them. Later, Jag nominated Blue and Felicia. However, after Blue won one of the Power of Vetos, Jag quickly changed his target to the showmance as he deemed them a bigger threat than Cirie and Felicia to his true alliance with Bowie and Matt. Later, Blue took herself off the block and Jag took Felicia off the block, replacing them with America and Cory. With two members of the alliance on the block at the hands of another member, the alliance was destroyed with the members now actively targeting each other.

Jag Bains is the only member of the alliance to win a Power of Veto competition.
