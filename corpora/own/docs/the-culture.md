The Culture is an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

The Culture alliance formed during Week 9 under Barrett Pfeiffer’s Head of Household reign. Following the nomination of The Icons, Dee Valladares and Rick Devens secured a deal with Yash Patel and Taylor Brown. In exchange for voting out Angela to save Dee, the newly formed foursome agreed to shift their strategic focus toward dismantling the trio of the F.A.P. Alliance.
