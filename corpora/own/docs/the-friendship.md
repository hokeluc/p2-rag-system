The Friendship, also known as the "Nerd Herd" by some of its detractors, was an alliance on Big Brother 6 (US).

The Friendship was created early in the game. Though initially just a group of friends, the Sovereign Six’s creation and Eric Littmann’s eviction, led to Maggie Ausburn officially creating the alliance once she won Head of Household in week four.

With the house split down the middle, The Friendship seemed to be headed for an uphill battle as the Sovereign’s had more numbers and gained another number from Kaysar Ridha returning to the house. However, after Maggie was able to trick Howie Gordon in week five to turn on his alliance member James Rhine, The Friendship became the majority alliance for the remainder of the season.

After Ivette Corredero won the final Head of Household competition, she cast the final vote to evict on the last remaining member of the Sovereigns, Janelle Pierzina, leaving her and Maggie to be the final two. Ultimately, Maggie garnered 4 of the 7 jury votes, crowning her the winner of Big Brother 6 (US).

Every member of the alliance won a Head of Household competition.
The alliance members that were evicted were the same gender as the HOH that evicted them.
