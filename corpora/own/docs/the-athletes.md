The Athletes were one of the four cliques apart of the clique twist introduced on Big Brother 11. The four members were Jeff Schroeder, Jessie Godderz, Natalie Martinez, and Russell Kairouz.

This is the only Big Brother U.S. team (excluding duos) to have every member reach the jury stage.
This is the only team of its season to have four members.
Jeff is the only member to return for a future season.
