The Coven was an alliance on Big Brother 10 (US).

The Coven was created after their members April Dowling, Libra Thompson, and Keesha Smith realized Brian Hart was playing the house and succesfully turned the house against him.

The alliance was able to dominate the next few weeks with their affiliates and them winning competitions and successfully evicting two members of the other side of the house.

After April won HOH in Week 4, April intended to target Memphis Garrett, who she perceived as a threat. Jessie Godderz was nominated as the pawn as Dan Gheesling was able to convince April to nominate him. After the infamous Keesha’s birthday fight, Keesha and Libra defected from the alliance and began to go against April and campaign to keep Memphis. Keesha and Libra were successful as Jessie was evicted by a vote of 4-3. The alliance officially was over, and Libra and April were evicted within the next two weeks.

Every member of the alliance ended up on the Jury.
