# An Offer You Can't Refuse

The host tempts them with the prize in exchange for punishing the other houseguests. The one who accepts first will win the prize.

History[]

It first appeared in Big Brother 2 as a food competition. In the end, Bunky Miller won $5,000 in exchange for putting everyone else on peanut butter and jelly for four days.

It next appeared as a Veto competition in Big Brother 3. Danielle Reyes won the competition, winning her only Veto of the season.

It appeared as a Veto competition in Big Brother 4. Jee Choe won the competition, winning his only Veto of the season.
