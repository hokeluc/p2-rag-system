BASH (Be Awesome Stay Humble) was an alliance on Big Brother 20 that consisted of Angie Lantry (aka Rockstar), Bayleigh Dayton, and Haleigh Broucher.

Bayleigh is the only member of this alliance to win a Power App from the BB App Store while the twist was in play.
