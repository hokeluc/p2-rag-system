The Other People, also known as TOP, was an alliance on Big Brother 2 (US).

The Other People was created as a counter alliance to Chilltown, who made it very obvious about them working together in the game, and their obnoxious behavior in the house, led to The Other People in the house creating an alliance during Week 1.

After Nicole Nilson Schaffrich and Sheryl Braxton were nominated by Mike Malin, Nicole was able to convince the alliance to take a stand against Chilltown, who were targeting her, and to evict Chilltown’s pawn, Sheryl. Nicole was able to stay in the house, surviving a vote of 5 to 3, blindsiding Chilltown. Despite Krista Stegall taking out Autumn Daly, Hardy Ames-Hill was able to win his first Head of Household competition in week three. Hardy decided to nominate the showmance of Shannon Dragoo and Will Kirby, targeting Will, who was seen as the mastermind of Chilltown. The target eventually changed to Shannon, as Shannon decided to sabotage her own game to keep Will in the game. Later Shannon was evicted by a unanimous vote. After Kent Blackwelder won HOH, he targeted Mike, seeing him as a bigger threat than Will, due to his showmance with Krista and his competition prowess. Later Mike was evicted.

During Kent’s HOH reign, he went on a power trip and left many members of the house losing trust with Kent. After Hardy won HOH again, he nominated Kent and Will. The alliance began to crumble as Nicole had decided that she wanted to keep Will, as the two had formed an alliance. This led to alliance taking out Kent. After Nicole won HOH, Will informed her about the The Untouchables alliance. This caused Nicole to confront Hardy about the alliance. Nicole decided to nominate Krista and Monica, targeting Krista. This led to Monica’s defection from the alliance as she lost trust with Nicole and Hardy, even going on to have a heated argument with Hardy. Later Krista was evicted. After Hardy won his third HOH, he targeted Bunky, seeing him as the biggest competition threat left in the house, due to his wins in luxury and food comps. Hardy nominated Bunky alongside Will. Despite Monica campaigning to save Bunky, Nicole and Hardy cut a deal with Will and kept him in the game. Later Hardy cast the tie-breaking vote to evict on Bunky. After Will threw the HOH competition to Monica, Monica nominated the Hardy and Nicole duo, seeking revenge for Krista’s eviction. Will, seeking revenge for Shannon’s eviction, cast the sole vote to evict on Hardy. Despite two members of the alliance in Monica and Nicole being left in the game, they both were planning on taking Will to the final two. After Nicole became the final HOH of the season, she cast the sole vote to evict on Monica. On Finale Night, Chilltown member Will was able to receive 5 jury votes to Nicole’s 2, crowning him the winner of Big Brother 2 (US).

The Other People was the first alliance to consist of six or more people.
