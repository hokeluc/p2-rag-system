Million Club was an alliance on Big Brother 22 (US).

On Day 3 of Big Brother 22 (US), Ian Terry and Nicole Franzel formed the “Million Club” alliance. They formed it as the only former winners on the cast.

Later Nicole joined the dominant The Committee alliance. Nicole was put to the test in week 6, when Ian and committee member Tyler Crispen were nominated for eviction. In an emotional goodbye, Nicole decided to evict Ian. Seeing The Committee as a more beneficial alliance for her. Ultimately, Ian was evicted by a vote of 5-3, thus ending the alliance.

Both Ian Terry and Nicole Franzel are former winners.
Both members were on the jury.
As the jury vote was unanimous, both members voted for Cody Calafiore to win.
