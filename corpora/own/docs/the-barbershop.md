The Barbershop was an alliance on Big Brother 26 (US). The alliance was also known as the BS alliance, as most houseguests did not believe in it.

Cedric Hodges talked to Chelsie Baham about forming a similar alliance of The Pentagon, but without Quinn Martin. They talked about bringing in additional three other houseguest. Chelsie, had some doubts of the idea as Matt Hardeman, was one of the three new members, suggested to bring in.

On Day 5, the alliance was formed in the storage room of the house. Makensy Manbeck and Matt discussed after the formation, that they don't trust in the alliance as they don't have full trust with Cedric.

Matt became the first houseguest of the season to be evicted and in the alliance. Leah Peters and Makensy were the two members of the alliance voted to keep him in the house, which caused more to believe that there is no intent with the group. In Week 3, Cedric became HOH and tried to blindside one of the members, Makensy as a replacement nominee. Makensy later took herself off the block as she had a power to do so.

This alliance has the lowest-ranking and highest-ranking competitors from Big Brother 26.
