# Block and Roll

Block and Roll
is a recurring

Power of Veto
and

BB Block Buster
competition.

Rules[]

This competition is a variant of Perfect Shot:

1. The houseguests must use the allotted blocks to build the perfect path for their ball to roll down
2. They can test the path as many times as they want, but the only attempts that count are the ones where the ball is rolled through the tube
3. The first person to roll their ball through the tube and their path and land it into the box wins.

History[]

This competition first appeared in Big Brother 20 (US) during the Double Eviction. Angela Rummans was the first player to land her ball in the box, winning the Power of Veto

The competition appeared again in Big Brother 23 (US), where Derek Xiao won the first Power of Veto of the season

The competition reappeared in Big Brother 27 (US), this time as the BB Block Buster competition. Kelley Jorgensen managed to win, removing herself from the block.
