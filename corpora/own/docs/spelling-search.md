# Spelling Search

The

In this competition, houseguests look through an area for letters. One at a time, they must bring them back to their board. Once brought back to their board, they must spell a word out and try to achieve the longest, correctly spelled word, which will be revealed one at a time. For Big Brother 17 and Big Brother 18, each letter was assigned a point value, so instead of the longest word, the contestants need to come up with a word with the highest point value.

If there is a tie, the houseguest who did it the quickest will win.

The competition first appeared on Big Brother 11, during Week 1. Russell Kairouz won the competition, winning the first PoV of the season.

The competition reappeared on Big Brother 12, during Week 1 once again. Brendon Villegas won the competition, winning the first PoV of the season.

The following season, Big Brother 13, was the competition's next appearance. Brendon Villegas once again won the competition, winning his first PoV of that season. He used the exact same word as he used in the previous season.

On Big Brother 15, the houseguests competed in this competition, during the first week. McCrae Olson won the competition, winning his first ever PoV, alongside his first HoH win.

On Big Brother 16, the houseguests competed in this competition, during the first week. Donny Thompson won the competition, winning his first ever PoV.

The competition reappeared on Big Brother 17, during Week 1. Steve Moses, eventual winner, won the competition, securing his first PoV win of the summer.

In its next appearance, Big Brother 18, Paulie Calafiore won the competition, winning his first PoV of the summer.

After skipping the next summer edition, it reappeared on the special celebrity season, where Shannon Elizabeth won her first PoV and became the first female of U.S. franchise to win this competition.

The competition reappeared on Big Brother 26, during Week 1, Lisa Weintraub won the competition, securing her first and only PoV win of the summer. Lisa was the first female of U.S. civilian franchise to win this competition.

First appearing on Big Brother Canada 2, Kenny Brain won this competition, winning his second PoV of the season.

This competition is most commonly used during the first week.
Seasons 13 and 18 are the only American seasons to not have this competition in the first week.
