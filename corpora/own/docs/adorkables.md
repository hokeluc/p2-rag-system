Adorkables was an alliance on Big Brother 22 (US)

Adorkables was formed in Week 1, when Ian Terry, Kevin Campbell and Nicole Anthony formed the alliance.

Despite Kevin surviving eviction in Week 1, Nicole was nominated by Memphis Garrett. Despite not being the target, the house turned against Nicole due to her being viewed as a bigger threat than David Alexander. This and with Nicole going turning her back on Janelle Pierzina. Nicole was quickly evicted by a vote of 10-2.

Ian and Kevin remained aligned loosely for the rest of the game. Kevin tried to save Ian during Week 6, but was unsuccessful. This left Kevin as the sole remaining member of the alliance and he was evicted two weeks later.

Nicole Anthony is the only female member of the alliance.
Nicole Anthony is the only member of the alliance to be evicted pre-jury.
All the competitions won by this alliance, were won by Kevin Campbell.
