Team Dan was one of the four teams formed as part of the Coach Twist introduced on Big Brother 14 (US). Each team consisted of four returning "coach" veterans and their respective new players. This team was led by former winner Dan Gheesling, who coached and allied with Danielle Murphree, Jodi Rollins, and Kara Monaco.

Team Dan was notable for having a strong strategic leader in Dan Gheesling, often considered one of the best players in Big Brother history. Under his guidance, the team focused on loyalty and strong social gameplay. Dan’s coaching and competitive prowess helped his teammates manage nominations and build alliances.

This is the only team to feature 3 females and 1 male.
Dan Gheesling was the only returning coach who placed as high as Runner-Up during this season.
