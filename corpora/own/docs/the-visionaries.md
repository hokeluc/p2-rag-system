The Visionaries is an alliance on Big Brother 26 (US).

The Visionaries was formed during Week 2 by Kimo Apaka, Quinn Martin, and T'kor Clottey. The name of the alliance references the fact that all three members wear glasses.

Upon entering the game, Quinn was awarded the Deepfake HOH after winning the Upgrade competition. In Week 3, after his secret power was leaked to the entire house, Quinn chose to use the Deepfake HOH in the following week. During discussions Quinn revealed he was at the bottom of The Pentagon alliance. In Week 4, Quinn nominated Cedric, Makensy, and Tucker, with Tucker as his primary target. Tucker won the POV and removed himself from the block, leading Quinn to nominate Rubina, Tucker's showmance. Cedric, Makensy, and Rubina faced off in the AI Arena, with Makensy emerging victorious. The Visionaries then decided to flip the vote against Cedric, who was aligned with the current majority alliance. The plan succeeded, and Cedric was evicted 6-3 without Quinn being informed beforehand.
