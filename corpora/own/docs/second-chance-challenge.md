# Second Chance Challenge

The

Each week before the final eviction vote, the final three nominated contestants would compete in a last chance competition to save themselves. The winner of each competition would then be saved from the eviction vote with no chance to replace the nominee by reducing the three nominees to final two nominees.

The competition was later introduced to the American franchise in Big Brother 26 (US) as the AI Arena. With the success of the concept, the twist returned for Big Brother 27 (US), according to Julie in the premiere episode the twist would be a permanent aspect of the game, known in subsequent seasons as the BB Block Buster.

In the Chilean and United Kingdom versions, this competition has been named the Salvation competition.

At the start of Week 9, the remaining houseguests were introduced to Second Chance Week. On Day 60, the three nominated houseguests Adriana Fernandez, Christina Podolyan, and Sarah Jane Adams competed in the first ever Second Chance Challenge. In order to earn safety, the nominated houseguests had to build a 60-piece puzzle wall and then demolish it using a swinging wrecking drum. Sarah Jane demolished her puzzle first, saving herself from eviction.

Continuing Second Chance Week, an additional Second Chance Challenge was held on Day 61. As the nominated houseguests for the round, Ari Kimber, Daniel Hayes, and Sid Pattni competed for safety by attempting to balance on a slackline while holding a rope for as long as possible. After Ari and Sid both fell off of the slackline, Danny was crowned the winner and was saved from eviction.

As the final nominees, Kenney Kelley, Kimo Apaka, and Matt Hardeman competed in the first AI Arena competition, called "Glitch in the System". A series of looping videos of the other houseguest played on screens around the arena. The nominees had 2 minutes to determine which houseguest's image appears the most times. The winner would be the most correct answer in the fastest time. Kimo correctly guessed that Quinn appeared the most, earning safety for the week.

Angela Murray, Lisa Weintraub, and Tucker Des Lauriers competed in "Security Race". The players had to arrange pegs on the floor to correctly assembly a puzzle. Tucker completed the puzzle the fastest and earned safety.

Due to the activation of America's Veto, the viewers voted for Quinn Martin to be the replacement nominee. Immediately after this was revealed, he completed alongside Kenney and Tucker in "Data Dump". The nominees had to collect 20 red data balls amongst data balls flying around their boxes. There was no penalty for incorrect balls, but they did not count toward the total. Tucker collected all 20 balls the fastest and earned safety.

Nominees Cedric Hodges, Makensy Manbeck and Rubina Bernabe competed in "Upload, Download", where players had to roll 5 smaller balls on top of a ramp and then knock them off with 5 larger balls. Makensy narrowly beat Rubina to win the competition and earned safety.

Brooklyn Rivera, Cam Sullivan-Brown and Chelsie Baham competed in "Digital Decryption", in which players had to unscramble strings of letters to help them assemble 3 puzzles. After the players took so long to finish that the letters were unscrambled, Chelsie won the competition and earned safety.

Angela, Makensy and Tucker competed in "Puzzling Password", in which players had to solve a 10-piece puzzle in order to reveal a 10 digit number, which they would have to find from a collection of numbers, and plug it in. Makensy won her second AI Arena competition and earned safety.

Joseph Rodriguez, Kimo and Rubina competed in "Bandwidth Boosters", where they had to unravel a rope that they were tethered to from a large frame so that their rope would be long enough to plug it in to a socket and win. Rubina won her first competition and earned safety.

Amy Bingham, Kelley Jorgensen, and Zae Frederich competed in "Ransom Note", where they have to unscramble four words to reveal a phrase in order to find the correct object. The first one to find the correct answer wins. Kelley won with the crown being the correct object.

Adrian Rocha, Will Williams, and Amy competed in "Puzzling Prints". The players would arrange puzzle pieces to correctly form a footprint. Adrian completed the puzzle first and earned safety.

Adrian Rocha, Kelley, and Will competed in "Study the Study", where the players must find the correct number of differences between the two rooms that are nearly identical to one another within a minute and a half. The first player to find the correct answer or close to the correct answer without going over wins and if there's a tie, the person who locks in the answer first wins. With the correct answer of 8 differences, Kelley came closest with 7, winning her second BB Blockbuster and earning safety.

Jimmy Heagerty, Rylie Jeffries, and Kelley competed in "Safe Crackers", where the players must navigate three balls one at a time through a giant maze replicating a combination lock to land each one of them in three marked combination numbers. Rylie won the competition and earned safety.

Keanu Soto, Vince Panaro, and Zach Cornell competed in "Jumbo Gemstones", where the players must move one gemstone at a time to make four columns of the same colored gemstone. Keanu won the competition and earned safety.

Mickey Lee, Morgan Pope, and Rylie competed in "Settle the Score", where the players must roll a ball down their lane in an attempt to get the highest score within 2 minutes. They can discard their score if it's lower than the highest score by another player. With a score of 38, Mickey won the competition and earned safety after Morgan and Rylie each scored 29.

Ava Pearl, Katherine Woodman, and Kelley competed in "Hilltop Heist", where the players must navigate their ball down a sloped ramp where they must strategically place puzzle pieces to help guide their ball down the ramp. Kelley narrowly beat Katherine, winning her third BB Blockbuster and earning safety.

Ashley, Mickey, and Vince competed in "Forensic Frenzy," where they must grab colored balls while they fly in the air and place them in the correct sequence in their tube. If any of their color balls was accidentally placed in the incorrect order, they can release the bottom of their tube to start over. Vince won the competition and earned safety.

Ashley, Keanu, and Will competed in "Key to Safety", similar to Big Brother 26 where the players have to unravel a long rope that was tethered to a large frame in order for their rope to be long enough to plug their key into the socket. Keanu won his second BB Blockbuster and earned safety.

Lauren Domingue, Keanu, and Kelley competed in "Steal Power Orbs for the MasterMind So He Can Bring His Lair On-Line", where they have to retrieve power orbs from a ball pit in an attempt to land them into a small tube. The first player to land all four orbs within 4 minutes will win and if no one lands all four power orbs within the time, the person who lands the most orbs the fastest will win. Lauren won the competition and earned safety.

Ashley Trail, Taylor Brown, and Yash Patel competed in "Do Over" where they had to watch video slideshows of their fellow houseguests around the room and then lock in the houseguest they believed appeared the most. The first people to lock in their answer the fastest within 2 minutes will win. Yash was the first player to correctly guess Mallory, earning his safety for the week.

Jason De Puy, Melody Morris, and Rome Seymour competed in "Pirate's Booty" where they had to move one piece of pirate treasure at a time to make four columns of the same colored treasure. Jason won the competition and earned safety for the week.

Jason, LaTrice Verrett, and Mallory Aurichio competed in "Video Tape Tumble" where they have to remove video cassette tapes from their tower and transfer them one at a time to their rental drop-off bin within two minutes . The catch is that they have to remove the tapes without knocking their tower over; if their tower collapses at any point regardless before the two minutes are up, how ever many of the tapes they have transferred will be locked in. The person to transfer the most video cassette tapes the fastest without knocking their tower within two minutes will win safety. Mallory won the competition with transferring eight video tapes and won safety for the week.

Drew Campbell, Lyric Medeiros, and Melody competed in "The Paw Patrol Dino Puzzle", inspired by the new movie Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie, where the players have to put together a puzzle with the background showing first and then placing the pieces with the characters in the movie in the blank spots where the pegs will go. The first person to complete their puzzle and press their button will win safety. Drew completed the puzzle the fastest and earned safety for the week.

Barrett Pfeiffer, Chuk Anyanwu, and Haley Thogmartin competed in "Newton's Apples" where the players have to roll 5 smaller colored balls up a ramp in a small patch of grass and to try and roll 5 bigger balls to knock down their balls. Haley won the competition and earned safety.

Angela, Haley, and Kamu Kirk competed in "Space Race" where they have to collect 10 red balls and 10 yellow balls floating in the air and in their own respective colored tubes. If any ball isn't in the assigned colored tube, they can open the bottom of their tube to start over. In a close finish, Angela managed to grab all of her balls first and won safety.

Drew, Mallory, and Taylor competed in "BB Grand Cliptactular". A series of video loops from past Big Brother seasons were split into three sections and the players have to run to one side where different clues would appear one at a time for a total of three clues where they have to identify the correct section that features all of them. The first player to answer correctly first would win. Drew won his second BB Blockbuster even after the second clue was revealed and earned safety.

LaTrice, Rick Devens, and Taylor competed in "Your Game in Ruins", where they have to retrieve spheres from a ball pit in an attempt to land them in a small tube. The first player to land all four spheres the fastest would win and after four minutes, if no one lands four spheres within that time frame, it is the person who lands the most spheres the fastest first that wins. Devens won the competition and earned safety in a close finish with Taylor.

Angela, Dee Valladares, and Rick Devens competed in "Reinvent the Wheel", where they have to retrieve puzzle pieces from one side to another and then put together their wheel puzzle together with both sides being exactly the same to match the pictures. The first player to solve their puzzle first would win. Devens won his second consecutive BB Blockbuster and earned safety.

Barrett, Melody, and Taylor competed in "Crash Site Cover-Up", where they have to roll five balls on a platform that will tilt and have to land them on their receptacle. The first player to land all five balls first would win and if anyone who doesn't land all five balls within four minutes, it is the person who lands the most balls in the least amount of time. Melody won the competition and earned safety.

During press interviews for Big Brother 27 (US), Julie revealed that the AI Arena twist was not originally planned to last seven weeks on Big Brother 26 (US). However, production chose to extend it due to positive feedback both internally and from the fanbase.
Big Brother 28 (US) is the first season where every houseguest competed in at least one Second Chance Challenge competition.
Tucker Des Lauriers was the first houseguest to compete in the Second Chance Challenge competition in consecutive rounds.
He was followed by Amy Bingham, Adrian Rocha, Will Williams, Kelley Jorgensen, Ashley Hollis, and Keanu Soto all on Big Brother 27 (US) and Jason De Puy, Haley Thogmartin, Taylor Brown, and Rick Devens in Big Brother 28 (US).
He was also the first houseguest to win consecutive Second Chance Challenge competitions. He was followed by Rick Devens.
He was the only AI Arena winner in BB26 to be evicted before the Final 5.
Amy Bingham was the first houseguest to lose consecutive Second Chance Challenge competitions.
She was later followed by Will Williams, Ashley Hollis, and Taylor Brown.
Leah Peters and T'kor Clottey are the only houseguests who made it to the end of the AI Arena twist in BB26 without ever entering it. They both did so by avoiding nomination entirely.
Due to being evicted via a Hit The Road competition and never receiving a nomination either, Rachel Reilly became the only houseguest on BB27 to not compete in a Block Buster. However, she was eliminated before the twist concluded.
Kenney Kelley, Angela Murray (on BB26), Amy Bingham, Will Williams, Ashley Hollis, Taylor Brown, LaTrice Verrett, and Barrett Pfeiffer have all entered at multiple times on a single season without winning.
However, Angela won the Second Chance Challenge competition in her second season.
Taylor Brown has lost the most Second Chance Challenge competitions on a single season at four times giving her the lowest win loss ratio (0:4).
Melody Morris is the only houseguest to win the Second Chance Challenge competition on their third time competing in a single season.
Kelley Jorgensen is the first houseguest to win three Second Chance Challenge competitions, but her third win came on her fourth time competing.
Angela Murray won on her third time competing overall in her second season, after losing her first two on her initial season.
Kelley Jorgensen has competed in the most Second Chance Challenge competition, with five, and she faced a different set of opponents every single time.
Adrian Rocha is the first houseguest to compete in consecutive Second Chance Challenge competitions where he won his first time and losing his second time. He would be followed by Jason De Puy, and Haley Thogmartin.
Adrian and Jason were evicted the same day they lost their second consecutive Second Chance Challenge competition while Haley survived.
Rubina Bernabe, Vince Panaro, Angela Murray, and Melody Morris are the only houseguests whose first win didn't coincide with the first time they competed.
Angela Murray is the first houseguest to compete in the Second Chance Challenge competition in two different seasons.
She is the first houseguest to win their first Second Chance Challenge competition on their second season.
Tucker Des Lauriers, Makensy Manbeck, Keanu Soto, Lauren Domingue, Yash Patel, Rick Devens, and Drew Campbell are the only Second Chance Challenge winners to also win at least one HOH and one POV competition on a single season.
Mickey Lee was HOH (along with winning a POV and BBB) but did not win it via the HOH competition.
Angela Murray won all three challenges, but accomplished it across two seasons.
Drew Campbell is the first houseguest to win the BB Block Buster and then win the following Head of Household competition, followed by Melody Morris. However, Drew had to face the Year 1000 twist competitions before his HoH win, so Melody is the first houseguest to complete this feat uninterrupted.
