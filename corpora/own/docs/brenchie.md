Brenchie is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consisted of Brent Champagne and Frenchie French.

Frenchie won the first Head of Household competition to become HOH for the week. Frenchie intended to nominate Brent due to his goal of targeting strong competitors early on, dubbing them as "meatheads". Frenchie's plan was sailing smoothly until him and Brent had a heart-to-heart sometime before the nominations and Frenchie ultimately decided not to target Brent anymore and the two of them made a Final 2 deal together instead. The two became close throughout Week 1, with Frenchie including him in the majority alliance he created called The Slaughterhouse. Brent even convinced Frenchie to nominate Travis Long after veto was used on Kyland Young.

In the second week, Frenchie became the house target after his chaotic HOH reign. Brent and the rest of the Slaughterhouse ended up abandoning Frenchie in order to save their own games.
