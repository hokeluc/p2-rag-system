The Bookends is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

Bonding as the two eldest women in the house, Cirie Fields and Felicia Cannon instantly aligned together during the first night. However, the alliance wasn’t officially named until Week 2 (Day 12).

On Day 27, Felicia grew increasingly uneasy about her final two alliance with Cirie. She decided to approach Jared Fields, seeking a more secure alliance. Subsequently, Jared disclosed Felicia's actions to Cirie.

As tensions mounted, in Week 6, when Felicia became the target, both Jared and Cirie failed to advocate for her safety. This led Felicia to believe that their loyalty lay more with each other than with her, causing a rupture in their trust.

Week 7 brought a Fake Double Eviction, where Cory Wurtenberger won the HOH competition. He nominated Blue Kim and Jared, designating Jared as the primary target. In a last-ditch effort, Cirie approached Felicia, Bowie Jane Ball, and Mecole Hayes, seeking their votes to save Jared, only to be met with resistance. Cirie felt deeply betrayed by their refusal and their claim of not being the majority.

As the tensions continued to escalate, Week 8 witnessed the return of Jared to the house as a "BB Zombie" due to the Fake Double Eviction. In a meeting with Felicia, he, alongside Cirie, confronted her about casting a vote to evict Jared, emphasizing their feelings of betrayal. Jared, however, would fail to re-enter the game against Cameron Hardin.

On Day 61, they resolidified their trust with each other. However, a foe in Bowie Jane would become the new HOH, leading to several suspecting that she would nominate the Bookends in an act of revenge upon them not informing her of the Week 5 vote flip on Red Utley. With Bowie Jane finding herself strategically controlled by Jag Bains and Matt Klotz in the Mafia alliance, their plan to backdoor physical threat in Cameron would be executed instead, and he would be evicted unanimously over Cirie.

The Mafia would begin to win every HOH and nearly every POV from Cameron's eviction onward, and as a result of their lack of physicality and loss of trust with nearly every other outsider, the Bookends served little purpose other than as pawns. Upon the eviction of America Lopez at the final 6, only the Bookends would remain apart from the Mafia, and Cirie would be evicted unanimously on Day 93, followed by Felicia by Jag's sole vote on Day 96, marking the Bookends' defeat. The duo would also be the only two to vote for Matt to win over Jag in the jury, as the latter would win the game in a 5-2 vote.
