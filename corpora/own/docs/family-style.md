Family Style is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US). The alliance includes America Lopez, Blue Kim, Cameron Hardin, Cory Wurtenberger, Jag Bains, Jared Fields. Matt Klotz and Reilly Smedley. The eight person alliance was formed after the creation of The Handful, which is a sub alliance with five members.

After becoming Head of Household (HOH) of the week, Reilly Smedley celebrated her win with her 4 other roommates (Blue Kim, Cameron Hardin, Jag Bains, and Matt Klotz) of the scary themed room in the HOH room. Agreeing to all their own strengths to build a strong group, they then form The Handful alliance. They agreed if they wanted to run the house long term, they would need to pull in more housemates. They later revealed the group to America Lopez, Cory Wurtenberger and Jared Fields, and they agreed to form the big alliance called "Family Style".

Hisam Goueli, became the second HOH, and confronted Matt and Reilly separately about the Family Style alliance and learned that he was a strong target for them in the future. Hisam and Felicia Cannon (both in the The Professors alliance), mutually agreed targeting Reilly for eviction would then crumble this and the Handful alliances. Reilly would later be evicted by a 12-0 vote and the alliance would disband as America, Cameron, Cory, and Jared defected.
