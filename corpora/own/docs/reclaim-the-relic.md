# Reclaim the Relic

This competition was first used during the premiere of Big Brother 27 (US) where the houseguests had to search for the matching relic to one shown. Jimmy Heagerty won the competition.

The competition later appeared again in the premiere of Big Brother 28 (US) where houseguests had to find the "BB-tonium" vials that had numbers adding up to 28. Lyric Medeiros was the first to find her vial, though there was no extra prize for finishing first - the goal was to simply not be in the bottom two. Eventually, Ashley Trail and Barrett Pfeiffer were the last ones remaining, and since they failed to find the correct "BB-tonium" vials before the other 12 houseguests, they were not eligible to compete in the later immunity rounds.
