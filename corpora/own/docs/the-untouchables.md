The Untouchables was an alliance on Big Brother 2 (US).

Early in the game, Krista Stegall, Hardy Ames-Hill and Monica Bailey created The Untouchables alliance. Their strategy was to use cards to show who they wanted to evict that week, so that no other houseguests found out about this alliance, as it was meant to be a secret alliance.

The Untouchables proved to be a strong alliance, winning 5 total Head of Household competitions. They were able to stay safe under their competition wins and their relationships with each other and the other houseguests.

During Week 5, Krista told Will Kirby about the alliance. Will proceeded to inform Nicole Nilson Schaffrich about the alliance. After Nicole won the next HOH competition, she targeted the alliance, and after she confronted Hardy about the alliance, Hardy gave her the go to nominate the other two members. Nicole decided to nominate Krista and Monica alongside each other. Taken by surprise, Krista and Monica searched for answers and after they found out about Hardy betraying them, causing the three to engage in an argument. Later Krista was evicted by a vote of 3-0, being seen as a bigger threat than Monica. Krista’s eviction weakened the alliance even more, with Hardy and Monica having very little trust with each other. After Monica won HOH during Week 8, she nominated Hardy and Nicole for eviction. Will decided to cast the sole vote to evict on Hardy. After Monica lost the final HOH competition, Nicole cast the sole vote to evict on her, thus officially ending the alliance in defeat.
