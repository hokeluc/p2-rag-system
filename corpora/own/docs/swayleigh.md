Swayleigh was an alliance and showmance on Big Brother 20 (US).

Swayleigh was created quickly upon entering the house. Bayleigh Dayton and Chris Williams quickly grew close and took their relationship to a game level.

Swayleigh was blindsided when their ally Steve Arienta was evicted. This left the duo confused and paranoid. After former ally Kaitlyn Herman won HOH, Tyler Crispen convinced her to Backdoor Swaggy. Kaitlyn agreed and after Tyler vetoed Scottie Salton, Kaitlyn nominated Swaggy. Despite this Swaggy and Bayleigh were sure they had the votes to stay. However, come eviction night, Swaggy was evicted by a vote of 8-4. Bayleigh was evicted soon after by a vote of 7-1, after she failed to use her BB App Store power and a disastrous HOH reign.

On Finale Night, Swaggy C proposed to Bayleigh and she accepted. They got married on February 17, 2019 and are still together today.

Both members of the alliance were backdoored.
