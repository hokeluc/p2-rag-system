# Temptation Competition

The

The first competition of the season was a Temptation Competition that gave the houseguests a chance at taking a cash prize, but as a consequence, the Big Brother Swap took place. Following Jessica Graf's acceptance of the Den of Temptation, the competition returned to the house. For weeks 5, 6 & 7, houseguests have the option to opt in to compete in the Temptation Competition. The winner of the competition will win immunity for the week while the last place finisher will be automatically nominated as a third nominee.

The immunity variant of this competition would return in Big Brother 21 (US), while the luxury variant would return in Big Brother 28 (US).

The 16 houseguests were sequestered in separate pods and were given the offer of $25,000. The first houseguest to press their button would win the money. The identity of the person who pressed the button would remain anonymous, however their actions would unleash a consequence onto the winner and the house as a whole. Alex Ow, Cody Nickson, Jason Dent, Kevin Schlehuber, Mark Jansen, Ramses Soto and Raven Walton all pressed their buttons, but Kevin was the fastest. Since he accepted the offer, it required him to throw the first Head of Household competition. As another consequence for the button being pressed, Paul Abrahamian from Big Brother 18 entered the House as the 17th houseguest, setting off the Big Brother Swap, which ended up causing Cameron Heard to get evicted, with Paul taking his place.

Alex Ow, Jason Dent, Mark Jansen, and Matt Clines played in the first new Temptation Competition. The competition was Bowlerina and Mark ended up winning immunity, while Jason got last place and was the third nominee. Jason ended up getting vetoed, leaving the HOH's two nominees on the block. This competition was done one at a time, with an audience.

This week, everyone who was eligible played in the competition except for Christmas Abbott. The competition was Strangest Things and Cody Nickson ended up winning immunity, while Jessica Graf got last place and was the third nominee. Jessica ended up getting evicted that week in a 7-1-0 vote. This competition was played individually and privately and was timed.

Once again, everyone who could play did, except for Christmas. The competition was Where Were You? and Mark won immunity for the second time, while Matt got last place and was the third nominee. Matt ended up winning the POV. This competition was played all at once by everyone.

In Season 21, the competition was brought back in its Immunity variation at the Final 10, known as "America's Field Trip". Three houseguests were voted into the competition, where the winner would earn immunity, the runner-up would earn a punishment, and the loser would be nominated. Jackson Michie came out on top, earning himself safety, while Analyse Talavera survived and was left with a chicken costume as her punishment. Christie Murphy was left remaining and became the third nominee, before getting vetoed later on in the week.

In Season 28, at the Final 5, the initial Luxury variant was brought back. The houseguests were all gifted $1,000, but were all then tempted with their own button. The first person to push their button would earn $10,000, leaving everyone else with nothing. And unknown to them, they would also unleash a "Pharaoh's Curse" upon the house, shortening the game week to just 3 days. If no one pushed the button, everyone would keep their $1,000. Rick Devens, Drew Campbell and Melody Morris all pushed their button, but Devens was first and earned himself the $10,000, while also letting loose the curse.
