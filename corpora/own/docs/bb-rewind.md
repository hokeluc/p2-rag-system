BB Rewind (also known as Big Brother Rewind) is a spin-off after show held for Big Brother 22 (US) hosted by Keshia Chanté.

The first after show attempted by CBS began in 2004 with House Calls: The Big Brother Talk Show The series primarily featured a discussion about events occurring in the Big Brother house. House Calls aired every weekday alongside new episodes of Big Brother from Big Brother 5 until its cancellation prior to season 11 in 2008. The next companion show began in 2012 with former Big Brother houseguest Jeff Schroeder known as Big Brother: Live Chat. The show aired each week following the live eviction when Schroeder would interview the evicted houseguests. However, On August 10, 2017 Schroeder announced that he was moving to Colorado and would no longer be able to host the show. Ahead of season 20 it was announced by Julie Chen on Twitter that Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US) runner-up and winner Ross Mathews and Marissa Jaret Winokur would be filling in the gap left by Schroeder with a new show called Off the Block. Off the Block finished with season 20, and there was no after show for season 21. Before the second episode of season 22 aired, the official Big Brother Twitter account announced the new after show, with Keshia Chanté as host.

The show airs on Friday or Saturdays, a day or two after live eviction episodes of Big Brother 22 and is a Facebook Watch exclusive.

Each episode generally consists of the following format in order:

Breakdown: Keshia and the guest discuss the biggest moments from the recent episodes of Big Brother.
Interview: Keshia interviews the most recent evicted houseguest. In Episodes 4 and 5, the segment instead showed an extended interview with Julie Chen-Moonves, filmed immediately after the most recent eviction episode. In Episode 6, both interviews were featured as opposed to one over the other.
Live Feed Exclusive: An exclusive highlight from the Big Brother Live Feeds that is only shown on BB Rewind.
