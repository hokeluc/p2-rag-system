The Secret Alliance was the dominant alliance on Big Brother 3 (US).

Composed of the unlikely duo of Danielle Reyes and Jason Guy, the alliance flew under the radar to secretly usurp control from the initial power structure in the house, the Cartel. Using their strategic savviness and great social positioning to navigate the season, both Danielle and Jason reached the final three, along with affiliate Lisa Donahue. With Lisa winning the final Head of Household, Jason was evicted by Lisa for being a huge social threat. Danielle reached the final two, but eventually lost to Lisa in a landslide 9-1 vote.

For their high-level gameplay, The Secret Alliance is considered as one of the most iconic alliances in the show's history, with Danielle being recognized as arguably one of the greatest players by fans and players alike.

The Secret Alliance was created during Week 1. After being left out of the initial power structure, Danielle Reyes and Jason Guy created the alliance during the first few days of the game, dedicating their loyalty to each other.

Despite the duo being blindsided at Lori Olsen’s eviction, their ally Marcellas Reynolds won the next Head of Household competition, leaving them room to maneuver and play the game. After Marcellas nominated Josh Feinberg and Tonya Paoni, two members of the initial majority alliance, Danielle and Jason aided in evicting Tonya. They viewed Josh as someone on the outskirts of the majority alliance and thus more likely to flip.

Despite Danielle and Jason losing an ally in Amy Crews in Week 3, they regained some power after Gerry Lancaster won Head of Household. Gerry targeted the showmance of Eric Ouellette and Lisa Donahue, placing them on the block alongside each other. During the past few weeks, Danielle had been getting closer to Lisa and starting to talk game with her. This led to the duo deciding they needed to keep Lisa as an important number in the future.

The duo created a plan where they would vote opposite ways, resulting in Gerry breaking the tie to evict Eric. However, after Chiara Jude Berti leaked that she was flipping her vote, Jason joined Danielle in voting out Eric. After Chiara won the Week 5 Head of Household competition, Danielle and Jason managed to convince her to go after Josh for his untrustworthiness, despite him being a member of her alliance. Later, Chiara put Josh on the block, and he was subsequently evicted in a unanimous vote.

After Amy returned to the game through a house vote in which Danielle and Jason both voted for her, she won Head of Household. Amy used her power to target Chiara, whom she had a long-time rivalry with, nominating her and her showmance Roddy Mancuso. Despite Danielle pushing to evict Roddy, Chiara was evicted by a vote of 4-1.

In Week 7, the duo won their first Head of Household competition. Danielle continued to push her anti-Roddy agenda this week. However, Jason refused to target him once again due to their close bond. Jason ended up nominating Amy and Gerry, with the intent of evicting Gerry for an easy week. Later, Gerry was evicted unanimously.

Despite Amy and Roddy being nominated by Marcellas the following week, trouble started to brew once Amy won the Power of Veto and stated that she wanted to use it to save Roddy, due to a deal they made and their close bond. However, Danielle and Jason managed to convince her not to do this, as they told her this move would result in her personal eviction. Later, Amy discarded her veto, and Danielle was finally able to evict Roddy.

Despite attempting to throw the next Head of Household competition, Jason won by accident. Seeing Marcellas as their biggest competition threat, the duo targeted him, placing him on the block next to Amy. After Marcellas won the Power of Veto, Danielle was able to convince him not to use it by telling him that Amy was going home either way and that not using it and forcing Jason to make a replacement nominee would make the house happy. Marcellas obliged to this idea and discarded the veto. However, Danielle and Jason immediately stabbed Marcellas in the back as they voted to evict him, with Jason even casting the tie-breaker vote.

After Danielle won her first Head of Household competition, she left Jason off the block. Despite Lisa posing more of a threat to them in competitions, the duo decided to evict Amy due to seeing her as less deserving to be in the final three.

Despite strategically dominating the season, the duo’s dream of making it to the final two together ended once Lisa won the final Head of Household competition. Lisa decided to evict Jason, due to her stronger relationship with Danielle. In the end, Danielle’s diary rooms and Lisa’s likability lead to her victory in a landslide 9-1 vote, with Danielle only receiving Jason’s vote to win.

The Secret Alliance is the first official alliance to reach the Final Three on Big Brother (US).
By extension, it is also the first alliance to be made in Week One and last all the way to Final Three without losing a member.
Danielle and Jason would eventually start a podcast named after this alliance in 2020.
Future houseguests Rachel Reilly and Hannah Chaddha would eventually join as co-hosts as well.

In Matt Hoffman's interview with Sharon Tharp on "The Exclusive," he shared that his strategic approach in Big Brother 12 (US) was inspired by The Secret Alliance gameplay he observed, and he referred the duo of Jason and Danielle from earlier seasons. Matt explained that he admired how their secret duo remained undetected and he wanted to emulate this, but then adapted the strategy by establishing a covert core group, which formed The Brigade alliance. He credited the math and numbers aspect of their strategy as stemming from his own perspective of the game.
