Jokers is one of the four teams that are a part of the team twist introduced on Big Brother 23 (US). The team consisted of Azah Awasum, Britini D'Angelo, Derek Frazier, and Frenchie French. The teams dissolved when the team competition ended after week four.

Frenchie won the first competition of the season, apart of the live move in, becoming one of the four winners that later became Team Captains. He was the first member of Team Jokers and selected three fellow houseguest (2 girls and 1 guy). After the selections, Team Jokers won the first Head of Household (HOH) competition, which resulted in Frenchie becoming the first HOH and his team immune from the first eviction.

In week 2, Frenchie was the first teammate to be nominated for Eviction, in the team, quickly followed by Britini. The week's HOH, Kyland, nominated Frenchie due to the chaos that he brought to the house and their alliance and nominated Britini due to her loyalty to Frenchie and the Jokers. Before nominations, Frenchie volunteered himself to play in the Wildcard Competition, after a team deliberation on who should compete. Frenchie had loss the competition, if he had won it would have granted him safety from nominations. The week's Power of Veto winner, Derek X, did not use his power to take someone off the block.

A selected teammate will compete in a new Wildcard Competition. That houseguest would compete for sole immunity, meaning that if they win, they will be given the option to take safety, while the rest of the team remains vulnerable for nomination and eviction. If the winner decides to accept safety for the week, it will result in a punishment that may affect them, their team, or the entire house. Due to the team winning the HOH competition in week one, the Jokers were not eligible to compete in the Wildcard Competition.

Frenchie declined the first Double or Nothing Twist on Day One. Had he accepted and won the challenge, it would immune him and the rest of the team from eviction for another week. Had he accepted and lost, Frenchie would had been dethroned as HOH and his entire team would be vulnerable for the first eviction.
Two of the Jokers, Azah and Derek F, made it to Finale Night, placing 3rd and 2nd respectively.
The Jokers won the least amount of competitions, with just three wins.
