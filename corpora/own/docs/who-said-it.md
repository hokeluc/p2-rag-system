# Who Said It?

The houseguests are asked a series of questions about the evicted houseguests. Houseguests competing in the competition have to answer who they think said the statement that is given to them, and if they get it right, they score a point. This continues for a number of rounds.

The houseguest that has the most points at the end of the competition becomes the new HoH.

The competition was first used during Big Brother 3. Houseguest Jason Guy won the competition, winning his first HoH of the summer.

The following season, Big Brother 4, houseguest Justin Giovinco won the competition, winning his first HoH of the season.

Appearing for the final time as "Who Said It?" in Big Brother 5, houseguest Nakomis Dedmon won the competition, winning her second HoH of the summer.

The competition then appeared as a variant titled "Diary Room Confessions" in Big Brother 10, where houseguest Renny Martyn won her first and only competition of the season.
