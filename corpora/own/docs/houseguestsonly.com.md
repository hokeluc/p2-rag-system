# HouseguestsOnly.com

In this competition, the houseguests must decipher a series of clues to match the correct label to each scenario. The houseguest(s) that correctly labels all scenarios in the fastest time wins. Each competition has a different theme, and the format varies tends to vary from season to season.

Big Brother 20 (US): Debuted as a Power of Veto competition, with individual timed runs. Tyler Crispen had the fastest time and won the POV.
Big Brother 21 (US): Returned as the "Nightmare" Whacktivity competition. Ovi Kabir was the first houseguest to correctly match all three smells, winning the Nightmare Power. However, he was evicted before he was able to use it.
Big Brother 23 (US): Used for the Head of Household competition. Xavier Prather had the fastest time and was crowned HoH.
Celebrity Big Brother 3 (US): Used as the Power of Veto; Carson Kressley won the POV and saved himself from eviction.
Big Brother 25 (US): Returned as the Johnny Mac-themed Head of Household competition. Jag Bains was the first to correctly match all five skulls, becoming the fourth houseguest to win HOH two weeks in a row.
Big Brother 26 (US): Featured as the opening night Downgrade competition for the first group of houseguests. Chelsie Baham matched the fewest correct emotions in 90 seconds, downgrading her game to a BB Mascot.
Big Brother 27 (US): Used as the first Power of Veto competition of the season. Ashley Hollis won the competition, securing her safety for the week.
