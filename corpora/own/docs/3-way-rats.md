3 Way Rats is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Three Way Rats was created between America Lopez, Cory Wurtenberger, and Matt Klotz in week four. The alliance was created as America and Cory wanted to lock down their relationship and alliance with Matt.

America Lopez is the only member not to win a competition.
