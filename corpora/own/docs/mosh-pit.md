The Mosh Pit was an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

Mosh Pit was created in Week 6 as a merger between F.A.P. and The Icons. Their primary goal was to ensure that Kamu Kirk was evicted soon, although most remained unaware of the Diamond Power of Veto which would allow that to happen in the same week.

At the veto ceremony, Devens used the Diamond Power of Veto on Dee and put up Kamu as the replacement, and the Mosh Pit agreed with this.

Due to Angela mentioning previous alliances and the Crossovers talking together without them, Melody and Mallory grew suspicious of being there being a previous alliance and that they were actually add-ons. Furthermore, Yash Patel's consistent campaigning and their friendship with Taylor Brown caused them to doubt overtly going against their wishes. They discussed possibly saving Kamu and questioned Drew on whether he was loyal to them or the Icons.

Drew told Devens of this interaction and encouraged him to spend more time with Melody and Mallory so that they will not doubt Mosh Pit. However, this caused Devens to worry about Drew's close friendship with the other members of the Mosh Pit and he told Dee and Barrett that he may flip on them along with Melody and Mallory. Barrett told Dee and Devens that Drew encouraged LaTrice to view Devens as a threat during her HoH, further driving a wedge in the Mosh Pit.

Mallory and Melody continued to worry about the Mosh Pit and whether it would last after Kamu's eviction. They told Drew of their plan to save Kamu, but he successfully dissuaded them against it. Melody continued to question Drew about his loyalty to the Mosh Pit, but he insisted that the Mosh Pit was not two alliances merged. However, the following day, Mallory and Melody decided against evicting Kamu due to a continued lack of support from other Mosh Pit members. Incensed, Devens planned to make a speech if Kamu was not evicted revealing both the Mosh Pit and the Crossovers. He told Drew of this a few hours before the live episodes, threatening to expose his entire game and get him evicted if Melody or Mallory do not vote with them.

During the period that the BB Time capsule was in the house, America only voted for members of this alliance to enter the time capsule, with the only member to not enter it being Barrett
Mallory is the only member to get evicted before the Jury phase
