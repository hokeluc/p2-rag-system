The Icons is the dominant alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

Composed of former players from both Big Brother and Survivor, The Icons enjoyed dominance and control in maneuvering the eviction results through their effective social game, clever use of advantages, and key competition wins, thwarting several coup attempts by their enemies and striking pre-emptively against people who were forming ill-intent against their game. While Angela Murray was eventually evicted over Dee Valladares, Dee and Rick Devens managed to reach the final four, but after outsider Taylor Brown won the final three Head of Household and their ally Drew Campbell won the final Power of Veto of the season, Dee and Devens were nominated, with Drew casting his vote against Dee. Ultimately, in a do or die situation, Devens was able to clinch the Final Head of Household and eventually, the victory for his risky and fearless dominant gameplay in a 6-1 jury vote over Taylor.

The Icons immediately connected due to Dee and Devens being friends outside the house, and Angela being the only other returning player. When Dee won the first HoH, the trio used it to solidify a main alliance alongside Drew Campbell and Barrett Pfeiffer called The Crossovers with them in the center. Dee and Devens were also a part of the power alliance The Red Corner, but to ensure their main alliance did not become paranoid, Devens informed them of its name and the members.

The veteran players maintained strong connections throughout the house through Drew, Barrett, and the Red Corner, successfully getting out Ashley Trail in week 1 when she almost gave away their alliances to Melody Morris. Devens won both HoH and veto in week 2, and was provoked by Angela into making a large move, targeting Rome Seymour and Jason De Puy. They orchestrated a merge between the Crossovers and Red Corner into the The Toolshed. Rome was successfully evicted by the end of the week. However, throughout week 1 and week 2, both Angela and Dee had become increasingly paranoid about Drew and his friendships with those not in the Toolshed. In week 2, Devens also won the Time Capsule prize of the Diamond Power of Veto, only informing Dee although Angela suspected he had won.

In week 3, Red Corner member Kamu Kirk won the HoH and planned to target Jason with Mallory Aurichio as the back-up target. Both Drew and Barrett wanted to save Mallory, with Drew continuing to campaign for her even after Barrett stopped, further inciting the paranoia between all three Icons. Devens, recognizing Kamu and Drew were close, purposefully separated the pair and made sure the Red Corner were also distrusting of Drew. Dee successfully convinced Barrett not to warn Drew about this, as he was worried about becoming a target for defending him. Dee also won the BB Bribe in the blockbuster, and lied to both Devens and Angela about it, leading to suspicion that Mallory had won.

In week 4, another Red Corner member in Haley Thogmartin won HOH. She wanted to target Melody, Taylor Brown, and La Trice Verrett. However, Devens, Dee, and Angela swayed her to nominate Drew after Haley opined that she could not nominate "three girls," had promised safety to Yash, and needed a male pawn on the block initially. Unbeknownst to Drew, Barrett, Haley and the Red Corner were then convinced to target him due to his perceived untrustworthiness, with his close ally and "flirtmance" Melody as the secondary target.

During the week, Drew's blind loyalty to the Toolshed alliance worked in his favor, eventually convincing Dee that Drew should remain in the game as a shield and someone to target the Red Corner later on. Dee convinced Devens of this strategy, and Barrett and Angela were persuaded to save Drew if he lost the Blockbuster. A few hours before the blockbuster, Dee grew worried about Lyric Medeiros due to her friendship with the Red Corner, believing that Melody was a better option to keep, and convinced the other Icons to save her as well. That week, Drew won the Blockbuster, allowing the Crossovers to successfully blindside the Red Corner by evicting Lyric over Melody.

The Toolshed imploded due to the Icons betrayal, which was exacerbated when the first non-ally to the Icons won HoH in LaTrice. LaTrice initially planned to target the icons, specifically Devens, but was convinced otherwise by her allies and instead targeted the Red Corner. Dee and Angela also decided to lie about their votes, both claiming to have evicted Melody, leading to more distrust. Angela was racked by guilt and admitted the truth to Haley and Chuk Anyanwu, causing large fights and Dee to admit her rogue vote to Kamu along with her power. Dee also informed Devens of her power, feeling she could not lie anymore after Mallory publicly lost the Time Capsule that week. Despite Haley remaining the main target, on eviction day Dee decided that Chuk was a bigger issue to the Icons and successfully flipped the vote for him, although this was ultimately unnecessary due to Haley winning the blockbuster.

Dee and Devens threw the week 6 HoH, annoying Angela, and Yash won. Dee and Devens were confident in this win, but Drew and Yash separately revealed to them Yash's plan to nominate both Angela and Dee with Angela as the target. Panicking, Dee planned to use her bribe to force him to nominate Drew instead, but ultimately offered it to him to just keep her and Angela safe, which he refused. Yash also won the veto, and planned to keep nominations the same with Dee as the new target. Devens agreed to use the DPoV on Dee, with Dee telling Angela of this power. They merged with the F.A.P. alliance, creating the Mosh Pit , to ensure their safety when this move was made. Furthermore, information leaked from Drew revealed that Kamu had turned on Dee, leading to Devens to decide he would be the new target.

Devens went through with the plan and saved Dee, nominating Kamu in her place. However, their lack of reassurance to Mallory and Melody caused them to doubt if the Mosh Pit was real or simply a way to ensure this move did not backfire on the trio. Drew encouraged Devens to spend more time with them to cement the alliance, worrying Devens about his loyalty as well as Mallory and Melody's. Devens and Dee threatened both Drew and Barrett, promising to expose the Crossovers, the Toolshed, the Mosh Pit and F.A.P. to the house if they did not convince them to evict Kamu. Angela won the blockbuster and Kamu was evicted.

Dee won the HoH in week 7, regaining power from the prior week and planned to target Yash, Taylor, and LaTrice. However, Mallory and Melody's actions the prior week caused her to consider blowing up the Mosh Pit and nominating Mallory. She and Devens also continued to distrust Drew, although Angela now did trust and want to work with him. Dee planned to use her bribe on Drew to force him to always protect her and Devens for the rest of the game, threatening to nominate him and his allies if not, but this was ultimately not allowed by the powers rules.

Angela was the only member to not win any Head of Household competitions.
All members were competing on at least on their third appearance in a reality competition show.
