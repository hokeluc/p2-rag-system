The 7 Deadly Sins (previously known as the Savage 7) is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The 7 Deadly Sins (originally named the Savage 7) was created towards the end of week three. The alliance included Blue Kim, Cirie Fields, Felicia Cannon, Izzy Gleicher, Jag Bains, Jared Fields, and Matt Klotz.

On Day 30, after Jag was evicted by a unanimous 10-0 vote against fellow alliance member Blue, Matt opted to use the BB Power of Invincibility to save his 7 Deadly Sins ally Jag and thereby cancelling the eviction — keeping both of Cameron Hardin's final nominees in the game.
