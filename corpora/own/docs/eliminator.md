# Eliminator

The contestants will be asked a question and the first person to answer correctly gets to eliminate a houseguest of their choice. If they answer incorrectly, they will be eliminated. Last houseguest left standing will win.

This competition originally appeared on Big Brother 6 (US) in Week 5.
