The Mafia is the dominant alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

It formed late in the season as Bowie Jane Ball, Jag Bains, and Matt Klotz came together to work as a trio. The alliance grew out of their earlier connection with The Minutemen and became stronger once Bowie Jane won Head of Household. From there, the three helped each other stay safe and take control of the endgame. Their wins in the closing weeks let them influence key evictions and keep their side in power. By the time the house was down to the final three, The Mafia had become the dominant force in the game.

The Mafia began in Week 10 as a trio between Bowie Jane Ball, Jag Bains, and Matt Klotz, although the alliance was not officially named until Matt’s Head of Household reign in Week 13 at the final 5. The alliance was an extension of The Minutemen, who wanted to make a strong alliance with then-HOH Bowie Jane.

After Bowie Jane finished her HOH reign by successfully and unanimously evicting a large threat in Cameron Hardin, Jag won 2 consecutive HOHs (1 secret) and POVs, which he used to evict Cory Wurtenberger and Blue Kim by unanimous votes. During Day 86's Double Eviction, Bowie Jane would win HOH again and Matt POV, ending in the eviction of America Lopez by a 3-0 vote.

The Mafia kept in power again with Matt winning HOH and Jag POV at the final 5, evicting Cirie Fields by a 2-0 vote. Finally, Bowie Jane won her third HOH and Jag his record-breaking seventh POV, with the latter evicting Felicia Cannon with his sole vote.

After Felicia was evicted, The Mafia became the only three houseguests remaining, and officially dissolved. Matt won Part 1 of the Final HOH and Jag won Part 2. Jag would win Part 3 and evict Bowie Jane with his sole vote, subsequently winning the game over Matt by a jury vote of 5-2.

The Mafia won every HOH during the jury phase.
One of their members would also win the Power of Veto every possible week of the jury phase, but not every POV, due to the BB Power of Multiplicity giving Blue Kim a bonus POV in Week 11.
Bowie Jane Ball is the only member to not win a Power of Veto competition.
