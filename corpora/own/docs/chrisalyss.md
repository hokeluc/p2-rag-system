ChrisAlyss is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consist of Alyssa Lopez and Christian Birkenberger.

Christian won the first competition of the season, apart of the live move in, becoming one of the four winners that later became Team Captains. He chose Alyssa as his second member of his newly formed team, Kings. The two (in week one) got close to one another, which led to the first Head of Household (HOH), Frenchie to consider them as a Showmance. Frenchie intended to target Christian immediately due to his wish of getting a "meathead" out of the game first, however, Christian ended up winning the first Wildcard Competition of the season, earning himself safety and ruining Frenchie's plans. Instead, Frenchie decided to nominate Alyssa due to his suspicions of a Showmance between the two of them.

Sometime after week 2, the two of them ended up becoming an actual Showmance. During Christian's HoH week in week 4, he successfully used his power to vote out Whitney Williams unanimously after hearing that she had plans to target the both of them as a Showmance. However, in week 5, when Derek Xiao won HoH, he culminated a plan to break up the Showmance before The Jury phase of the game begun, specifically targeting Christian rather than Alyssa due to his challenge prowess. Christian and Alyssa campaigned together to keep the former in the game and almost had their way, but Christian was ultimately voted out anyways. Alyssa and their other Kings teammate, Xavier Prather, were the sole votes to keep Christian.

Despite both of them intending to pursue their relationship after the show, Alyssa and Christian both announced that they had broken up less than a week after the finale.
