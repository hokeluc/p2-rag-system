# Knockout

Two people are picked to go up to a podium and they will be asked a question about a picture.
The first player to buzz in with the correct answer eliminates their opponent. They then choose the next two to face off.
If they answer incorrectly, then they are eliminated and their opponent advances.
If nobody buzzes in within a certain time frame(if the completion is played live) they will both be eliminated.
The last player standing becomes the new HOH.
