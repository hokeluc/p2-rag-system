Drenchie is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consist of Derek Frazier and Frenchie French.

Derek and Frenchie immediately aligned together on day one with Frenchie picking him to be on the Jokers team, saying that Derek reminded him of his best friend. Frenchie and Derek were each other's number ones in the beginning of the game, making a Final 2 deal with each other in only the first couple days. After Frenchie's chaotic HoH reign during the first week, in week two, Kyland Young nominated Frenchie for eviction. Derek was the only one of Frenchie's allies to stick with him in his final week while the rest of them distanced themselves from Frenchie in order to save their own games. Derek cast the sole vote to keep Frenchie while the rest of the house voted to evict him, thus ending this alliance.
