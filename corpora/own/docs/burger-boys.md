Burger Boys is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The Burger Boys alliance, an all-male group, came together on Day 8, during the first week of the season. Vince Panaro and Zae Frederich lead its formation, imagining a five person pact that would include Rylie Jeffries, Will Williams, and Zach Cornell. The alliance's name was a nod to the popular Five Guys burger chain. Zae came up with a nickname for each Burger Boy member: Rylie as the "barbecue sauce," Vince as the "cheese," Will as the "lettuce," Zach as the "patty," and Zae as the "buns," with each ingredient of a burger intended to resemble how they functioned in the alliance.

Their formation was created after Vince's victory as the first Head of Household of the season. Ironically, Vince's first act was to nominate Zae, perceiving him as a significant challenge threat and believing he was already seen as the "Mastermind's Accomplice" by other houseguests. The goal of the alliance was to help keep an even ratio of male and female houseguests, as there were nine women and eight men in the house.

Despite the alliance's quick inception, Zae ultimately became the first houseguest evicted in Week 1. This outcome was largely due to Rachel Reilly's efforts, who successfully rallied votes to keep Amy Bingham as a strategic move against any male alliances in the house. Two of the three members of the Burger Boys alliance that were eligible to vote, voted to keep Zae, with Rylie being the sole exception.
