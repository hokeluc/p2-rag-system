ShellTown was a Final Two alliance on Big Brother 17 (US) that consisted of Jace Agolli and Austin Matelson. The alliance was not successful as Jace was the first houseguest to be evicted while one of their two affiliates, Julia, was a jury member and Austin, one of the two members in the alliance, ended up finishing in 5th place. However, their other affiliate, Liz became the runner-up.
