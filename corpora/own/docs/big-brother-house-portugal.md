Second House[]

The second house, originally constructed for Secret Story Portugal, was utilized solely for Big Brother VIP in 2013.

Located in the same exact spot as the original Big Brother house, this new complex was reconstructed in the year 2010, as the original structure had been demolished in 2004, following the discontinuation of Big Brother by TVI.

This new Big Brother house underwent a remarkable transformation to introduce one of the Big Brother's most memorable twists: the division between the 'Rich House' and the 'Poor House'.

Third House[]

To celebrate the 20th anniversary of the format in Portugal, TVI decided to rent a luxurious mansion to produce a commemorative edition. With a selling price of 6.5 million euros, the property stood out for its technology and innovative design.

The Big Brother production rented the house and carried out a partial remodel. Although adapted as much as possible for the reality show, the house retained its charm and luxury, providing a unique environment for housemates and viewers.

The mansion was located in Ericeira region remained a landmark in the show's history, symbolizing a memorable chapter during its successful journey, having been utilized for three editions between 2020 and 2021.

Current House[]

The current house was built during the summer of 2021 after being meticulously planned to adapt to the new concept of future-oriented housing. The project involves prefabrication in a warehouse.

This new construction became a necessity after the previous house in Ericeira, rented by the production of Big Brother between 2020 and 2021, was vacated. With the termination of the rental agreement, there arose the need to have a dedicated space for future productions, prompting the construction of the new facility.

The choice of location was carefully considered, taking into account various factors. Despite the challenges, Malveira was selected due to its proximity to the Venda do Pinheiro studios, where the Sunday galas take place.
