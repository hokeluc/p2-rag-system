Queens was one of the four teams on Big Brother 23 (US).

Claire Rehfuss won the first competition of the season, granting her the team captain for the Queens. She chose Kyland Young, Tiffany Mitchell and Travis Long to be apart of her team.

After losing the Team Captain competition and the Wildcard Competition. All members of the Queens were left vulnerable as none of them remained safe. HOH Frenchie French, decided to nominate Kyland Young as a pawn next to Alyssa Lopez. However after Alyssa was able to convince him to change his target and Derek Xiao winning the POV, Travis became the target. Later Travis was nominated as a replacement nominee and evicted by a vote of 11-2.

The Queens managed to stay relatively intact up until the final 8. Despite Tiffany Mitchell being HOH, after Alyssa the original target won the POV. Tiffany decided to backdoor Claire. Claire was then evicted by a vote of 4-1. Once again, Kyland won HOH and betrayed the team and targeted Tiffany for eviction. Tiffany was successfully evicted by a vote of 3-0. Thus leaving Kyland the only remaining member of the Queens.
