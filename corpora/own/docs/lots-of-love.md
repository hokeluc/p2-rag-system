Lots of Love was an alliance on Big Brother 21 (US).

Lots of Love was created in Week 2 between Christie Murphy, Jack Matthews and Tommy Bracco as a sub alliance inside Gr8ful.

For the first few weeks of the game the alliance proved to be successful, working alongside the other Gr8ful members and later Six Shooters members to run the game.

On Day 44, Jackson Michie brought up to Jack the idea of targeting Christie and that Holly and Kathryn Dunn knew each other from outside the house. Jack quickly brought this information back to Lots of Love and Analyse Talavera. This prompted an argument between the Six Shooters, which led to Jackson and Holly leaving the alliance. After Jessica Milagros won HOH, she targeted Jack for eviction, nominating him alongside Jackson. With Jessica as HOH, this gave the other side of the house an opportunity to form a counter alliance to the previous dominating alliance. Despite campaigning from the alliance, the other side of the house had solidified Jack as the target and he was evicted by a vote of 6-2. Christie and Tommy aligned with Analyse and Nick Maccarone and Tommy kept them safe by winning HOH and POV in Week 7. However, Analyse and Nick were evicted the following weeks. This left Christie and Tommy on the outs of the house, yet they managed to stay safe by Tommy using the POV on Christie the following week. After it was announced to be a Double Eviction, Nicole Anthony won HOH and targeted the duo. She nominated them alongside each other and Christie was evicted by a vote of 3-0. After Tommy failed to win HOH or POV, he was evicted by a vote of 2-0, thus officially ending the alliance.

All members of the alliance voted for Jackson Michie to win.
