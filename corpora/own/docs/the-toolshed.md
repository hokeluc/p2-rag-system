The Toolshed was an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

The Toolshed was formed during Week 2 as a merger between two major power structures in the house: The Crossovers and The Red Corner. The alliance was established to consolidate control and execute a major backdoor strategy against Rome Seymour. The name of the alliance was coined by Drew Campbell.

For the first month of Big Brother 28 (US), The Toolshed and its sub-alliances (including The Red Corner, The Crossovers, and The Icons) dominated the game by winning every Head of Household competition and systematically evicting houseguests on the other side of the house. However, despite their absolute control, internal friction quickly began to form among its members. Drew became a focal point of distrust within the alliance due to perceptions from both The Red Corner and The Crossovers that he was playing both sides, compounded by his close bond with Melody Morris. Meanwhile, Angela Murray, Dee Valladares, and HOH Haley Thogmartin held constantly fluctuating opinions regarding Drew's loyalty. Additionally, alliance dynamics grew more complex due to Kamu Kirk's growing friendship with Lyric Medeiros, which originated during her showmance with Rome and grew stronger during Kamu's Week 3 HOH reign.

During Week 4, Haley made the first official internal strike against The Toolshed by nominating Drew alongside Melody and Taylor Brown. Pitching Drew on the idea that he was merely a pawn, Haley actually made him her primary target. Drew subsequently failed to win the Power of Veto, which was won by Taylor.

Following Taylor's Veto win, Haley then pivoted her replacement nominee plans from Mallory Aurichio to Lyric, aiming to minimize blood on her hands while testing Kamu's loyalty by asking him to be present when informing Lyric of her nomination. Taylor used the Veto on herself, and Haley officially named Lyric as the replacement nominee. Drew's target status marked the first major rift inside the alliance, signaling a breakdown in trust between sub-factions like The Red Corner and The Icons.

With Drew remaining on the block alongside Melody and Lyric, he remained entirely unaware of his precarious standing, continuing to believe he was an untouchable pawn. However, late on Day 30, Dee had a sudden strategic realization: because Drew was inherently loyal to The Icons, evicting a reliable number like him was a tactical blunder compared to sending home Melody, who would actively target them. Over the course of the night, Dee and Rick Devens successfully maneuvered a last-minute vote flip within The Toolshed to spare Drew and redirect the crosshairs onto Melody.

On Day 31, the dynamics shifted once again mere hours prior to the eviction to save Drew and Melody. Realizing they could use both Drew and Melody as weapons to eventually target their own allies in The Red Corner, The Icons mounted a second campaign to save Melody and target Lyric instead. The campaign successfully rallied a new majority. Drew successfully wins the BB Blockbuster competition. With Drew safe and only Melody and Lyric remaining as nominees, The Icons executed their strategy shift, blindsiding Lyric in a shocking eviction vote and leaving Chuk, Haley, and Kamu stunned, exposing deep, irreversible fractures within The Toolshed.
