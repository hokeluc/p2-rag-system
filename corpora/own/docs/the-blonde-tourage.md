The Blonde-Tourage was an alliance on Big Brother 15 (US)

Their strategy was to get out the "floaters". They planned on using whoever they could to help further themselves in the game.

The alliance began to implode in week one after Kaitlin Barnaby became jealous of Jessie Kowalski for becoming too close to Jeremy McGuire. Kaitlin and Jessie then began to get into an argument. Leading to their being unwanted tension throughout the alliance.

Following the POV meeting, David Girton and Jessie were nominated. Thankfully for the alliance their target and house outsider Elissa Slater was nominated as well. However after Helen Kim and The Moving Company managed to flip the majority of the house in David. David was evicted by a vote of 7-5-0 much to the shock of the alliance.

Despite Aaryn Gries winning the following HOH. She targeted Elissa, however due to her winning the MVP twist. She nominated Nick and the same people who evicted David last week. However, they gathered the votes to get Nick evicted this week. Then for the next two weeks, Jeremy and Kaitlyn were evicted. Thankfully for Aaryn and GinaMarie Zimmerman they were able to find spots and alliances in the house. Unfortunately for Jessie, she became an outsider and was evicted at the final 9.

Main article: 3 A.M.

During the week of Jessie’s eviction, Amanda Zuckerman, Andy Herren, McCrae Olson and Aaryn formed an alliance called 3 A.M. The alliance looked to be successful as Aaryn won her 4th HOH and nominated power duo Elissa and Helen for eviction. Later Helen was evicted.

Following Helen’s eviction, Elissa won HOH. This spelled bad news for the alliance as none of them had the best relationships with her. Later she nominated Aaryn and McCrae for eviction. Despite Amanda winning the POV and saving McCrae. Elissa still nominated Andy. Later Aaryn was evicted by a vote of 5 to 0.

Main article: The Exterminators

Right before Aaryn’s eviction, GinaMarie joined Andy, Judd Daugherty and Spencer Clawson to form The Exterminators. This proved to be a very successful alliance as Andy, GinaMarie and Spencer ended up making it to the final 3.

Andy won the final HOH competition. He decided to bring GinaMarie to the finals. Unfortunately for her GinaMarie lost in 7-2 vote. She didn’t even receive old alliance member Jessie’s vote.
