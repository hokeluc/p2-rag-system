Cartel was an alliance on Big Brother 3 (US).

Beginning as early night one, Cartel was created by Josh Feinberg, who quickly began to play the game hard. Despite a few replacements in members, by the end of Week 1, Cartel had solidified a strong six.

Being a six-person alliance, Cartel quickly became the majority alliance. After Lisa Donahue became the first Head of Household, Cartel targeted Marcellas Reynolds. Lisa decided to nominate him and Lori Olsen. After Gerry Lancaster won the Power of Veto, he vetoed Marcellas. This caused the alliance to distrust him and he was kicked out of the alliance. Lisa decided to nominate Amy Crews as a replacement nominee. Cartel decided that Lori was a greater overall threat. Lori was evicted by a vote of 5-4. Despite outsider Marcellas winning Head of Household and Tonya Paoni being evicted, Roddy Mancuso won Head of Household and reclaimed their majority position. Roddy decided to target his friend Amy, whom many in the alliance wanted evicted. Later Amy was evicted by a vote of 7-0.

After their old alliance member Gerry won Head of Household, he targeted Eric Ouellette for eviction, deeming him the biggest threat of the Cartel alliance. Gerry nominated him alongside Lisa. The remaining members of the alliance decided that Eric was better to keep. They decided to campaign to the house to evict Lisa. After Lisa was told this information, she decided to defect from the alliance, in favor of aligning with The Secret Alliance. Later Eric was evicted by a vote of 4-3, after Gerry cast the tie-breaking vote to evict on him. After Chiara Jude Berti won Head of Household, Danielle Reyes was able to convince her to turn on Josh Feinberg, who had gained a reputation for being untrustworthy. Chiara decided to nominate her Showmance, Roddy alongside him, seeing Roddy as a vote to keep Josh. Later Josh was evicted by a vote of 5-0. After Chiara betrayed Josh, the alliance was down to just the Chiara and Roddy, as Lisa had already turned on the showmance. Later Amy returned to the game, leaving Chiara and Roddy as the house outsiders. Later Amy won Head of Household and nominated Chiara and Roddy. Later Chiara was evicted by a vote of 4-1. Two weeks later, Roddy was evicted by a vote of 3-0.

Despite the odds against her, Lisa managed to make it to the final two. On Finale Night, Lisa became the winner of Big Brother 3 (US), winning by a jury vote of 9-1.

Every member of the alliance voted for Lisa Donahue to win.
