Big Brother Live Chat (known specifically as Big Brother Live Chat with Jeff Schroeder and later the Big Brother Aftershow and the Post Eviction Interview) is a spin-off after show held from Big Brother 14 (US) until Big Brother 19 (US) hosted by Big Brother 11 (US) & Big Brother 13 (US) houseguest Jeff Schroeder.

For Big Brother 18 (US), the show re-branded as the Big Brother Aftershow. In addition to speaking with an evicted houseguest every week, Jeff would be speaking with past houseguests about the game and their life after the show. Guests to the show would replace cases in which the evicted houseguest would immediately go back into the house or were immediately sent back into sequester.

For Big Brother 19 (US), the show reverted back to the Post Eviction Interview on the Live Feeds after the eviction. Jeff Schroeder's last episode would be on July 28th, 2017 as he would go on to move to Denver, Colorado to work for the new show, Daily Blast Live. The show would go on to be cancelled after the double eviction on August 18, 2017.
