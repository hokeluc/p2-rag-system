Double the Fun is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

In the second week, Rachel Reilly suggested a final two with Ava Pearl due to the imminent eviction of her closest ally, Amy Bingham. In Week 3, the two solidified their final two and named it.

In Week 5, Ava won the HOH 'Knockout' competition, swore her loyalty to Rachel, and re-solidified her final two.

The name is a double entendre with the acronym "DTF".
