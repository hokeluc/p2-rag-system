Coast2Coast was an alliance on Big Brother 20 (US)

On Day 2, Tyler Crispen and Kaycee Clark formed a final 2 deal. Later they named the alliance “Coast2Coast.”

Main article: Level Six

Shortly after the alliance was formed, Tyler and Kaycee joined the dominant Level Six alliance. Where Tyler and Kaycee used the alliance to dominate the game.

Tyler and Kaycee found themselves in the final three with affiliate JC Mounduix. After Kaycee won the final Head of Household competition. She chose to bring Tyler to the final 2. Kaycee ended up winning by a vote of 5-4 becoming the winner of Big Brother 20 (US).

Coast2Coast (along with The Hitmen and both editions of The Renegades) is the most successful two person alliance in the US version of Big Brother, with both members reaching the final two in the same season. They would be followed by The Root, The Black-Ops and The Minutemen.
