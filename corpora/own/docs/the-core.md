The Core, also known as CCC, is an alliance on Big Brother 26 (US).

The Core was a sub-alliance within the Pentagon created week 1. However, over time, Chelsie began to become closer to Brooklyn. This, along with other issues Chelsie and Cedric had with Cam, caused them to become closer to eachother and exclude Cam from their final 3 plans. Once Cedric and Brooklyn were evicted, Chelsie and Cam began to work closely again.
