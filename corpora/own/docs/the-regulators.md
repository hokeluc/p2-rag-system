The Regulators was an alliance on Big Brother 13 (US).

The Regulators alliance was formed in order to combat the Golden Key twist and the returning players. It was formed by Dominic Briones early in the first week.

Due to three consecutive HOH wins by the Veterans alliance, the new players were left to their whims. During the first week, The Regulators were shocked when Keith Henderson was evicted over Porsche Briggs. Following Keith’s eviction, Cassi Colvin was backdoored. Then Dominic was evicted the following week. Despite affiliate Daniele Briones winning HOH and keeping the sole remaining member Lawon Exum safe. Lawon was evicted the following week as infamously offered to be nominated and evicted. His wishes were granted and he was evicted after he then lost the battleback to Brendon Villegas.

Other than Dick Donato who walked, this alliance made up the entirety of the pre-jury.
This is the only alliance in Big Brother 13 (US) to have a male as its highest ranking member.
Lawon was the only member to make it to the individual phase of the game.
Thus far, this is the only alliance to be evicted in succession (but not in four consecutive weeks).
Every member of this alliance was recruited for the show.
