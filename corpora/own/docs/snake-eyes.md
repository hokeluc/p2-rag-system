Snake Eyes is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US). The two of them officiated their alliance in week 3.

Claire and Tiffany immediately aligned when Claire chose Tiffany to be on the Queens team with her at the beginning of the game. The two of them expressed interest in targeting the guys early and having the girls go far in the game, working on a couple side-alliances together like Jackpot and The Kingslayers. The two of them would often spend time together and talk game together the most. In week three, the two of them agreed on a Final 2 deal and called their alliance "Snake Eyes". Claire explained to Tiffany the meaning behind the name was "when you roll two ones because we're both number ones". However, in cam talks, Claire expressed interest in cutting Tiffany right before the end and Tiffany expressed that her true loyalty was with her mission in The Cookout. The two of them still cared for each other and trusted each other the most out of anyone else in the game, though.

In week eight, Tiffany won HOH and intended to target Kyland and Sarah Beth. However, the Secret HOH was in play in the High Roller Room and that meant that anyone in the house could easily dethrone Tiffany as HOH and change her nominations. To prevent this from happening, Claire saved her BB Bucks and spent them on playing in the Secret HOH competition (which was only played by herself and Derek Frazier). Claire won the competition and won the coin toss, making herself the Secret HOH of that week. Even though Claire wanted to take out either Kyland or Xavier, she kept Tiffany's nominations the same due to her loyalty to Tiffany. Claire immediately told Tiffany when she won the Secret HOH and Tiffany was happy for her.

In week nine, the members of the Cookout (besides Tiffany and Hannah Chaddha) wanted to take out Claire. However, with Tiffany and Claire being so close, Tiffany wanted Claire to stay for another week and won another HOH with intentions to target Alyssa Lopez instead. Unfortunately for Tiffany and Claire, Alyssa won the veto that week, leaving Claire as the only non-Cookout member left. Tiffany took Claire into their room and explained to her that she couldn't bring herself to put up a black person after the many seasons of racism black people on the show had to endure, and that she had no other option but to put Claire up. Claire sobbed and told Tiffany that she completely understood, even though she was sad that she had to go. The two hugged it out and cried, with Claire begging Tiffany to make it to the end of the game because she didn't want to vote for anybody else to win. Tiffany chose not to tell Claire about the Cookout alliance and sadly put Claire on the block and sent her home that week, ending this alliance.
