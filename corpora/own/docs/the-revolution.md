The Revolution is an alliance formed on Day 1 of Big Brother 18 (US) as soon as the Returners - Frank Eudy, Da'Vonne Rogers, James Huling, and Nicole Franzel - entered the house. They made an agreement to target the Returners.

Jozea Flores is the only member to not make it to the jury stage.
