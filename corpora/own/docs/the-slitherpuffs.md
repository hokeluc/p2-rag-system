The SlitherPuffs was an alliance on Big Brother 20 that consisted of Angie Lantry and Haleigh Broucher.

The SlitherPuffs was formed in Week 1, as a sub-alliance to the FOUTTE. Worried about the stability of their alliance, Rockstar and Haleigh formed a final two deal and vowed to work together. The two formed back-up plans as a way to keep each other safe if FOUTTE failed to work together.
