Tiffney is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consist of Tiffany Mitchell and Whitney Williams.

Tiffany and Whitney began an alliance together in the first week due to their bond over both being single mothers. However, this alliance was very short-lived and quietly ended the very same week, after Tiffany and the rest of the house began to distrust Whitney's other closest ally at the time, Frenchie. The two of them did not interact much anymore after week one and Tiffany contributed to Whitney's unanimous eviction in week 4.
