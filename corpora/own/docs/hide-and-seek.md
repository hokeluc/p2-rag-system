# Hide and Seek

Houseguests are given a set amount of time to observe an area. After the set time is up, they are given statements about which areas are safe to hide in. Players who do not choose a spot that satisfies the statement are eliminated. The process continue until there is one houseguest remaining who is crowned the Head of Household.

History[]

On Big Brother 25, the competition was held in a large open area, and each "hiding zone" had a limit to how many houseguests could hide in that zone.

On Big Brother 27, the competition was changed so that there was no limit to how many houseguests could choose one room to hide in, but they needed to lock in their answers in the Diary Room prior to the reveal of which rooms were safe.
