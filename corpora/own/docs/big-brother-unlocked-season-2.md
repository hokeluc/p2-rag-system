Big Brother: Unlocked/Season 2 is a spin-off after show held for Big Brother 28 (US) hosted by a panel of alumni.

On May 13, 2026, it was announced that Unlocked would return for a second season alongside Big Brother 28 (US).

Later on June 30th, it was announced that Taylor Hale and Derrick Levasseur would be returning to host the second series alongside Jerry O'Connell who was a guest on the first season of Unlocked. O'Connell returns to the series as a full-time host after filling in as a last minute replacement for Julie Chen-Moonves back in 2024, during season 26's Week 8 Live Eviction after she was quarantining from a COVID infection. Previously, in the previous series of Unlocked, O'Connell was a guest panelist for the second episode recapping the third and fourth weeks of the season.

This season will feature a live studio audience for the first time, in an attempt to bring the fans directly into the action. Parodying the 1,000th episode, Unlocked celebrated its 10th episode this season on September 4.

The show airs bi-weekly on Fridays on CBS and Paramount+ at 8 PM EST.

The program features Taylor, Derrick and Jerry leading recurring segments including: "Something Surprising" along with a brief interview with the most recently evicted houseguest from the Thursday night before. There would also be "never-before-seen" footage from the live feeds that never made it into the final episodes until now, the segment being titled "Missing Moments". The first episode featured special preseason interviews for the all of the houseguests in their homes, including some just moments before they entered the house in the green room and backstage. The three hosts also made their predictions in a "Time Capsule" and are set to open it up during the season finale.

Every episode, a former alumni will come back to host their own unique pre-recorded segment where they discuss the houseguests, along with a dedicated "Intern Andrew" segment.

Each Unlocked episode ends with one last segment, a "Sneak Peak" at the upcoming Head of Household competition that would be shown on the following Sunday episode.
