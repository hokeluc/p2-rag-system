Brown Sugar Babes is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Brown Sugar Babes was created towards the end of Week 1, the alliance included Cirie Fields, Felicia Cannon, Jared Fields, and Mecole Hayes (four of the five Black houseguests, minus then-target Kirsten Elwin).

Felicia Cannon was nominated alongside every other member of the alliance at one point during the season.
