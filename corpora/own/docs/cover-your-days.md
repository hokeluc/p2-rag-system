# Cover Your Days

This competition has multiple formats: In one format, the goal is to earn points, while in others, the goal is to survive enough rounds to eliminate all other competitors, or solve a set of clues pertaining to days the fastest.

If a competitor buzzes in with the incorrect number of days, the counter resets to 0.

A common system used in this competition is the three strikes system. A player receives one strike when they are the last to buzz in with the correct day number(s). If a player receives three strikes, they are eliminated. This continues until only one player remains and that player is crowned the winner.
