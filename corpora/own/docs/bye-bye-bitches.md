Bye Bye Bitches was an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

Bye Bye Bitches was formed in the first week of the season with Felicia having a theory of the 5 housemates sharing the scary themed bedroom (Blue Kim, Cameron Hardin, Jag Bains, Matt Klotz and Reilly Smedley). Felicia stated that she believes that group has built a strong group connection, especially after Reilly becoming that week's Head of Household. Felicia shared her theory to her roommates and wanted to solidify the five of them to create a new alliance. Felicia came up with the name Bye Bye Bitches after looking at a sign on the wall with the verbiage.

Not knowing that the five in the scary themed room, is an actual alliance that had been formed earlier called the The Handful, that alliance pulled Jared Fields to join. Jared then realized his mother Cirie was not apart of that alliance, and shared the news to his mom, confirming that alliance and Felicia's theory to the Bye Bye Bitches.

The Bye Bye Bitches would soon become strategic power players. In Week 3, Felicia won HOH and backdoored affiliate Hisam Goueli for his previously 'dictator-like' HOH reign, which ended in his unanimous eviction.

The next two weeks would see the alliance to continue to manage to keep large targets off their backs going into the final 12 and nearly single-handedly control the flow of information.

Bowie Jane would defect from the alliance after being left uninformed about Red Utley's 8-2 eviction. Immediately after, in Week 6, Cameron would win his second HOH, and made a big move in targeting the alliance, initially nominating Izzy and Felicia. Jared would win the Power of Veto, and fearing that if he used it his mother would likely be the replacement nominee, he chose not to use it. After a vote flip initiated by America Lopez would see Izzy evicted by an 8-1 vote, the alliance was left on unstable ground.

After a Double Eviction on Day 51 and the BB Zombie twist coming into play, Jared would be eliminated in 11th place, and with Cory Wurtenberger leading Cirie's outer allies to drift away from her, she, as the alliance leader, was likely doomed. Mecole, although a mere satellite member of the alliance, would be unanimously be evicted the following week, leading to the duo known as The Bookends as the only ones remaining.

After Cirie and Felicia were the only two of the alliance left going into the jury phase, their poor ability in winning increasingly physical competitions would lead them to be seen as pawns by the now-dominant Mafia alliance. After Day 86's Double Eviction, the two were the only non-Mafia members remaining, and Cirie would be evicted by a 2-0 vote on Day 93, followed by Felicia's eviction by Jag's sole vote on Day 96, marking the Bye Bye Bitches' defeat.

"Bye bye, bitches!" is a quote from former houseguest Janelle Pierzina during Big Brother 6 (US) as she won her first ever Head of Household. It's also restated on one of the signs in the comic bedroom where most of its members reside.
