# Big Brother 12 (US)

The twelfth season of Big Brother was produced by Endemol USA and Fly on the Wall Entertainment, with Allison Grodner and Rich Meehan returning as executive producers. CBS officially announced the season on September 10, 2009, just five days before the finale of Big Brother 11.

Executive producer Allison Grodner expressed enthusiasm for the upcoming season, stating:"We're excited for the summer. [...] We're excited for a brand new cast of terrific competitors. And we do have that. There are personalities and backgrounds of people, types of people that we have not seen before in the Big Brother house, which is hard because we've been doing this now for 11, 12 seasons."

Casting for the season began during the final week of Big Brother 11, with hopeful contestants submitting videotaped auditions due by April 23, 2010. Open casting calls started on March 27, 2010, in Los Angeles, California, and continued across several U.S. cities including Chicago, Washington, D.C., Las Vegas, and Columbus, Ohio. Contestants selected as finalists were flown to Los Angeles for the final round of casting, where forty finalists were chosen.

The Saboteur: One houseguest; Annie Whittington was to cause chaos, havoc, and paranoia in the house. She would be given $50,000 if she was to survive until Week 5, but ended up being evicted first. However; following a Pandora's Box opening on Week 5, America voted on which Houseguest to offer the role of the Saboteur to for two weeks. Ragan Fox was given this offer and accepted. He succeeded in his tasks and was rewarded with $20,000.
Pandora's Box: Pandora's Box returned this season, which; when opened either unleashed a reward or a punishment for either house or the HOH that opened it.
Diamond Power of Veto: Matt Hoffman was given the Diamond Power of Veto after opening Pandora's Box. It allowed the holder to take a nominee off the block and name its replacement.

Hayden Moss reluctantly won the HOH competition on the first day in the Big Brother House. After seeing Brendon Villegas and Rachel Reilly start an obvious showmance, he decided to use their newfound relationship as a scapegoat for his nominations. Claiming he wanted to break up the obvious couple, he nominated them. Hayden formed "The Brigade" alliance with Enzo Palumbo, Lane Elenburg, and Matt Hoffman. Brendon won the POV competition and removed himself from the block. Because Annie Whittington had failed to make any friendly gestures toward Hayden, and because she spent a lot of time with Brendon and Rachel, Hayden put her on the block in Brendon's place. She became emotionally upset and ranted around the house, which ultimately led to her unanimous eviction. Annie was the Saboteur, but the Houseguests did not presume this.

Rachel Reilly was crowned the new HOH at the end of a numbers-estimating HOH challenge. Rather than try to get even with Hayden Moss for nominating her, she decided to target players in the house she personally did not like. She nominated Britney Haynes and Monet Stunson and explained that she only wanted strong competitors. Britney won the POV challenge. Matt Hoffman convinced Rachel to put himself on the block as a pawn, because he secretly knew his "Brigade" alliance would protect him. At the POV ceremony, Britney removed herself and Rachel filled the nomination with Matt. Matt tried to play it cool like he was mad about being on the block, but Rachel got upset and ratted out his volunteered spot. At the eviction ceremony, Monet was eliminated. Only Kathy Hillis and Britney did not vote for her.

Matt Hoffman was crowned HOH after an endurance competition. Not wanting to make any waves in the house, Matt decided to nominate two "floaters" who seemed to schmooze with everyone else. He nominated Kathy Hillis and Andrew Gordon. He also banked on them not winning the POV. At the Veto competition, Brendon prevailed and did not change the nominations. Andrew became upset and lashed out at Kristen Bitting in the bathroom and bedroom. His unstable mood continued to swing. On eviction night he called out Kristen and Hayden Moss's secret romance that he witnessed at night. Kristen became instantly furious, but Julie Chen hushed her. Andrew was voted out unanimously.

At the HOH competition, the Houseguests could target players by pitting them in a memory-recall challenge. Kristen Bitting had it out for Rachel Reilly, but in the end Rachel won. Rachel then shouted "Floaters you better grab a lifevest! Kristen...", which upset Kristen and they began shouting at each other. At the nomination ceremony, Rachel nominated Hayden Moss and Kristen Bitting. She claimed that it was because Kristen was a floater and was latching onto Hayden's successes. At the Veto competition, Britney Haynes won, and considered using it, but declined after Rachel told her if she used it, Britney's closest friend Lane Elenburg would be the replacement. Hayden suffered a day in solitary confinement and Kristen had to wear the hippie leotard for an entire week. "The Brigade" and other houseguests stood behind Hayden, with the exception of Kathy, and voted Kristen out.

At another endurance HOH competition, Matt Hoffman was victorious yet again. Citing that he missed his opportunity to break up the power couple in Week 3, Matt nominated Brendon Villegas and Rachel Reilly. Big Brother gave the Houseguests the opportunity to prepare for the Veto competition and Rachel and Brendon stayed up all night preparing. At the competition the next day, their training did not pay off and they were the first two eliminated. Britney Haynes won the POV competition. Inside Rachel became agitated and started arguments with multiple people including Kathy Hillis and Ragan Fox. Britney did not change the nominations because she wanted both Rachel and Brendon out of the house. In the HOH room, Matt opened Pandora's Box and received the Diamond Power of Veto. Pandora's Box also unleashed a new Saboteur on the house, as Ragan was offered the role of Saboteur for the next two weeks. At the elimination ceremony, Rachel was evicted unanimously for her competence at HOH challenges.

Ironically enough, Brendon Villegas won the HOH competition - which was a physical rope unknotting course. Everything he did all week "was for Rachel", as he claimed multiple times. He nominated Ragan Fox, who had gotten into multiple arguments with Rachel the previous week, and Lane Elenburg as a pawn. Ragan won the POV. Before the Veto ceremony, Brendon opened Pandora's Box when he was offered a 24-hour vacation with Rachel. However, he had 24 hours alone at a resort while Rachel Reilly was let back into the house. She got into arguments with Ragan and other houseguests, and it was a generally tense 24-hours. After Rachel left and Brendon returned, Ragan removed himself from the block and Brendon nominated Matt Hoffman in his place. "The Brigade" decided to keep Lane and evict Matt, but Matt used his Diamond Power of Veto at the last minute and chose Kathy Hillis as his replacement nomination, and insulted Brendon. Kathy was evicted unanimously.

At the HOH competition, the last female in the house, Britney Haynes was victorious. Matt Hoffman wanted Enzo to be nominated because he made it clear that he wanted Matt out of the house. "The Brigade" continued to splinter when Hayden Moss and Enzo tried to convince Britney to nominate Matt as a pawn. Although she promised Brendon Villegas that she would not nominate him if he spared her the previous week, she nominated him and Enzo Palumbo. At the Veto competition, Houseguests could earn points by taking punishments of spend points on rewards. Lane and Hayden made off with prizes while Brendon and Enzo received multiple punishments. Brendon eventually won the competition and later used the POV to take himself off the block. Before the Veto challenge even took place, the Brigade tried to convince Britney to backdoor Matt. After the POV contest, Matt began to rally the troops to evict Ragan Fox. Seeing this as disloyal to his best friend, Britney realized Matt had no character and placed him on the block next to Enzo. Although Matt tried to mend relationships with Ragan, he missed an opportunity to reveal the Brigade alliance (even though Ragan figured it out himself) and Matt was voted out unanimously.

Just before Matt's eviction, Julie shocked the houseguests by informing them that they were going to experience a double-eviction night. Immediately following Matt's departure, the houseguests went to the back yard to perform an HOH competition. Hayden Moss won the mostly physical and slightly memory recall challenge. He had to immediately nominate two houseguests, and he chose Ragan Fox and Brendon Villegas. Minutes later, the houseguests all competed in a memory POV competition, and Ragan was victorious. He removed himself from the block mere moments after the competition, and Hayden replaced him with Britney Haynes. The houseguests were allowed a couple of minutes to strategize during commercial, but in the end Brendon was finally evicted. The final 5 then celebrated the fact that they had made it so far in the game.

The HOH competition was not held until the next day due to the double eviction episode. At the competition, Lane Elenburg won, despite the fact that he was trying to throw it so Enzo Palumbo could win and do the dirty work. His goal was to evict Ragan Fox but he needed a pawn. He asked Hayden, Enzo, and Britney who wanted to be the pawn. All three of them strongly argued against the position. In the end, Lane nominated Ragan and Enzo Palumbo. At the POV competition, it came down to Ragan and Enzo in a competition that fused memory recall, creativity, and athleticism, but it came down to a foot race and shoving match. Enzo won and took himself off the block. Lane put Hayden Moss on the block in his place, afraid that Hayden and Enzo would decide to take out Britney if she were the pawn. Although Ragan tried to rally Britney and Lane to keep him in the house for strategic reasons, Britney ultimately voted to evict Ragan, and he left the house.

At the HOH competition, Hayden Moss took an early lead and won a nimble-fingered competition. The houseguests started facing terrible boredom and a Reward challenge spiced things up. Britney Haynes won $10,000. She and Lane Elenburg were then nominated by Hayden. Hayden won the Power of Veto and chose to keep his nominations the same, leaving Enzo Palumbo with the deciding vote of evicting Britney from the House. Following her eviction, the three remaining members of the Brigade competed in a jungle-themed HOH competition, the first of three competitions that would determine the Final and most powerful Head of Household.

The Brigade celebrates the fact that they successfully made the final 3. Now, all bets are off as the next HOH gets to decide who sits next to him on finale night. The final HOH competition is played out in three challenges, to give the remaining houseguests a fair shot at securing a place in the final two. During the first endurance HOH competition, Hayden Moss won an endurance challenge. Days later, Lane Elenburg beat Enzo Palumbo in a memory recognition challenge, meaning Enzo is nominated. At the live HOH part 3 competition, Hayden narrowly beat Lane in a personality guessing quiz, claiming the final HOH. He then chose to evict Enzo.

Rachel Reilly won $5,000 at the Week 4 Veto competition.
Brendon Villegas, Hayden Moss, and Enzo Palumbo won a screening of The Other Guys in a reward competition in Week 4. Rachel Reilly automatically won as HOH.
Matt Hoffman won the Diamond Power of Veto when he opened Pandora's Box as HOH in Week 5.
Ragan Fox was secretly awarded $20,000 for successfully completing a two-week reign as the Saboteur. None of the houseguests knew about his prize.
Lane Elenburg won a phone call from his family in the Week 7 POV competition. It was a secret, but he openly admitted it.
Hayden Moss won $5,000 and a trip to Hawaii in the Week 7 POV competition. The house knew someone other than Ragan and Brendon won it, but Hayden has yet to reveal his winnings.
Lane Elenburg won $91.17 by opening Pandora's Box as HOH in Week 8.
Britney Haynes won $10,000 in a hide-and-seek Reward competition.
Britney Haynes won $25,000 as America's Favorite Player.

Hayden Moss received 24 hours of solitary confinement in the Week 4 Veto competition.
Kristen Bitting had to wear the "hippietard" for an entire week due to her placement in the Week 4 Veto competition.
Brendon Villegas received four punishments during the Week 7 Veto competition. He had to shave his head bald, he chose to be a have-not for three consecutive weeks, he had to be handcuffed to another houseguest for 24 hours (which he chose Britney Haynes), and during those same 24 hours he had to take a chum bath every hour for 24 hours.
Enzo Palumbo chose to wear the penguin suit for an entire week in the Week 7 Veto competition.
Enzo Palumbo decided to donate all the clothes he had in the house to charity, except for the clothes on his back, in the Week 7 Veto competition.

Paola Aviles would have been the fourteenth houseguest, but chose to quit during the pre-show sequester period.
The house almost always voted unanimously the entire season. Kathy voted differently twice, while Britney did it once.
This season has the most Pandora's Box twists with four.
This is the third season to have an all male final three, the other two before it were Big Brother 1 and Big Brother 10.
This season is tied with Big Brother 16 for the most overall finalists, with four: Hayden Moss and Lane Elenburg made the final 2 on this season, Rachel Reilly made it on Big Brother 13, and Enzo Palumbo made it on Big Brother 22.
