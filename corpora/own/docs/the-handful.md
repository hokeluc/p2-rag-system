The Handful is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US). The alliance includes Blue Kim, Cameron Hardin, Jag Bains, Matt Klotz and Reilly Smedley. The five person alliance is a sub alliance of the Family Style, that was formed after the creation of the The Handful.

After becoming the first Head of Household, Reilly celebrated her win with her 4 other roommates (Blue Kim, Cameron Hardin, Jag Bains, Matt Klotz and Reilly Smedley) of the "scary-themed" room in the HOH room. Agreeing to all their own strengths to build a strong group, they would then form the Handful. Agreeing that if they wanted to run the house long term, they would need to pull in more alliance members. They later revealed the group to America Lopez, Cory Wurtenberger, and Jared Fields, and they agreed to form a larger alliance called Family Style.

However, Jared would then realize that his mother (Cirie Fields) was not a member of this alliance, and shared the news to his mom. This news would confirm the alliance to Cirie's newly formed alliance, the Bye Bye Bitches. The Bye Bye Bitches would then form The Professors by adding Hisam Goueli and Red Utley, as an alliance made of the older houseguests. Cirie would then tell Hisam about The Handful alliance, subsequently making him confront Reilly about him being a potential target in the future. After she confirmed him being a future target, Hisam would target her after becoming the second HOH and him and Felicia Cannon agreeing that it would crumble the Handful/Family Style onion alliance. America, Cory, and Jared from Family Style and later Cameron from the Handful would defect, and Reilly would be evicted in a 12-0 vote, thus ending the Handful.

Blue is the only one to not win a Head of Household competition.
