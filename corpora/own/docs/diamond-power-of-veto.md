# Diamond Power of Veto

The

It was reintroduced in Big Brother 12 (US), as a second Veto gifted from Pandora's Box, allowing for the holder to save one of the nominees on live eviction night and name the replacement nominee themselves. Essentially a more powerful variation of the Double Power of Veto that would be introduced two seasons later, and a weaker version of the Coup d'Etat that was first activated the season before. The HOH, traditional POV Holder, and the person saved by that POV, are ineligible to be nominated by the Diamond Veto.

While not an official diamond veto, the veto holder in Week 1 of Big Brother Canada 3 had the power to choose the replacement nominee due to the fact that all of the Houseguests were declared the Head of Household that week. Sindy Nguyen won the veto and removed herself from the block, putting Pilar Nemer on the block in her place.

Weaker variants of the power came into existence in later seasons. Big Brother Canada 12 saw the introduction of the Executive Power of Veto, which let the saved nominee choose the replacement instead of the Veto Holder. Big Brother 26 (US) had America's Power of Veto, where America chose the replacement nominee after a public vote was held following the airing of the Wednesday night Veto episode.

The Diamond Power of Veto was introduced as the final Veto of Big Brother 4 (US) and was essentially identical to the Golden Power of Veto. It was used by Alison Irwin to save herself from the threat of eviction. Erika Landin was the only possible replacement as Jun Song was already up on Nomination Block and Robert Roman was the HOH. Alison then cast her sole vote to evict Erika.

It was then reintroduced with greater power in Big Brother 12 (US) - if the winner chooses to use it, they will name the replacement nominee rather than the Head of Household. It was awarded to Matt Hoffman as a gift from Pandora's Box in Week 5. A week later, when he was up for eviction due to being nominated by Brendon Villegas, he used it on himself and nominated Kathy Hillis as the replacement. Kathy was then evicted in a 5-0 vote against Lane Elenburg.

The Veto returned in Big Brother 28 (US) as one of the powers to be granted from the BB Time Capsule. Rick Devens entered the Time Capsule in Week 3 and chose the space that held the Diamond Veto. He was able to use it for the following six veto ceremonies. During Week 6, when Yash Patel nominated his closest ally and fellow Survivor player Dee Valladares, he used it to save Dee, nominating Yash's ally Kamu Kirk in her place. Kamu was then evicted in a 8-1 vote against Haley Thogmartin.

It was a choice for America's Care Package during Week 3 of Big Brother: Over The Top, along with the Double Power of Veto and the Boomerang Power of Veto, but was not selected by Scott Dennis.

It later appeared as an option for fans to choose in Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US) as part of the Pick-A-Veto twist on Day 20 alongside the Spotlight Power of Veto and the VIP Power of Veto. However, it was not selected by the audience.

After two unsuccessful attempts at revival, it once again appeared in Big Brother 21 (US). The prize for winning the Panic Whacktivity Competition in Week 3 was the ability to transform the Golden Power of Veto into the Diamond Power of Veto, anonymously granting it to whoever won that week's Power of Veto Competition, to be used at one of the following four Veto Meetings. It was won by Christie Murphy, but after nearly using it in Week 4, she chose not to. In Week 6, the final week she could have activated it, HOH Jessica Milagros also won the Power of Veto, rendering the power moot, so Christie allowed it to expire.

In Big Brother Canada 12, the Executive Power of Veto was introduced. Functionally, it was nearly identical to the Diamond Veto, with the one exception being that whoever was saved by the Veto would choose the replacement nominee, instead of the Veto Holder themselves, assuming they're not the same person. HOH Elijah Kazlauskas kicked off a Chain of Rejection, which ended in Victoria Woghiren being granted the Executive Veto. She chose to use it on Todd Clements, who then chose to nominate Vivek Sabbarwal as his replacement nominee. Vivek was then immediately evicted in an 8-0 vote against Tola Eam.

Big Brother 26 (US) brought another variant of the Diamond Veto, with America's Power of Veto being earned by Makensy Manbeck in Week 1 as a result of her winning the Upgrade Competition, part of the season's premiere night twist. This variant is also functionally identical to the Diamond Veto, with the exception being that America votes to choose the replacement nominee, instead of any individual in the house. It had to be activated at one of the first four Veto Ceremonies, following the decision made by the traditional Veto Holder. After word of this power spread throughout the entire house, Makensy was re-nominated at the Week 3 Veto Ceremony by Cedric Hodges in an attempt to flush out the power, after Tucker Des Lauriers used the POV on Angela Murray instead of himself. The plan worked, as she immediately used America's Veto and took herself off the block. After the episode aired on Wednesday night, a public vote for the replacement nominee was opened, due to close on Thursday morning. During the live eviction episode, America's replacement nominee was revealed to be Quinn Martin, who immediately competed in the AI Arena against the other nominees Kenney Kelley and Tucker. Tucker won in the Arena, forcing Quinn to face the eviction vote, which he then survived against Kenney in a 10-1 vote.

Quinn Martin is the first victim of a Diamond Veto variant to survive eviction.
