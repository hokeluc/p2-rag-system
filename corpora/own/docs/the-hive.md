The Hive was an alliance on Big Brother 20 that consisted of Angie Lantry (aka Rockstar), Bayleigh Dayton, Faysal Shafaat, Haleigh Broucher, and Scottie Salton, which was an expansion to both the FOUTTE and The Sacred Six alliances.

Bayleigh is the only member of this alliance to win a Power App from the BB App Store while the twist was in play.
They were all evicted consecutively: Bayleigh, Rockstar, Faysal, Scottie then Haleigh.
Rockstar is the only member of this alliance to not win any major competition. Every other member has won either an HOH or POV competition during their time on the show.
Faysal is the only member to target and successfully evict one of their own, as in week 8 as he nominated Scottie Salton intentionally and Scottie got evicted.
Rockstar is the only member of the alliance to be a victim of the Hacker Competition as she was nominated by Kaycee Clark in week 7 when Kaycee won the Hacker Competition.
Haleigh is the only member of The Hive to vote for Tyler Crispen in the final 2
