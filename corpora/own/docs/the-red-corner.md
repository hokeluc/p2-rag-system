The Red Corner was an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

The Red Corner alliance was established in the early days of the game by Chuk Anyanwu, Haley Thogmartin, and Kamu Kirk. Following Dee Valladares' win as HOH, the alliance aimed to maintain influence with both Dee and Rick Devens. Although Dee refrained from nominating any of the four for eviction, she quickly grew frustrated with Haley’s presence in the house, suggesting the alliance was less stable than it initially appeared.
