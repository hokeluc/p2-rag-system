The Pink Swear Alliance was an alliance on Big Brother 5 (US).

The Pinky Swear Alliance was formed as an alliance to go against The Four Horsemen. The members of the alliance all did a pinky swear with one another, to show their allegiance to the alliance.

During the first few weeks in the house. The opposing alliance The Four Horsemen stayed in power and took out Mike Lubinski, Lori Valenti and Holly King. During this time Jase Wirey and Scott Long were being obnoxious and going on a power trip, starting arguments with many other houseguests.

The alliance came to power once Diane Henry won HOH in Week 4. She nominated the two leaders of The Four Horsemen in Jase Wirey and Scott Long. Despite Jase winning the veto. The alliance was still able to evict Scott. Following Diane’s HOH, the alliance proved victorious again as Nakomis Dedmon won HOH. This week the Twin Twist was also revealed to the houseguests which let Natalie Carroll enter the house. The alliance decided this was the week to put the Six Finger Plan in motion. Later Nakomis nominated Diane and Marvin Latimer for eviction with the intent of putting Jase up after the veto was used. Later after Drew Daniel used the veto on Diane. The alliance successfully nominated Jase and evicted him.

The alliance proved victorious again as Adria Klein won HOH. At this time there were only 3 houseguests not in the alliance. To the surprise of the rest of her alliance. Adria nominated Marvin and fellow alliance member Will Wikle for eviction. Later Adria won the veto and didn’t use it. Despite having the numbers to keep Will. Natalie flipped her vote and voted to evict him. Then Adria cast the tie-breaking vote to evict on him. This angered the rest of the alliance as once Nakomis won the following HOH. She targeted the twins and nominated them together. Despite Adria winning the veto, Natalie was still evicted by a vote of 4-1. During this time aswell, Diane started to grow closer to Drew and actively defected from her alliance in favor of working with him. Following Natalie’s eviction, Marvin won HOH and Adria was evicted. Following this Marvin was evicted.

Following Marvin’s eviction. Nakomis won her third HOH. She decided to nominate the two members outside of the alliance in Drew and Michael Ellis. However Diane saw an opportunity to force Nakomis to nominate Karen Ganci. Through the help of Drew and Michael, Diane won the veto. She decided to veto Drew which forced Nakomis to nominate Karen. Later after Drew and Diane both voted to evict Karen, she was evicted.

Following Karen’s eviction, Drew and Michael won the HOH and veto for the week and Michael voted to evict Nakomis. Following this Drew became the final HOH and he evicted his Showmance Diane.

Every member of the alliance was on the jury.
