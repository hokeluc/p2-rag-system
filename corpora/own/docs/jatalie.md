Jatalie was an alliance on Big Brother 18 (US).

Despite being on opposite sides of the house at the beginning of the game. James Huling and Natalie Negrotti formed a showmance and alliance.

James and Natalie proved to be a strong force playing both sides of the house for weeks. They were able to take out big house targets and helped lead the revolution against Paulie Calafiore. They continued to play both sides of the house for weeks.
