The Sovereign Six, often referred to as S6 or Sea-Sick, was an alliance on Big Brother 6 (US) and Big Brother 7 (US).

The Sovereign Six was created in opposition to The Friendship alliance. The alliance was formed by Kaysar Ridha and later joined by Janelle Pierzina, Howie Gordon, Rachel Plencner, James Rhine, and Sarah Hrejsa beginning in Week 3 when they successfully backdoored Eric Littmann the leader of The Friendship.

The power continued to shift week by week, but ultimately the downfall of the Sovereign Six came when Maggie Ausburn convinced Howie Gordon to turn on James Rhine, who at that point, had become distrustful to the alliance for attempting to play both sides to save himself. Howie decided to nominate James and Sarah Hrejsa. This is known as one of the worst moves in the history of Big Brother. This forced the Sovereign Six into a minority position for the remainder of the game and gave The Friendship numbers in the jury. This caused James and Sarah to defect from the alliance, and Sarah was evicted. Even with Kaysar Ridha returning to the game, the alliance still remained a minority, especially after Kasayar gave up the HOH competition to The Friendship member Jennifer Vasquez, who would betray him by getting him evicted that week.

The power continued to change week by week, and Janelle Pierzina was able to be the last remaining Sovereign Six member left in the house, making it to the final 3. Janelle planned to bring Ivette Corredero to the finals over Maggie Ausburn. Janelle’s plan could’ve resulted in her winning the game. Janelle would get the jury votes of Howie, Rachel Plencner, April Lewis (despite being a member of the opposing alliance revealed post-season she would’ve voted Janelle over Ivette in a final 2) and likely James. On Finale Night, however, Janelle failed to win the final HOH and was evicted after Ivette (who won the Final HOH) cast the sole vote to evict her on her, placing 3rd and becoming the 7th and final member of the jury.

The Sovereign Six was revived in Big Brother 7 (US) when members Janelle, Howie, Kaysar, and James all returned to the Big Brother House. This time, the alliance was mainly known as the Season 6 Alliance. The four members of the Sovereign alliance enjoyed a power run for the first 4 weeks as Janelle, James, and Kaysar managed to win the first 4 HOH competitions, but their power soon faded after Danielle Reyes won during the fifth week and backdoored Kaysar, although her initial target was Janelle. Of the revived members of the Sovereign Alliance, Howie was the only member to not finish the season in the same place that he finished the previous season.

The Sovereign alliance again began to fracture during Week 4. Janelle had acquired her second HOH win when James had asked Janelle to nominate a member of the Chilltown alliance, Janelle refused. Prior to Janelle's HOH win James had gone against his wishes nominated houseguest Jase Wirey as a replacement nominee during his HOH week. The decision was what the majority of the Sovereign alliance wanted. James agreed to do so only if the next time his alliance won the HOH they were to nominate either Mike Malin, or Will Kirby. Janelle as the preceding HOH nominated Mike but when Mike had won the POV James wanted Janelle to nominate Will to ensure Will's departure. Janelle however, declined and instead nominated fellow houseguest Diane for eviction. Feeling betrayed, James then secretly betrayed the alliance and started a new alliance with houseguests Danielle, Mike, and Will entitled The Legion of Doom, with the goal of evicting Janelle. Kaysar, Howie and James were evicted throughout the next 3 of 4 weeks. Janelle went on a comp run throughout the game and made it to the final 3 once again. On Finale Night, Mike won the final HOH and cast the sole vote to evict her on her, placing 3rd once again.

Both times this alliance appeared:
Janelle reached the final three and was the highest placing member of the alliance.
Kaysar was evicted right before the jury started forming.
James placed 7th.
This is the first alliance in Big Brother history to win the first 8 Power of Veto competitions (In Big Brother 6)
