Derstian is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consisted of Christian Birkenberger and Derek Frazier. However, the alliance failed to make a significant impact on the game, as the two of them failed to trust each other and didn't mind throwing each other under the bus when needed.

In week one, after Derek's closest ally at the time, Frenchie, began to trust Christian and even made a Final 2 deal with him, Derek created an alliance with him too. However, despite being in an alliance together, the two failed to actually trust each other, with Derek even telling Frenchie in week two that if he won HoH, he'd put Christian up. During Christian's HoH reign in week 4, he made a deal with Derek and the rest of his team, the Jokers, that he would not put any of them up due to their alliance. In week 5, Derek Xiao put Christian on the block and Derek chose not to stay true to his alliance with Christian, voting with the majority of the house to send him home.
