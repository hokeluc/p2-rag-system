The Brains were one of the four cliques apart of the clique twist introduced on Big Brother 11 (US). The three members were Chima Simone, Michele Noonan, and Ronnie Talbott.

Ronnie is the only member of the clique to have a normal HoH reign.
Michele's reign was cut short due to Chima's expulsion.
Jeff used the Coup d'Etat during Chima's reign, pulling both of her nominations off of the block.
This is the only clique to have members in different statuses.
Michele was a juror.
Ronnie was a pre-juror.
Chima was expelled.
This is the first clique to have all of its members out of the game.
This is the only clique to have none of its members play twice or more.
