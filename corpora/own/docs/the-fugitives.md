The Fugitives is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

The Fugitives were created on Day 53, two days after Cameron Hardin re-entered the house as a BB Zombie. The name was inspired by The Brigade, with each member adopting nicknames in the style of the brigade; Gunner/The Gunslinger/The Getaway Man, The Outlaw, and The Bounty Hunter.

In Week 10, Bowie Jane Ball unexpectedly became the new Head of Household. This unexpected development would set the stage for a strategic move by Matt Klotz and Jag Bains as they would conspire to execute a strategic backdoor plan targeting Cameron.

Bowie Jane would soon nominate Cirie Fields and Felicia Cannon for eviction, with Cirie squarely in her crosshairs as the primary target. However, things would take a twist when Jag shared to Bowie Jane that Cameron had a lack of trust in her.

Adding to the drama, Jag's victory in the Power of Veto competition would empower him to influence the proceedings. In light of this newfound information and the POV win, Bowie Jane would make Cameron the replacement nominee. This fallout would permanently end The Fugitives and make Matt and Jag the dominant duo as The Minutemen, and later would chose to have Bowie Jane as their third member, forming the dominant Mafia alliance.
