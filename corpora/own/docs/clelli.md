Clelli was a showmance and alliance on Big Brother 17 (US).

Quickly upon entering the house, Clay Honeycutt and Shelli Poole instantly got close. Their close relationships turned into an alliance.

Main article: The Sixth Sense

Clay and Shelli went on to join The Sixth Sense alliance. This combined with Shelli’s competition prowess led them to be a dominating force in the house. Taking out members of the opposing alliance The Goblins week after week.

During Week 5, Clay, Shelli and Vanessa Rousso made an alliance with the other side of the house to take out Austin Matelson. However after the BOTB competition went wrong. Vanessa was forced to be the one to nominate him. After Clay and Shelli realized they needed Austin as a number, they convinced Vanessa to nominate Jason Roy instead. This angered the other side of the house who blamed Vanessa along with Clay and Shelli for the betrayal. After opposing alliance member James Huling won HOH, he targeted Clay and Shelli after Vanessa convinced him not to target her and most of the house wanted one of them out as they were a Showmance. This led James to nominate Clay and Shelli next to each other. After both failed to win the POV, Clay asked to be evicted to save Shelli. Ultimately, Clay was evicted unanimously and Shelli was evicted the week after.
