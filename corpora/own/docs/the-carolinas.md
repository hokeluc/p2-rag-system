The Carolinas is an alliance on Big Brother 22 (US).

Tyler and Christmas, along with their closest allies and winners of their respective seasons, Kaycee Clark and Josh Martinez, were rumored to have planned a pre-game alliance. However after Kaycee and Josh both tested positive for COVID and were removed from the cast, Tyler and Christmas were left to work as a duo.

The alliance name refers to Christmas and Tyler being from North Carolina and South Carolina respectively.
