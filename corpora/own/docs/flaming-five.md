Flaming Five was an alliance on Big Brother 21 (US).

The flaming five was created by Nick Maccarone as a sub-alliance inside the dominant Gr8ful and Unde9able alliances. Despite it being real to Nick, Isabella Wang, and Sam Smith, Christie Murphy and Tommy Bracco viewed it as a fake alliance and held no real loyalty to the alliance.

Isabella Wang is the only member of the alliance to not win a competition.
