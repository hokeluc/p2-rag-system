Flower Power is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The Flower Power alliance was officially created in Week 9 between Ava, Kelley, and Lauren during the latter's Head of Household reign over their shared friendship and disillusionment with most of the other houseguests. Unbeknownst to the trio, they soon became the primary opposition to The Judges alliance following the elimination of Rachel Reilly in the White Locust twist.

Lauren's HOH week ended in a less than ideal manner as her target Keanu Soto won the BB Block Buster, and Will Williams, a well-liked player by the alliance, was narrowly evicted after Ashley Hollis flipped the vote off of herself. Vince Panaro of The Judges became the next HOH, and nominated Ava and Kelley with the former as his target due to him worrying about her overuse as a pawn.

However, Morgan Pope, one of Vince's closest allies and Lauren's rival, strong-armed Vince into nominating Lauren by using the Power of Veto on Ava in order to become his sole ride-or-die ally. Despite Lauren winning the Block Buster, The Judges settled on evicting Kelley in order to break up Flower Power after the votes tied with her and Keanu. Morgan won the HOH during the Double Eviction and successfully evicted Lauren in a 2-1 vote following Keanu's POV win. Thus, Ava became the sole remaining member of Flower Power and the alliance went extinct after only nine days in existence.
