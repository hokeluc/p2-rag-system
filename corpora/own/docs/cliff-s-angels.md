Cliff's Angels was an alliance on Big Brother 21 (US).

As the the four house guests in the alliance were not part of Gr8ful,they began to develop bonds. However after Jessica won HOH in Week 6, the alliance was offically created.

Every member of this alliance won the Power of Veto exactly once.
Every member of the alliance either did not use the Veto, or used it to save another alliance member.
Cliff Hogg III is the only member of the alliance to vote for Jackson Michie to win.
