The Fatal Spies was an alliance on Big Brother 18 (US) that consisted of Natalie Negrotti, Michelle Meyer, Bronte D'Acquisto, and Bridgette Dunning. The name was made as a joke of a combination between "The Fatal Five" and "The Spy Girls."
