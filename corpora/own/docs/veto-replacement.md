# Veto Replacement

Veto Replacement is a twist in Big Brother Canada 2 and Big Brother 19 (US) where one houseguest who is not picked in the Power of Veto competition can replace one of the selected competitors with themselves.

The twist was first introduced in Big Brother Canada 2 during Week 3. Canada was given the opportunity to vote for one houseguest, and the chosen individual was granted the ability to replace a drawn veto player.

The twist then appeared again in Big Brother 19 (US) as part of the Den of Temptation in Week 2. The winning houseguest received the "Ring of Replacement," allowing them to remove an existing player selected to play in the veto competition and compete themselves. Its use in the American version was tied directly to fan voting just like in Canada.
