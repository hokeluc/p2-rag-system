The Big Brother: 25th Anniversary Celebration is a special looking back at the first twenty-four seasons of Big Brother (US) a week before the premiere of Big Brother 25 (US) on July 26, 2023.

While initially available on Paramount+ as Episode 0 of Season 25, and later having its own tab under the Big Brother show page, it eventually was removed early on into the season and has not been restored since.

After rumors of an anniversary special, it was reported by Twitter user Reality BBQ that a twenty-five season special was planned to air on July 26, 2023 to commentate the franchise. It was later officially revealed in a press release on July 13, 2023 that the special, produced by Entertainment Tonight, was to premiere on July 26, 2023, exactly one week before the premiere of season 25.
