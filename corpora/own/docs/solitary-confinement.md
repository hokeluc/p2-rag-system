# Solitary Confinement

Solitary Confinement is a recurring punishment in Big Brother. It results from a Power of Veto competition. A HouseGuest who receives this punishment is required to spend 24 hours inside a designated room. A camp toilet and slop (and in some cases bread) are provided. It is usually won in the Yankee Swap competition.

Joseph Rodriguez spent 24 hours inside the "Mattrix", which was the Have-Not Room. AI Deepfakes of Matt Hardeman were placed inside to be constantly talking.
