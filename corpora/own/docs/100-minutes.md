# 100 Minutes

100 Minutes
, known specifically as

100 Minutes of ____
in

Big Brother Canada
, is a recurring

Power of Veto
and

Head of Household
competition.

Rules[]

Each houseguest is in their individual coffin. At different intervals, a distraction will take place trying to offset the counting of the houseguests. The houseguest that presses their buzzer/leave the coffin the closest to the time was given mark (without going over in the US version), will win the competition.

History[]

The Competition originally appeared as a Power of Veto during the third week of Big Brother 10 (US). Keesha Smith ended up winning the competition.

The Competition appeared as a Power of Veto during the second week of Big Brother 12 (US). Britney Haynes ended up winning the competition.

Canada[]

The Competition originally appeared as a Head of Household during the ninth week of Big Brother Canada 3*.* Sarah Hanlon ended up winning the competition.

The Competition then appeared as a Power of Veto during the seventh week of Big Brother Canada 5*.* William Laprise Desbiens ended up winning the competition.

The Competition appeared as a Power of Veto during the eighth week of Big Brother Canada 10*.* Betty Yirsaw ended up winning the competition.

Celebrity (Quebec)[]

The Competition originally appeared as a Head of Household during the sixth week of Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 1*.* Jean-Thomas Jobin ended up winning the competition.

The Competition appeared as a Head of Household during the tenth week of Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 2*.* Claudia Bouvette ended up winning the competition.

The Competition appeared as a Head of Household during the third week of Celebrity Big Brother Quebec 5*.* Sinem Kara ended up winning the competition.
