# Unitard

The Unitard is a recurring item in American version of Big Brother. It is attire that is usually a form of punishment that a housemate can receive as a result of a Power of Veto competition (and in some cases a Head of Household competition). The houseguest that earns it will usually have it wear it for the remaining duration of the week or for seven days. The notable Red Unitard was used in seasons 8 through 10, and a different version has appeared in every subsequent season. It is typically earned in the Yankee Swap competition.

The unitard made its first appearance in season 8, where it was won by Jen Johnson as a result of the Cutthroat Christmas Power of Veto competition in Week 2.

The unitard was earned by Sheila Kennedy in the Week 4 Power of Veto competition, Color of Veto.

The unitard was earned by Michelle Costa in the Week 4 Power of Veto competition, Big Brother Slapshot.

This was the first variation of the unitard. It featured oversized goggles, a black cape, and gold shorts with "Captain Unitard" written across the front of the top. It was earned by Lydia Tavera in the Head of Household competition, HOH Invitational Golf Tournament, in Week 6.

This was the second variation of the unitard, which was tie-dyed and dubbed the "Hippietard". It was earned by Kristen Bitting in the Week 4 Power of Veto competition, The Wizards of Pinball.

This was the third variation of the unitard, which was green, adorned with a pink tutu and a dunce cap, and had "I'm with stupid" written on the front. It was called the "Humilitard" and was earned by Jordan Lloyd in the Week 6 Power of Veto competition, Big Brother Cornhole.

The fourth variation was won by Frank Eudy in the Week 4 Power of Veto competition, Field of Veto. This version was purple with "BBU" written on the front and was adorned with a blue and white skirt. It was called the "Spiritard".

Candice Stewart was given a clown unitard, or "Clownitard", to wear as a punishment after the Week 6 Power of Veto competition, Frog Darts.

Nicole Franzel was given a "Germitard" after winning it in the Week 4 Power of Veto competition, BB Cup. The Germitard was a German folk costume and came with a large sausage attached to a pitchfork and a beer stein.

Jackie Ibarra was given a "Knight in Shining Armortard" after winning it in the Week 6 Power of Veto competition, Game of Throws. The Knight in Shining Armortard was a silver unitard with a helmet, knight armor, and a shield. It also came with Vanessa Rousso becoming her "squire" and being shackled together with her for the next 24 hours, cleaning Jackie's shield, armor, and helmet. After the 24 hours were up, Vanessa was allowed to remove the squire costume, but Jackie had to continue wearing the armor for the rest of the week.

Nicole Franzel was given a Super Safety Costume after winning it in America's Care Package in Week 8, along with immunity for the week. The SSC is made up of an orange unitard with a yellow cape and a traffic cone hat, along with reflective tape that can light up.
Corey Brooks was given an American flag-themed unitard dubbed the "Patriotard" in the Week 8 Power of Veto competition, Zingbot for President. It came with a ballot box glove, a bald eagle puppet, and an American flag-themed top hat.

Jason Dent was given an "X-tremitard" to wear after winning it in the Week 7 Power of Veto competition, BB Adventure Tours. It was a blue, pink, and yellow unitard with a yellow helmet and a giant pink "X" on the top; the words on the chest spell out "X-treme".

Kaycee Clark was given a Pinwheel Unitard to wear after coming in last place during the season's opening competition, The Trash Folder. It was rainbow-colored and came with wings attached to the arms, blue shorts, and a pinwheel over the torso. To add to the punishment, whenever the pinwheel, dubbed the "Pinwheel of Doom", began to spin, Kaycee was not allowed to leave whatever room she was in until it stopped.
Later, after winning the Week 6 Power of Veto competition, Boom Power Trip, she was given a "Health Nut-itard", a tan unitard that says "Space Pecs" on both arms, along with an additional peanut costume to wear over it. This included two giant dumbbells and a yellow headband that also reads "Space Pecs". This marked Kaycee as the first person to wear two unitards in one season.

Tommy Bracco was given a BB Explorer Costume to wear after winning it in the Week 6 Power of Veto competition, Tossed in Space. It is a gray unitard designed to resemble a rocket ship. It reads "BB Explorer" on both arms and has black dots around the neck representing bolts, along with a blue circle with a silver outline and additional "bolts" representing a window. It also has two red parts protruding from the torso and one in the back to represent the rocket's legs. It comes with flame-styled shorts and a silver skirt around the waist, containing more "bolts" around the top and bottom. It includes a large, red, cone-shaped hat representing the rocket's nose. As part of the punishment, two giant cans are occasionally strapped to the forearms; these can either function as flashlights or contain stink bombs that release a strong, unpleasant odor, depending on the task Tommy must complete.

Janelle Pierzina was given the All-Star Mascot costume after being chosen as a +1 by Kaysar Ridha in Week 1. The outfit includes a yellow pointy hat with small LED lights covering the sides. The main piece is a large yellow star with two large eyes and a mouth, also covered with LED lights that shine yellow. Under the star outfit, Janelle also wore a gold-colored unitard, which was a short dress with short sleeves that she could wear while sleeping or doing activities.
Nicole Franzel was given her third unitard in her Big Brother career, the "Slopitard", after winning it in the Mathcathalon Power of Veto competition in Week 5.

Claire Rehfuss was given a Carditard to wear after winning it in the Kingdom of Curl-A-Lot Power of Veto competition in Week 5.

Indy Santos was given a Punkitard to wear after winning it in the "Punkaroo" Power of Veto competition in Week 6.

Matt Klotz was given the Duhamel-a-manic unitard to wear after winning it in the Buddy Ball Power of Veto competition in Week 6.
Cirie Fields had to wear a Superhero Academy Uniform after losing the "Time to Take Flight" Power of Veto competition in Week 11.

Tucker Des Lauriers was given the BBAI Assistant unitard to wear after winning it in the AI Apocalypse Power of Veto competition in Week 6.

Keanu Soto was given the Butt-ler Did It unitard to wear after winning it in the Elusive Target Power of Veto competition in Week 5. Vince Panaro was given the Hard-Boiled Detective unitard to wear in Week 5.

Since Angela Murray did not win her competition in the BB Time Capsule, she was given the Hard-Boiled Detective unitard to wear for one week starting on Day 11.

The original Red Unitard was another eligible punishment from the Time Capsule, but it was never landed on.

Since the tenth season, the Veto competition in which this punishment is earned has alternated between Weeks 4 and 6, until Season 18, where it appeared in Week 8 in correlation with Zingbot's annual appearance. In Season 19, it appeared in Week 7, and in Season 27, it appeared in Week 5.
In the case of Season 11, it is likely that the HOH competition featuring the unitard was originally meant to be played as a Veto competition.
Frank Eudy was the first man to don the unitard.
Lydia Tavera, Kristen Bitting, Candice Stewart, Indy Santos, and Tucker Des Lauriers have all been evicted while wearing the unitard.
Kristen Bitting and Tucker Des Lauriers are the only people to have worn the unitard and not made the jury.
Nicole Franzel is the first HouseGuest to have worn the unitard twice, having worn it in Season 16 and Season 18. After wearing it again in Season 22, she became the first person to wear it three times.
Kaycee Clark is the first HouseGuest to have worn the unitard twice in one season, that being Season 20.
Season 18 is the first season to have had two contestants wearing unitards at the same time, namely Nicole and Corey. Coincidentally, the two were in a showmance during the season.
If including Frank, Big Brother 18 is the first season to have had three different contestants wear unitards at any point in their Big Brother careers.
Nicole Franzel is the first person to have worn the unitard and made it to the final two in the same season. She went on to win the game and was followed by Kaycee Clark.
Both Nicole and Kaycee won the game after wearing the unitard twice overall.
