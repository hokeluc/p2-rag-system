# Niagara Balls

The first iteration during Big Brother 2 (US) was a food competition, with everyone competing together. The houseguests attempted to catch a series of 100 balls containing grocery items for the week. Some of these contained luxury items requested by the houseguests. Balls that hit the ground were out of play, and if a player attempted to pick up an out-of-play ball, they would be disqualified.

In Big Brother 4 (US) and Big Brother 8 (US), the rules were as follows: the houseguests start on a platform down below, where five thousand balls are dropped onto them, ranging in a multitude of colors. The first player to place the required number of green balls into their container wins.

From its reintroduction in Big Brother 26 (US) onward, each houseguest must collect their balls in individual chambers, with a fan beneath the grate they stand on blowing the balls through the chamber. The color and amount of balls, alongside the number of tubes needed to be filled, additionally changes each season. For instance, the Big Brother 26 (US) version required filling one tube by catching twenty red balls, the Big Brother 27 (US) version required twenty orange, green, and blue balls to be deposited in a specific color pattern, and the Big Brother 28 (US) version required ten red and ten yellow balls to be placed into separate left and right tubes in order to win.
