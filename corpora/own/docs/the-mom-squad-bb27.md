The Mom Squad 2.0 is an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The "Mom Squad" alliance, featuring Amy Bingham and Rachel Reilly, originated in Week 1. When Amy was nominated that week, Rachel strategically rallied numerous houseguests to secure the votes needed to keep Amy in the game, after nominee Zae Frederich got into an argument with Rachel. She was assisted by Morgan Pope, who found Zae to be too calculated and intelligent from the start, which led to his eviction over Amy in a 9-5 vote.

After Amy was named as a replacement nominee by Week 2 HOH Jimmy Heagerty against many of his allies' wishes, the house continued to view the duo of Amy and Rachel as being far too powerful, and many considered voting out Amy in the event she lost the BB Block Buster. Amy minimally campaigned for herself and began speaking poorly of Rachel to others. Rachel and the duo's affiliate Ashley Hollis decided that it was too early to go against the grain, leading to Amy's eviction by a 13-0 vote against Will Williams.

Coincidentally, Rachel's sister, Elissa Slater, was also in an alliance titled 'The Mom Squad' during Big Brother 15 (US).
In S27 E3, Rachel acknowledges that this alliance is The Mom Squad 2.0.
Amy Bingham is the only member of this alliance to be nominated and recieve votes to evict.
