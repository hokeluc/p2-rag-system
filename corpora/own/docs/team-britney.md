Team Britney was one of the four teams apart of the coach twist introduced on Big Brother 14 (US). The four members were Britney Haynes, JoJo Spatafora, Shane Meaney, and Willie Hantz.

This is the second team to feature someone getting expelled, following The Brains.
Britney Haynes is the only team coach to not be the highest or lowest placing member of her team.
