Terek was a short-lived alliance on Big Brother 23 (US) only during week one of the season. The alliance consisted of of Derek Xiao and Travis Long. Travis was the first member of the alliance to be evicted.

Derek X won the first Power of Veto (POV) of the season. Derek X used the POV, to take off Kyland, who was originally nominated for Eviction by the first Head of Household (HOH), Frenchie. Derek X stated that he took Kyland off the block, as he didn't believe Kyland deserved to be nominated and to gain some favors with the HOH Frenchie. In result, Frenchie nominated Travis as a replacement for Kyland, due to Travis' strong potential in the game and lack of other potential houseguests to nominate. Travis was the first member of the alliance and houseguest of the season to be evicted in the first eviction, by a vote of 11-2. Derek X voted to evict Travis, dissolving the two member alliance.
