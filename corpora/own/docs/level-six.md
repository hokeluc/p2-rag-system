Level Six was the dominant alliance on Big Brother 20 (US).

While the alliance initially found themselves in a 7-6 deficit, they managed to get in the majority after Tyler Crispen flipped Kaitlyn Herman to their side at the end of Week 1. After getting into the majority, they quickly took control of the game and held on until the end, even during the weeks where at least one member was nominated. Despite only winning 8 of the 15 HoH's, they were in control for all 15 HoH reigns.

Four of their members, along with affiliates JC Mounduix and Sam Bledsoe, made it to the jury phase. By the final 6, the alliance successfully eliminated everyone from the other side of the house. The final two featured two members from the alliance, who had also formed their own sub-alliance before joining Level 6. Due to their dominance and loyalty, this alliance can be considered one of the greatest and most loyal alliances in Big Brother history.

Level Six formed in Week 1, during Tyler Crispen's reign as HoH. With half of the house immune from eviction, Winston Hines began to form a core alliance, bringing in Angela Rummans, Brett Robinson, and Rachel Swindler, becoming the first four members. Rachel and Winston then pulled in Kaycee Clark and Tyler, respectively. To keep minimal blood on his hands, the alliance agreed to nominate Sam Bledsoe and Steve Arienta for eviction, as they were the first eliminated from the HoH competition, with a plan to nominate Bayleigh Dayton as a replacement if needed. The alliance wanted to keep Sam safe to work with her in subsequent weeks and had JC Mounduix voting with them. With JC and Sam on their side, they would hold the majority if Steve was evicted.

Going into the first eviction, the alliance was at a 7-6 deficit. However, Kaitlyn Herman, a member of the rival FOUTTE alliance, grew frustrated with her allies Faysal Shafaat and Haleigh Broucher’s closeness. Tyler used this opportunity to flip Kaitlyn, resulting in Steve's eviction and Level Six gaining power.

After Steve's eviction, Kaitlyn became HoH. Tyler convinced her to backdoor FOUTTE leader Swaggy C. She nominated Winston and Scottie Salton (FOUTTE member). After Tyler won the PoV, he removed Scottie, and Kaitlyn replaced him with Swaggy. Swaggy was evicted 8-4.

Week 3 brought trouble as Scottie won HoH, marking the first week Level Six was not in power. Scottie nominated Brett and Winston, considering them a strong duo. After winning PoV, Scottie left nominations untouched. Despite two Level Six members on the block, they had enough votes to evict Winston, seen as a loose cannon. Winston was evicted 6-5.

In Week 4, Sam Bledsoe became HoH and nominated Kaitlyn and Haleigh. When Faysal won PoV, he removed Haleigh, and Sam replaced her with Angie Lantry (FOUTTE). Kaitlyn was evicted 9-1. However, Sam’s bonus life was automatically used, giving Kaitlyn a chance to return. Kaitlyn failed the Do or Die challenge, leading to her permanent eviction.

Week 5 was tough as Bayleigh Dayton won HoH and nominated Brett and Rachel, initially targeting Brett with Scottie as a backdoor. Rachel’s paranoia made Level Six question her loyalty. Tyler won PoV but left nominations the same after Bayleigh told him Angela would be the replacement nominee. Level Six voted to keep Brett. Rachel was evicted 5-4.

Week 6, Angela won HoH and successfully backdoored Bayleigh. In Week 7, Haleigh became HoH and nominated Angela and Kaycee, targeting Angela. Kaycee won the Hacker competition, removed herself from the block, and put up Rockstar as a replacement. Tyler won PoV and removed Angela; Haleigh put Kaycee back up. Rockstar was evicted 5-1.

Despite Faysal winning HoH in Week 8, JC manipulated him to turn on Scottie. Nominations were Scottie and Brett. Brett won PoV, removed himself, and Faysal nominated Kaycee. Scottie was evicted unanimously, 6-0.

Angela won HoH in Week 9 and nominated Faysal and Haleigh. Kaycee won PoV and kept nominations the same. Faysal was evicted 4-1. Following his eviction, the four jurors competed in a Battle Back Competition which Scottie won, earning reentry.

After Scottie's return, Tyler won HoH and nominated Scottie and Haleigh. Kaycee won PoV and left nominations intact. Scottie was evicted again unanimously, 5-0.

Kaycee then won her first HoH, nominating Haleigh and Sam. She also won PoV and kept nominations the same. Haleigh was evicted 4-0.

In Week 11, Brett and JC discussed targeting Angela and Kaycee. Brett tried to convince Tyler to do the same, but Tyler, who had a final 3 with Angela and Kaycee, informed them. The trio then decided to target Brett after Haleigh's eviction. Julie announced this would be a Double Eviction week.

After Haleigh’s eviction, the final six competed for HoH; Tyler won and nominated JC and Sam to backdoor Brett. Brett was persuaded to throw the PoV. Angela won PoV, removed JC, and Tyler nominated Brett in his place. Brett was evicted unanimously 3-0.

Angela won HoH next and nominated Sam and JC. Kaycee won PoV and left nominations. Sam was evicted unanimously 2-0.

Going into the final 4 HoH competition, JC won HoH, securing a final 3 spot and spoiling the others’ plan. JC nominated Angela and Tyler. Kaycee won PoV and cast the sole vote to evict Angela.

Tyler won part one of the final HoH, while Kaycee won parts two and three, securing final HoH. She cast the sole vote to evict JC. After the jury Q&A, the jury voted Kaycee as the winner of Big Brother 20 (US) by a 5-4 vote.

Tyler Crispen is the only member of this alliance to win a Power App from the BB App Store while the twist was in play.
Winston Hines is the only male member evicted before jury.
Rachel Swindler is the only female member evicted before jury.
Brett Robinson holds the most nominations at four.
Angela Rummans and Kaycee Clark were the last Level Six members to be nominated, both receiving their first nominations in Week 7.
