# Feel The Burn

The houseguests must perform a set of reps in either a certain time limit or faster than their other houseguests.

The Competition originally appeared as a Coaches Competition during the third week of Big Brother 14 (US). Janelle Pierzina ended up winning the competition

The competition then appeared as a Power of Veto competitor during the fifth week of Big Brother 23 (US). Britini D'Angelo won the competition.

The Competition appeared as a Power of Veto competitor during the seventh week of Big Brother 28 (US). LaTrice Verrett won the competition.
