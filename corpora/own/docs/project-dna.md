# Project DNA

Across different versions, the twist has been implemented in several variations, including:

Secret Siblings where estranged relatives who are unaware of each other's presence in the game
Twin Twist where identical twins swapping places under one identity before both entering as separate houseguests
Parent-Child Twist where a parent and child play the game together
Double Twin versions where multiple sets of twins swapping between separate living spaces, and other family-based adaptations such as parent-child pairs, spouses, or entire families competing either together or as individuals.

Secret Siblings: Michael Ellis and Nakomis Dedmon were estranged siblings who were unaware of each other before the airing of the show and revealed it to the rest of the House Guests during week 2.
Twin Twist: Adria Klein and Natalie Carroll were identical twins and would swap places every few days, playing as Adria. If the two made it to week 5 without being discovered or evicted, they would both enter the game as individuals.
Drew Daniel and Diane Henry also revealed that they both had twins, but their twins were not competing in the game.

Parent-Child Twist: Daniele Donato entered the house and was met with her estranged father, Dick Donato, competing against each other in conjunction with the Rivals twist.

This twist has also appeared on Big Brother 17 (US), following similar rules as it did in Big Brother 5 (US), however it only included the twin twist, not the secret siblings.

Twin Twist: The twist involved twins Liz and Julia Nolan who were both playing as Liz. Despite the houseguests finding out, they both made it to week 5, so they both joined the game as individual players.
Shelli Poole and Jeff Weldon revealed they each had a twin outside of the game.

Opposite of Big Brother 17, this season did not include the twin twist but instead featured the secret siblings twist. However, the siblings were not estranged and instead knew of the other's presence in the game.

Secret Siblings: A modified version of the "Secret Siblings" twist was implemented in this season. Alex and Morgan Willett entered the house knowing that they would be competing against one another, rather than being estranged.

Parent-Child Twist: Jared Fields entered the house and was later met by his mother, Cirie Fields, as a surprise 17th houseguest, with the rest of the house unaware that they were playing with each other.

In a variation of the twin twist, Big Brother Brazil 17 had two sets of twins enter on Day 1; a day before the rest of the cast entered. Unbeknownst to the cast; these twins faced an eviction on Day 7, where one twin of each pair would be evicted.

Twin Twist: This modified version of the twin twist was implemented this season. The twist involved brothers Antônio and Manoel, and sisters Emilly and Mayla.

In a modified version of the Project DNA twist, an entire family will move into the house on Day 1. The entire family consists of father Ayrton, mother Eva, daughter Ana Clara and cousin Jorge Lima. However, fans will vote for two family members to stay, evicting the other two family members. Father and daughter, Ayrton & Ana Clara Lima, would compete every other week, sharing only one vote.

On Day 1, Nick & Phil Paquette entered the house. Neither of them knew the other would be competing. Arisa Cox revealed that they would be playing as one person alternating who competes in comps each week but both would remain in the house whether it was their week to play in comps or not.

Twin sisters, María Camila & Valentina Abello, entered the game, competing as a single contestant. For the first week of the game, the two of them alternated shifts in the house, secretly switching places every 24 hours.
Mother and daughter duo, Yuyuniz Navas and Antonia Casanova, entered the house competing as separate housemates.

On Day 1, Xristos Varouxis entered the house. Later on Day 2, Panagiotis Varouxis entered the house and they both had to make the housemates believe that they were one person.

Armaan Malik entered the game with his two wives, Kritika and Payal. All three of them competed as separate housemates.

Twin Brothers: Two siblings, Boris and Leon Schneiderovsky entered the game on day 1. They were aware of each others' existences before they both entered the game.

Sisters: Two sisters, Daniella Pick and Sharona Pick entered the game on day 1. They were aware of each others' existences before they both entered the game.

Twins: Two identical twin sisters, Rinat and Shiran Gitter, both entered the game on day 1. They were aware of each others' existences before they entered the game.

Twin sisters, Diana and Ilana Taranenko entered the house competing as separate housemates.

Siblings Snir and Sapir Borgil entered the house as separate housemates.

Twin brothers Barak and Noam Madar, and a mother and daughter duo, Orit and Liron Tangi entered the house, all competing as separate housemates.

For the ninth season of Big Brother Naija, all housemates entered with their loved ones.

Double Twin Twist: As part of the season's theme called Double Up, two sets of twins mainly Toffi & Kenny Santos and JM & JP Lagumbay were introduced. Both pair would stay in two separate houses. Every once in a while, the twins have to swap places with their twin in between two houses without their secret being discovered by any of the housemates in two houses. If they failed on their secret task, they would receive a Forced Eviction as a punishment.

Secret Siblings: Just like in Big Brother 5 (US), two estranged siblings who were unaware of each other prior to the show entered the house. Apey Obera and Mark Obera were estranged siblings. They met and reunited through a secret task by Lou Yanong and Abi Kassem. When Mark became a housemate, the two merged into one at first. However, being 2-in-1/Pair Housemate, the relationship of the two did not go well to the point where Big Brother had no choice but to separate the two of them from being 2-in-1 housemates, with the help of the other housemates' and audiences' decision. The two was eventually separated and competed once again as individual housemates.

Secret Siblings: Raf Juane and Nathan Juane entered the house as individual housemates. During the first week of the season's Adult Edition, Big Brother gave them a secret task to hide their real identity as siblings to the rest of the housemates as part of the week's weekly task. Succeeding in keeping their secret for the weekly task would give the housemates a luxury shopping budget, and a special immunity for either Nathan or Raf.

Michael Ellis is the first male to be a participant of this twist.
He would later be followed by Dick Donato, Boris & Leon Schneiderovsky and Antônio & Manoel Rafaski.
Jason Roy is the only houseguest to be in two separate seasons in which this twist was featured.
Morgan Willett, Emilly Araújo, Nick & Phil Paquette and Dick Donato are the only houseguests to participate in this twist that went on to win the game.
Dick and Daniele Donato are the only houseguests involved in this twist to make Final 2 together.
Michael Ellis, is the first houseguest to participate in this twist and then make it to the Final 2, later followed by Dick and Daniele, Liz Nolan, Morgan and Emilly.
However, Leon Schneiderovsky made it to the final of his season, ending up placing 5th out of the five finalists.
The Israeli franchise of the show has the most occurrences of the twist, with six.
Big Brother 15 (US) and Big Brother 18 (US) featured twists where siblings of former houseguests would compete on a brand new season without their sibling. Those players involved were Elissa Slater (sister of Rachel Reilly), Paulie Calafiore (brother of Cody Calafiore) and Tiffany Rousso (sister of Vanessa Rousso).
Big Brother 14 (US) and Big Brother 25 (US) saw the siblings of players from other reality shows compete. Willie Hantz and Cory Wurtenberger are both the brothers of former Survivor players, with Willie also being the uncle of a different former player. Additionally, Jared is the son of Cirie, a former Survivor player who was later revealed to be playing alongside him.
