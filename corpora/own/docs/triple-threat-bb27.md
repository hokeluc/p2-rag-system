Triple Threat was an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The "Triple Threat" alliance, the very first alliance of the summer, came together in Week 1 with Jimmy Heagerty, Mickey Lee, and Morgan Pope.

Despite getting along well initially, the Triple Threat alliance faced some tension early on. Following Keanu Soto winning the Week 2 Power of Veto (courtesy of his secret power which enabled Kaycee Clark to play in the veto for him and subsequently win), Jimmy vacillated between renominations and ultimately decided on the previous week's nominee Amy Bingham as a safe option, a decision which frustrated fellow Triple Threat alliance members Mickey and Morgan, both of whom criticized it for being a seemingly weak move that they also had no say in.

Further threatening the security of the alliance was Kelley Jorgensen's secret veto power, which allowed her to compete in a timed veto competition following the veto ceremony to pull herself of the block. Unfortunately for Jimmy, Kelley won the competition, forcing him to name an unprecedented fifth replacement nominee. He settled on Will Williams, who was even more so aligned with Morgan and Mickey at the time. Because of this, both Morgan and Mickey began to question Jimmy's loyalty to their alliance during his HOH reign, deciding that he would be too big of a threat to bring deep into the game. Amy would ultimately go home by the end of Jimmy's HOH week, a move which didn't sit well with either of the two women.

The trio remained largely in-tact during Lauren Domingue's subsequent HOH reign. Morgan and Mickey's defection from the Triple Threat alliance would arrive sooner than anticipated. Rylie Jeffries initially won the Week 4 HOH, but Mickey quickly utilized her secret power to dethrone Rylie's HOH. She successfully avoided suspicion as the perpetrator and was crowned as the Week 4 HOH. Mickey nominated: Keanu, Kelley and Rylie with Keanu as her initial target. This sentiment would shift in the coming days. Mickey, was feeling remorseful for stealing Rylie's HOH, she mended their relationship by declaring to Rylie that he was only a pawn to take out Keanu in the veto competition. However, Keanu ended up winning his third consecutive veto, and Mickey had to pivot.

While, Rylie's crush and close ally Katherine Woodman was an ostensibly easy option for renomination. Mickey had other thoughts, fearing the repercussions of severing her ties with Rylie. Mickey waffled between her options at length, but quickly began seeing Triple Threat member Jimmy as a potential option. This idea was only further echoed by members of the house, many of whom were rubbed the wrong way by Jimmy's threat level and wished to see him go home. She was particularly swayed by Morgan, with whom she talked at length about the prospects of a Jimmy renomination and what the fallout would look like. Due to the combined effects of the bad taste Jimmy's HOH reign left in the duo's mouths, a pitch from Keanu blaming Jimmy for endangering Morgan and Mickey's game, and information being leaked of a storage room conversation that was spearheaded by Jimmy that involved Vince Panaro, Kelley, and Keanu. The duo began seriously considering defecting from the alliance and joining sides with players such as Vince and Zach Cornell. Despite a solid pitch from Jimmy's ally Rachel Reilly to nominate Vince for his shifty gameplay and surrounding worries of putting Jimmy on the block. Mickey was ultimately swayed to nominate Jimmy, with him becoming the main target for the week.

Following the veto ceremony, Morgan and Mickey reaffirmed their commitment to no longer work with Jimmy, declaring the Triple Threat alliance to be over while talking to the cameras together. Similarly, Jimmy, who was blindsided by the renomination and only found out about it moments before, also decided to cut ties with Mickey and Morgan. He was hoping to target their side of the house if he were to survive eviction and win the HOH in the subsequent week.

Unfortunately for Jimmy, he would later lose the BB Blockbuster to Rylie and was sent home by a vote of 9-2 against Kelley, only receiving Rachel and Ashley's vote to save.

This alliance is the second to be named Triple Threat, following the Triple Threat alliance of Big Brother 22.
The members of this alliance are the only three people to always vote in the majority during Big Brother 27.
