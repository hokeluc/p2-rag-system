Oasis was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

Formed by Pooch Pucciarelli that included many physically strong men in the house, this alliance quickly dismantled as he fatally volunteered to be a pawn against house target Taylor Hale and was unanimously evicted by the house himself instead.

All surviving members except Daniel Durston, along with the other outsiders of the house, formed the season's dominant alliance The Leftovers that took control of the game.

Oasis was created by Pooch Pucciarelli who wanted to align with the guys and create an all-guys alliance.

The alliance got leaked by Daniel and Kyle Capener, who told some of the girls about it. After Pooch volunteered to be nominated in order to ensure Taylor Hale be evicted, the girls in the house used this as an opportunity to take out a major threat and flipped the vote against Pooch. Despite campaigns from Monte Taylor, Kyle, Matt Turner and Joseph Abdin, Pooch was evicted by a unanimous vote of 12-0.

Due to the alliance unanimously voting out Pooch. The alliance lost trust with one another especially losing trust with Daniel. The alliance was partially revived when Kyle, Joseph, Monte and Turner joined the season's eventual dominant alliance, The Leftovers .

Oasis has the largest placement gap between the lowest placing member and the highest placing member of any alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).
