The Crossovers were an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

The Crossovers alliance was created in Week 1 during Dee Valladares' HOH reign, expanding on the initial trio of reality TV veterans: Dee, Rick Devens, and Big Brother 26 (US) returnee Angela Murray.

The Crossovers were the dominant alliance in the early phase of the game. The veteran players maintained connections to the Red Corner alliance, while Barrett and Drew held connections to Mallory and Melody. By positioning themselves favorably in Week 1, they were the primary catalyst for Ashley's eviction after she posed a risk of revealing the alliance's positioning in the house. Devens' win in Week 2 further solidified their strength, allowing them to target Rome and Jason—Rome for his perceived strength and influence, and Jason due to information Drew received indicating he was targeting the veteran players. After Rome's eviction, the Crossovers maintained power during Kamu's Week 3 HOH reign by influencing him to target Jason and forming the Toolshed alliance, a merger between the Crossovers and the Red Corner.

Throughout Week 3, Angela began to mistrust Drew because he was "all over the house" and less connected to the alliance than in earlier weeks. She shared these concerns with Devens and Dee, who became further convinced of Drew's disloyalty when Drew floated saving Mallory despite her explicit desire to target the veterans. Devens pulled Kamu away from Drew during Week 3 and turned the entire Red Corner against him. Even Barrett, who had formed a Final 2 alliance with Drew (The Jugglers), saw that the tide had turned and hesitated to push for Mallory's safety out of fear of being perceived as untrustworthy.

After Haley's HOH win in Week 4, she originally wanted to target Melody, Taylor, and La Trice. However, Devens, Dee, and Angela swayed her to nominate Drew after Haley opined that she could not nominate "three girls," had promised safety to Yash, and needed a male pawn on the block initially. Unbeknownst to Drew, Haley and the Red Corner were then convinced to target him due to his perceived untrustworthiness. Even Barrett, who shared a Final 2 agreement with Drew, refused to warn him, fearing he would become the replacement nominee.

During the week, Drew's blind loyalty to the Toolshed alliance worked in his favor, eventually convincing Dee that Drew should remain in the game as a shield and someone to target the Red Corner later on. Dee convinced Devens of this strategy, and Barrett and Angela were persuaded to save Drew if he lost the Blockbuster. That week, Drew won the Blockbuster, allowing the Crossovers to successfully blindside Kamu, Chuk, and Haley by evicting Lyric over Melody.

With the successful blindside, Drew remained convinced of the Crossover's strength and turned on the Red Corner. However, Dee and Devens continued to want to target him after the Red Corner are evicted. They told him that Kamu spearheaded a brief decision to make Drew the main target, which he believed. Angela and Dee also lied to the Red Corner, claiming they voted to evict Melody, leading to the Toolshed imploding.

After LaTrice Verrett won HOH in week 5, she initially considered both Drew and Barrett as pawns, but ultimately nominated only Barrett alongside Chuk and Haley, with Haley as the main target. Despite a member on the block, the Crossovers felt safe as long as Haley and Chuk did not win the veto.

Angela, feeling guilt, admitted to Haley that she evicted Lyric, whilst Dee continued to lie. With Mallory Aurichio winning the Time Capsule, Dee admitted her power to Devens. She also tells Kamu she evicted Lyric, before telling him about her power as well. Haley and Angela got into a large fight after Haley claimed that Angela told her Dee was the other flipped vote. However, when Kamu gains the veto and betrays the Red Corner by not using it, it seems like Haley's fate is sealed.

LaTrice holds a meeting in the HOH room that includes Drew and Barrett in order to rally the new players against the Icons. Taylor tells the Icons of this, painting a target on Drew's back, however Drew and Barrett agree to tell a partial story of the meeting to the Icons in order to exonerate themselves, Mallory, and Melody. Barrett tells Dee about the meeting, blaming LaTrice for all discussion, leading to the icons becoming suspicious of her and Taylor.

Throughout the week, Devens, Barrett, and Drew all discuss possibly flipping the vote to evict Chuk over Haley, however these discussions ultimately did not amount to any plan. However, a few hours before the Blockbuster, Dee chooses to try and flip the vote in the occasion that Barrett wins the blockbuster to have Chuk evicted. She and Devens successfully get the votes of the Crossovers, with the possibility of Mallory and Melody as well. When Haley wins the blockbuster, this is not necessary and Chuk is evicted next to Barrett.

Yash won the HOH knockout competition, during which Dee and Devens picked each other to be eliminated due to not wanting to be seen as a duo. Yash spent several hours with Drew and Kamu in the HOH room, where Kamu pushed him to nominate Dee as the main target, not believing her claims of wanting to work with him. Yash wanted to target either Angela or Haley, whilst also considering Barrett as a pawn with a Haley backdoor option. Drew attempted to push back on the Icons and Barrett being nominated, but ultimately conceded to Yash and Kamu's plans. Yash later told Devens about these nominations, but Drew played down his involvement and lied about knowing the nominations for certain.

Dee and Devens began to panic about possibly being nominated, trying multiple times to learn information and convince Yash to change his mind. Dee plans to use her bribe to convince him to nominate Drew, Mallory, and Melody instead. She ultimately offers him several different deals with the bribe, but Yash rejected them all. Devens then attempted to convince Yash to nominate himself instead, hoping to use the DPoV without making it apparent that Dee is his number one, but Yash again refused. Yash and Kamu also told Drew about Dee being the one to want him evicted in prior weeks, leading to him agreeing to her nomination. Ultimately, Yash nominated Dee, Angela, and Haley for eviction.

Dee and Devens planned for him to use the Diamond Power of Veto on her if she or Barrett did not win the veto. When Yash won the veto, Dee reckoned with the fact that she is likely the main target. Drew told the Crossovers about the conversation between Kamu and Yash, exposing Kamu's betrayal of the Icons and making him the main target of Devens. The Crossovers then merged with the F.A.P. alliance of Drew, Barrett, Mallory, and Melody to make the Mosh Pit.

Whilst both Drew and Barrett suspected that Devens had a power he would use to keep Dee safe, the pair only told Angela of their plan for the veto ceremony. At the ceremony, after Yash did not use his veto, Devens used the Diamond Power of Veto to save Dee and replace her with Kamu. Along with the Mallory and Melody, the Crossovers now had the majority to evict either Kamu or Haley. However, the situation caused not only Drew to privately declare his loyalty to F.A.P. over the Crossovers, but also for the likes of Yash and Taylor to finally realize the icons were all working together.

Melody and Mallory began worrying that the Crossovers existed and they were simply add-ons to a previously existing alliance, despite Drew's attempts to dissuade them, causing them to consider saving Kamu. Consequently, Drew encouraged Devens to spend more time with them, but this caused Devens to worry that the Mosh Pit was already breaking a part, and he told Dee and Barrett, causing further fear that Drew was also flipping to save Kamu. Barrett told Dee and Devens that Drew encouraged LaTrice to view Devens as a threat during her HoH, further driving a wedge in the Crossovers.

Melody and Mallory told Drew their plan to save Kamu, but he successfully convinced them to get rid of Kamu for now. However, the following day the lack of support from the rest of the Crossovers caused them to reconsider once more. Incensed, Devens planned to make a speech if Kamu was not evicted revealing both the Mosh Pit and the Crossovers. He first threatened Barrett with this, but Barrett encouraged him to do so, promising loyalty to the icons. Devens then told Drew of this a few hours before the live episodes, threatening to expose his entire game and get him evicted if Melody or Mallory do not vote with them.

Ultimately, the Crossovers were successful in convincing Mallory and Melody to evict Kamu. This came to fruition when Angela won the blockbuster and Kamu was evicted with only LaTrice choosing to evict Haley. Dee then won the next HoH, with Barrett throwing it to her, keeping the Crossovers in power. However, Kamu's allies blamed Drew and Barrett for convincing Mallory and Melody to switch, as well as Devens and Dee for Kamu being on the block anyway causing significant tension.

Dee, still not trusting Drew, planned to use her bribe to force him to never nominate or vote against either her of Devens for the entirety of the game, and if he refused she would nominate him and possibly Melody as well. However, production prevented her from doing so. She also considered turning on the Mosh Pit and using Mallory as a renomination due to Mallory's continued loyalty to Taylor and reluctance to trust the Mosh Pit.

Despite telling the others her plan was LaTrice, Taylor, and Yash, Dee planned to target Drew instead and destroy both the Mosh Pit and the Crossovers, however she felt as if she did not have a reason and so organized a Mosh Pit meeting in her HoH room. She attempted to get Drew to confess to wanting Dee evicted and blaming it on Kamu and Yash, but he did not. She then brought up Yash, who blamed Drew for his HoH nominations, prompting her to continue to want to target him. After ousting the others, Barrett also lied and claimed that Drew never warned him about being the replacement nomination.

The Crossovers lied to Drew and told him he was not going up. Drew won America's vote and entered the Time Capsule, winning the Never Not Pass. He told Dee, but she believed he was lying, and told him she did not care what America thought. She questioned why Drew did not tell Yash to put up Taylor and LaTrice, before revealing she is putting him up that week.
