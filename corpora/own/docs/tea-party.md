Tea Party was an alliance on Big Brother 27 (US).

The "Tea Party" alliance, consisting of Mickey Lee and Morgan Pope, formed in week 1. The duo was apart of a trio called, "Triple Threat" that formed the same week with, Jimmy Heagerty. Mickey and Morgan felt an instant connection as they would mostly share gameplay strategy and secret information from other houseguests to each other. As early as week 1, both women solidified strong relationships with several houseguests outside their duo most notability: Ashley Hollis, Vince Panaro, Will Williams, Zach Cornell, and "Triple Threat" member Jimmy.

Week 1, started off with a great success as they helped lead the charge to maintain a female majority in the house as there were nine women and eight men in the game. Morgan picked up the idea from Rachel Reilly to target the alpha males in the house to break-up and weaken any perceived all-male alliances. Vince was HOH and nominated Ashley, Amy Bingham, and Zae Frederich. When Ashley won the POV, discussions of potential replacement noms began to circulate. Fellow, Triple Threat member Jimmy was mentioned as a possible consideration. That did not sit well with Mickey and Morgan as they pitched an idea to nominate Keanu Soto as an alternative option for Vince to put on the block instead. However, Vince ultimately landed on Kelley Jorgensen to go up on the block in Ashley's place. With Zae being the only man on the block the target quickly switched to him. Mickey and Morgan took the opportunity to make sure they had the votes to evict Zae in the aftermath he was sitting on the block after the Block Buster competition. After Kelley won the Block Buster, Zae was evicted by a vote of 9-5.

Jimmy became the Week two HOH. This was good news for both Mickey and Morgan as he was a fellow alliance member in their trio. Jimmy nominated Keanu, Kelley, and Adrian Rocha for eviction. Tensions grew between the trio during this week particularly due to the aftermath of the veto ceremony. Keanu and Kelley both won special veto powers. They both removed themselves from the block and Jimmy had to name two replacement nominees. Jimmy landed on nominating Amy and Will in Keanu and Kelley's place. This did not sit well for Mickey and Morgan as they saw them as allies. When Adrian won the Block Buster it left Amy and Will on the block against each other. Amy was ultimately evicted by a vote of 13-0 and frictions between the trio grew. Mickey won an "HoH interrogation power" the same week. Knowing that Mickey could not trust Jimmy she quickly told Morgan about her secret power and the two vowed to hide this information from Jimmy.

Week 3, went as normal when Lauren Domingue became HOH. Although, Lauren was not a close ally and alliance member to Mickey and Morgan. They were able to keep each other safe during her HOH based on their good standing in the house and relationships with other players. Their biggest obstacle was advocating who to nominate as they wanted to protect many people in the house. Lauren nominated: Keanu, Kelley, and Will for eviction. Keanu and Kelley were already house targets however Will was a close ally to them. After Keanu won POV, they went to work to pitch ideas to Lauren for the replacement nominee. Lauren was considering renominating Ashley in Keanu's place. Both Mickey and Morgan pressured Lauren to nominate Adrian instead in hopes to keep Ashley safe as she was also an ally to them. With lots of campaigning, strategic swaying, and Ashley advocating for herself not to go up. Lauren ended up renominating Adrian in Keanu's place. This became a successful move for both Mickey and Morgan as they were able to get the votes to keep Will safe. After, Kelley won the Block Buster, Adrian was evicted by a vote of 8-12.

Week 4, took an interesting turn as Rylie Jeffries became HOH. Things became uncertain for Mickey and Morgan as they did not know how they stood with Rylie given him being a wildcard. Feeling nervous of what could happen. Mickey activated her HOH interrogation power in hopes of keeping them both safe for the week. In order for her power to work Mickey had to successfully avoid being detected as the interrogator by the HOH Rylie. If she is able able to avoid suspension she successfully dethrones Rylie as HOH and replaces him as HOH. However, if she is unsuccessful in avoiding suspicion and Rylie guesses correctly. Then her power becomes no good and Rylie remains HOH, putting an even bigger target on the duo. With the help of many of their allies. Mickey was able to avoid suspicion and became the HOH for the week. This became great news for the trio as the two of them were able to keep each other safe for the week. Mickey nominated Keanu, Kelley, and Rylie for eviction with Keanu as her main target. After Keanu won POV, Mickey had a difficult decision to make regarding the replacement nominee. Many people were advocating for Katherine Woodman to go up in Keanu's place. However, Mickey had different thoughts as she wanted to both form a new working relationship with Katherine and be on good terms with Rylie as the house wanted to keep him for the week. Many people pitched an idea for Mickey to nominate Vince. When this information got back to Morgan. She pitched an idea for them to turn on their Triple Threat alliance member Jimmy as they were already at odd with him. Wanting to stay loyal to Morgan and maintain a solid trust Mickey landed on putting Jimmy on the block in Keanu's place. Rylie won the Block Buster and Jimmy was evicted by a 9-2 vote, officially ending Triple Threat. After Jimmy's eviction, Mickey and Morgan formed, "The Melting Pot" alliance with Vince and Zach.

Ava, became the week 5 HOH and nominated two Melting Pot alliance members: Vince and Zach. Mickey was successful by advocating to keep her and Ashley safe. While Morgan did everything in her power to keep herself safe that week. When Katherine won POV there were talks about her using the veto to nominate Morgan. Zach had a "BB Buy Off power" that he could also use to remove himself from the block during the veto ceremony. This put Morgan in immense danger of going up on the block as the target. In the aftermath, Katherine kept the nominations same and Zach decided not to use his power assuming he had a good chance of surviving eviction. Keanu ended up winning the Block Buster and the house decided to vote out Zach over Vince and he was evicted by a vote of 8-2. Mickey and Morgan were able to survive another week.

Rachel became the week 6 HOH. This was bad news for the duo as Rachel found out that they only wanted to keep her in the game until jury. Rachel nominated the remaining Melting Pot members: Mickey, Morgan, and Vince. When Lauren ended up winning POV. Mickey and Morgan made several attempts to sway Lauren to use the veto on them. However, Lauren had other intentions and used the veto on Vince instead. Upset with Lauren's decision, Rachel nominated Rylie in Vince's place. This set up a very crucial Block Buster competition for Mickey and Morgan as one of them had to win in order for both of them to survive the week. When they were both campaigning for votes. Morgan felt uncertain about Mickey as she was discussing game with Katherine using a bag of M&M's. Something she felt betrayed by as it was their way of strategizing prior to that moment. Morgan opted to form a separate alliance called, "The Judges" in order to secure the votes in the event she was sitting on the block after the Block Buster competition. Mickey ended up winning the Block Buster and Morgan was able to survive the vote by 5-4 with Rylie being evicted.

During week 7, Vince became the week 7 HOH. In the hopes of staying loyal to The Judges alliance, Vince nominated Ava, Kelley, and Mickey. This took Mickey by surprise as she was in The Melting Pot alliance with Morgan and Vince. She also did not realize she was considered an outsider and a house target. Mickey ended up winning the POV and Vince nominated Katherine in her place alongside Ava and Kelley. Morgan advocated for Kelley stay and Mickey advocated for Katherine to stay in the event either one of them was sitting on the next to each other after the Block Buster. Kelley went on to win her third Block Buster competition and Katherine was evicted by a vote of 7-8. Keanu became the week 8 HOH, he nominated The Melting Pot trio, Mickey, Morgan, and Vince. Morgan won POV and Ashley was nominated in her place. Vince won BB Block Buster and Mickey was evicted by a 6-1 vote.
