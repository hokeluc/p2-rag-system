# Food Competition

The

Food Competition
was a weekly competition in which HouseGuests competed to determine their food privileges for the week. This competition was held from

Big Brother 2 (US)
through

Big Brother 10 (US)
. From

Big Brother 2 (US)
to

Big Brother 6 (US)
, losing HouseGuests were restricted to a diet of peanut butter and jelly. Starting with

Big Brother 7 (US)
,

slop
replaced peanut butter and jelly as the punishment food. The Food Competition was discontinued after

Big Brother 10 (US)
and was replaced by the

Have/Have-Not Competition
in

Big Brother 11 (US)
. The

Head of Household
always retained automatic food privileges.

Typically, the Head of Household would host the Food Competition. HouseGuests competed either as individuals, in teams, or collectively worked towards completing a task. In some versions of the competition, the objective was to win as many types of food as possible.

Trivia[]

The Food Competition was derived from the Weekly Task, an essential Task in which housemates needed to succeed in order to secure the house's budget for weekly supplies. If they failed, the housemates would be forced to ration staple foods for the entire week.
