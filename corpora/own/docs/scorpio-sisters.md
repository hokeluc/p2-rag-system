Scorpio Sisters was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

On Night 1 of Big Brother 24 (US), Alyssa Snider and Paloma Aguilar formed an alliance called the “Scorpio Sisters”.

Despite both being put on backstage by Pooch Pucciarelli that could potentially jeopardize their survival in the house, Paloma’s social and manipulation skills led her to gain early control of the house. They both quickly formed the Girls Girls alliance. This and with Paloma’s strong relationships to many other houseguests led to early success. After Paloma told Taylor Hale that the former was targeting Monte Taylor, Taylor told Monte this information. Upon hearing this, Paloma used her influence upon the house to successfully get Taylor nominated instead as a replacement nominee by Head of Household Daniel Durston.

Alyssa and Paloma were in full control of the house during the first week. However, due to the mental stress of playing the game in the house, Paloma started spiraling and ultimately decided to walk from the game, leaving Alyssa on her own. Thus, this officially ended the alliance.
