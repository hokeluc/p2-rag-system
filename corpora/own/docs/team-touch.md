Team Touché was an alliance on Big Brother 14 (US).

After coaches Dan Gheesling and Britney Haynes lost two-thirds of their teams in the game, they bonded together. With Danielle Murphree and Shane Meaney involved in a showmance, the four decided to align, forming the "Team Touché" alliance. The name originated from Danielle’s frequent misuse of the word "Touché," which the other members never corrected.

Main article: The Quack Pack

Although strong as a group of four, Dan Gheesling believed expanding the alliance was necessary to secure majority votes in the house. Consequently, Team Touché brought in Ian Terry and rebranded the alliance as the "Quack Pack."

Every member of the alliance reached the jury phase of the game.
