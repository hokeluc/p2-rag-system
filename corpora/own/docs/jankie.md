The Big Brother "Junior Artificial Neuro-Kinetic Intelligence Entity", aka JANKIE is the secondary antagonist of Big Brother 26 (US), who unintentionally tortured the Final 8 houseguests during the "JANKIE World" twist in Week 9.

At the start of Week 10, he was deleted by his creator AINSLEY for being "too fun." However, after her defeat during the season finale, he was revived, and production teased that he may return in future seasons. However, according to Julie Chen-Moonves, he is not slated to appear during Big Brother 27 (US).

He is voiced by Chris MacEwan, a regular member of the Big Brother crew, primarily working as a tape librarian.

During Week 9 of Big Brother 26, JANKIE initiated the "JANKIE World" twist, effectively transforming the backyard into a carnival-themed, highly controlled environment where the Final 8 houseguests were forced to live for an entire week.

Houseguests endured numerous challenges and restrictions including:

Sleeping on uncomfortable cots outdoors, with only portable toilets available.
Limited food options restricted mainly to pizza and ice cream.
Compulsory participation in dance parties and sing-alongs to JANKIE’s catchy theme song multiple times a day.
Harsh environmental conditions including extreme heat and constant bright carnival lighting which disrupted sleep.
No privacy or traditional social gameplay spaces, which severely impacted usual strategic discussions.

Despite the oppressive conditions, the twist featured two Power of Veto competitions – the traditional veto and a special "JANKIE Veto." Angela Murray won the veto and used it to save Kimo Apaka, which led to T'kor Clottey’s nomination replacement and eventual eviction.

AINSLEY decided to delete JANKIE at the start of Week 10 for being "too fun." He resurrected during the finale and it was described as an funny moment and that possibly hinted at possible appearances in future seasons. However, it has been confirmed by Julie Chen-Moonves that JANKIE will not appear in Big Brother 27.

JANKIE’s voice actor, Chris MacEwan, had previously voiced the narrator of the Whacktivity Competition and various camp-themed twists during Big Brother 21 (US).
The "JANKIE World" twist lasted exactly seven days, during which houseguests faced no escape from mandatory carnival-themed activities.
Official JANKIE merchandise, including t-shirts and hats, were released due to significant fan demand online.
The twist significantly disrupted normal Big Brother strategy as houseguests were confined to the backyard, severely limiting private conversations.
