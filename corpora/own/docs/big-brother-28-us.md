# Big Brother 28 (US)

Once again, this season was revealed by production ahead of an official renewal announcement from CBS, when they updated their casting website on August 1, 2025 during Big Brother 27 (US).

On May 13, 2026, it was announced that the season would have a 90-minute premiere on Thursday, July 9, 2026, followed by another 90-minute episode the next Sunday. The traditional Sunday, Wednesday, Thursday episode schedule returned, with all episodes airing at 8 PM throughout the season. The 90-minute Wednesday episodes returned for this season, while the Sunday and Thursday episodes would remain at an hour length following the premiere week. This season marked the 1000th episode of Big Brother US, a primetime television first, and featured the most programming hours in the show's history. It was also announced that the companion show, Big Brother: Unlocked, would return for its second season, airing every other Friday at 8 PM starting on July 10.

On May 26, 2026, it was announced that this season would be the last one to air on Global in Canada. Global was the home of Big Brother US in Canada since its debut, and aired Canada's own Big Brother franchise from 2014 to 2024, after picking it up from sister channel Slice. On June 4, 2026, it was later announced that future seasons would air on CTV, following Bell Media's acquisition of Big Brother Canada.

On June 30, 2026, CBS announced that actor Jerry O'Connell would join the second season of Big Brother: Unlocked alongside returning hosts and former winners Derrick Levasseur and Taylor Hale from the first season. O'Connell returns to the franchise after appearing on the second episode of Unlocked in season 1. Prior to that, O'Connell was brought in last minute to guest host the Week 8 Eviction in Big Brother 26 (US) after it was announced that Julie Chen-Moonves was quarantining from a COVID infection.

In the official release, O'Connell stated, "Big Brother is my summer and rolls into my fall too. I’m already watching on Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays, so the fact that CBS is now paying me to do it is kind of amazing. It’s an absolute honor to sit next to Derrick – ‘Champ,’ as I call him – and Taylor, who is my all-time favorite Big Brother winner (sorry, Jag). This is what we call in showbiz a dream gig. I’ll do my best to be the voice of the fans, but as Julie says, expect the unexpected. And most importantly, remember to love one another. I’m soooo excited to join the Big Brother fam!"

The first episode of the sideshow would go on to premiere on July 10th, the night after the premiere.

Unlike the previous season, Unlocked now featured a live audience for tapings and included voting aspects that would change the game.

On July 1st, 2026, CBS released the official house for the first time as the "Time Trip" theme, in which was leaked earlier via the Chinese Promotional Leak earlier in the preseason.

The theme has centered around time travel as the franchise prepares for it's landmarked 1,000 episode that will air during the season. The house has, "turning time into the ultimate twist. Houseguests will enter a world where rooms, relics and competitions transport them across moments in time, while early twists shake up the game from the start. Inspired by decades like the ’80s and Y2K, new powers and challenges can disrupt strategy and alliances."

Earlier in the week, Julie Chen-Moonves led journalists on a tour inside of the house.

As the season is a theme of a blast to the past, Julie hinted as some previous season aspects could be returning to the Big Brother game.

In a quote to Men's Journal reporter Sharon Tharp, she stated, "time travel, so that could go forward or backwards, and that will play into some of the competitions. Maybe we’ll do the new twist, which is you love that competition in Season 4, but they have to be in, like, I don’t know, surrounded by dinosaurs, who knows."

Julie would then state that "Controversial people will be factored in." When asked who, and the possibility of which other possible reality show could be featured, she stated, "If you’re a Big Brother fan, you’ll be like, ‘Aha!’ And I think a lot of people will be like, ‘Yes!’ And I think some people [will be] like, ‘What?’ But it’s good TV. And if you love reality television beyond Big Brother…you’ll be excited as well."

On July 6, 2026, CBS announced the Big Brother: Broveal, a YouTube livestream that will be hosted by Julie as she reveals the cast and dives deeper into the theme, the house and the twists. Following this inaugural stream, the YouTube channel will be used to showcase Live Feeds for free for limited periods following the conclusion of each episode, including Unlocked. Live Feeds will still be hosted with full access for Pluto TV and Paramount+ subscribers.

The reveal was set as a live premiere on YouTube on July 6th to air on July 7th. However, an hour before it was to go live, it was taken offline in order to, according to Hamsterwatch, swap out a houseguest.

In a post by Hamsterwatch, the letters "A A B C D D H J K L L L M M R R T" were revealed to be the initials of the seventeen houseguests that would be entering the house. When the cast was revealed, 13 initials would go on to match the 14 houseguests revealed by CBS. It was then discovered that Levi Banks was supposed to be the fourteenth houseguest, but was replaced in the final moments by Yash Patel after it was revealed in a preseason article that was not adjusted before the reveal.

That would be the explanation for the additional "L" and no "Y".

Shortly after the reveal, the official Paramount press site released a press release stating that, "Additional surprise Houseguests will be revealed on air," signifying that more houseguests would be revealed on the premiere episode.

Three hours after the initial cast release, Entertainment Weekly's Dalton Ross revealed that Survivor contestants would be included in the season's cast. In an exclusive announcement for Entertainment Weekly, former Survivor: Edge of Extinction and Survivor 50: In the Hands of the Fans contestant Rick Devens would be joining the cast.

In the same article, it stated "And Devens is not the only former Survivor competitor that will be part of the new cast, as EW has learned that a second former castaway will also be vying for Big Brother’s $750,000 prize. The identity of that second player has yet to be revealed."

As well as the Rick Devens speculation, former Survivor 45 winner and fellow Survivor 50: In the Hands of the Fans competitor Dee Valladares was leaked online by various social media accounts. However, it would be revealed that Dee's participation would not be revealed until the first episode of Big Brother: Unlocked.

One hour later, Sharon Tharp exclusively revealed that Angela Murray from Big Brother 26 (US) would be the sole Big Brother returnee. By returning, season 28 becomes the second season in a row that a single Big Brother alum would return to play alongside new houseguests.

The theme for the season was revealed to be time travel. Throughout the season, the houseguests would be traveling through time to compete in competitions and twists.

On premiere night, it was revealed that Enzo Palumbo from Big Brother 12 (US) & Big Brother 22 (US) was behind the dining room "time machine" with his "company" "Meow Meow Enterprises"

According to Enzo, the multi-verse laser from Big Brother 25 (US) & Big Brother: Reindeer Games that was broken by former houseguests Britney Haynes, Danielle Reyes & Frankie Grande gave him the idea for time travel, that led to this season's twist format.

The opening shot of the season was a tribute card to Big Brother 27 (US) houseguest Mickey Lee who had passed away after a series of cardiac arrests following complications from the flu.

For the 1000th episode, CBS sent out invitations out to previous houseguests to come to the live show. However, like with the Season 20 premiere, CBS would not pay for flights to Los Angeles, so houseguests who wished to attend must fly out themselves.

BB Block Buster: Returning for a third season in a row, the BB Block Buster forces the Head of Household to nominate three houseguests for eviction. Then on eviction night, the three nominated houseguests will have a last chance opportunity to save themselves.
Premiere Night Safety: On premiere night, the fourteen new houseguests would not be eligible for the first Head of Household. As a result, the houseguests would compete for safety from the first eviction. After a qualifying competition, twelve of the fourteen would be divided into three groups of four in which one would win immunity for the week. The winner of each competition would unleash a "Reality Icon" into the game.
Additional Houseguests: After each competition on premiere night, a "Reality Icon" would then enter the game after receiving a letter telling them to show up to the Big Brother House in 2026. The icons were revealed as former Big Brother houseguest Angela Murray from Big Brother 26 (US), alongside Survivor alumni Rick Devens and Dee Valladares.
Fake Houseguest: In was initially teased that former winner Rachel Reilly would enter the game as the seventeenth houseguest for the second consecutive season, however, it would be revealed that she was not actually playing and another icon would be taking her spot in the game. This icon was then revealed to be Dee.

BB Time Capsule: During the first week, a fan vote opened up giving America the option to send a houseguest into the BB Time Capsule, giving them the chance to win a power or suffer a punishment from a prior season. Beginning in Week 2 and going until Week 7, the houseguest who wins the vote will enter the Time Capsule and compete to try and earn a power, or fail and receive a punishment. There will be six different powers and punishments up for grabs. Once a houseguest is voted into the Time Capsule, they are ineligible to go in again, win or lose.
Year 1000 Double Eviction: To celebrate 1,000 episodes, the houseguests faced a new twist during the start of Week 8, with no HoH, PoV, or Block Buster, but still ending with someone getting sent home before the next live show, leaving the Final 9 houseguests to make up the jury phase.
Stone of Safety: The houseguests will be facing off in four competitions. The winner of each competition will become safe for the night and can select one other person to save alongside them. The winner and the person they save are ineligible to compete in the rest of the competitions.
Stone of Sacrifice: The first houseguest who wins safety must sacrifice two houseguests, forcing them to face off in the next round of competitions. The winner then earns safety for themselves and one other person, and also chooses the next person (or people) to go against the person that was left behind. The winner of the fourth and final competition will then have the sole vote to evict between the remaining houseguests who are not safe.
Pharaoh's Curse: The Final 5 houseguests were given the chance to have everyone get $1,000, but they would be each tempted with their own button. The first person to push that button, if anyone, would earn $10,000 for themselves, leaving everyone else with nothing. If no one pushes the button, everyone keeps their $1,000. However, unbeknownst to the houseguests, pushing the button would subsequently unleash a curse on the house, an extra "Egyptian Eviction" to determine 5th place in the middle of the week. During the eviction, the houseguests would publicly cast their votes by destroying an artifact belonging to one of the nominees.
