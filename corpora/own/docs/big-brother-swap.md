# Big Brother Swap

This article is about the twist that appeared on Big Brother (US). For the international Big Brother Swap twist, see Housemate Exchange.

The Big Brother Swap is a twist that appeared on Big Brother 1 (US) and is one of the opening night twists on Big Brother 19 (US). The core mechanic of the twist is that a preexisting houseguest is replaced by a new houseguest, typically triggered by a temptation or offer that creates a vacancy in the cast.

After Week 9 of Big Brother 1 (US), it was revealed during the live eviction show that the six remaining houseguests would be offered $20,000 to walk from the game. If a houseguest accepted the offer, they would leave and be replaced by a woman named Beth Thieme. Ultimately, no one took the offer (including a subsequent $50,000 offer made later that night), and Beth did not enter the house.

Big Brother 19 (US)[]

On premiere night of Big Brother 19 (US), the sixteen houseguests were tempted with $25,000, with the warning that accepting it would unleash a personal consequence and a house-wide consequence. One houseguest accepted the money and was instructed to throw the first Head of Household competition. When the Houseguests returned inside, the doorbell rang and Paul Abrahamian re-entered the house as a houseguest.

Paul was granted the opportunity to save eight houseguests. The remaining eight competed for safety, leaving three houseguests nominated. The nominees then voted on whether their fate would be decided by house votes or a competition; by a 2–1 vote, they chose a house vote. By a vote of 8–3–2, Cameron was evicted, opening a spot for Paul.

The Big Brother Swap from Big Brother 19 is the first time in Big Brother history that a returnee re-entered the game as a Houseguest and was safe from eviction on premiere night.
