The Four Horsemen was an alliance on Big Brother 5 (US).

The Four Horsemen was created on the first day of Big Brother 5 (US) by Jase Wirey and Scott Long.

After Jase won the first Head of Household competition, the alliance seemed to be in a powerful position. However, Jase and Scott’s behavior would make the, very big targets for the opposing Pinky Swear Alliance. Despite this, the alliance was able to unanimously evict Mike Lubinski, who was openly going after the alliance. The following week, affiliate Marvin Latimer won Head of Household and the alliance aided in taking out Lori Valenti.

During the first few weeks of the game, Jase and Holly King had become a showmance, however, this would cause friction in the alliance as the rest of the alliance didn’t trust Holly. Along with this, conflict between Jase and Scott started to arise because of Holly due to their mutual liking of her. After Drew won Head of Household in week three, he put Holly on the block as the target with the goal of creating peace in the alliance. However, what the alliance didn’t account for was that Holly was a loyal and vital number for the alliance. Despite Jase’s pushes to keep Holly, she was evicted in a 7-1 vote later that week. Also this week, both Jase and Scott participated in a heated public argument with Diane Henry. After Diane won Head of Household in week four, Diane put Jase and Scott on the block and ultimately Scott was evicted that week. After Nakomis Dedmon won Head of Household, the entire house excluding Jase and Michael Ellis created a plan to backdoor Jase. After Drew won the Power of Veto, he sealed Jase’s fate as he used the veto allowing Nakomis to put Jase up and evict him in a 6-1 vote.

Despite Drew’s defection and their low numbers, Drew and Michael were able to reconnect and work together in the game.

Using their relationships and the breakup of the majority alliance, Drew and Michael were able to make it to the final two together where Drew won in a 4-3 vote.

The Four Horsemen had no members in the jury.
