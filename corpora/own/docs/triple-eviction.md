# Triple Eviction

| This article on Big Brother Wiki follows the North American format of the show. Note this may follow a different format than the original version of the show. |

During a Triple Eviction, one Head of Household (HOH) is named, and they must nominate three houseguests for eviction. A Power of Veto competition follows—where the HOH is not allowed to compete—and then the Power of Veto Ceremony is held. The houseguests then cast their votes to save one of the nominees. The two nominees who receive the fewest votes are subsequently evicted.

The first Triple Eviction occurred in Big Brother Canada 3 and returned in Big Brother Canada 5, Big Brother Canada 6, and Big Brother Canada 10.

The first instance of a Triple Eviction on BBUS took place during the finale of Celebrity Big Brother 1 (US). Although not officially called a Triple Eviction, the format was quite similar. For the second eviction cycle of the night, the final four houseguests competed in the Final HOH competition. The winner had to evict two houseguests, leaving one to join them in the Final Two. As this occurred during the final eviction cycle, no Power of Veto competition was held. This format returned in Celebrity Big Brother 2 (US), becoming the standard format for the final week of the American Celebrity seasons.

The first officially branded Triple Eviction in the United States took place during Big Brother 22 (US). Unlike the Big Brother Canada format, the US version featured two Fast Forward eviction cycles in one night, instead of a single cycle with two evictions.

There was a Triple Eviction planned for Big Brother Canada 4. However, since Ramsey Aburaneh walked from the house, the triple was reverted back to a double.
Kevin Martin is the first houseguest and first male houseguest to be evicted as a result of a Triple Eviction.
He is also the first and currently only houseguest to participate in 2 Triple Evictions.
Willow MacDonald is the first female houseguest to be evicted as a result of a Triple Eviction.
Brittnee Blair is the first houseguest to be named Head of Household during a Triple Eviction.
Marissa Jaret Winokur is the first US houseguest to be named HOH during a Triple Eviction, while Ariadna Gutierrez and Mark McGrath are the first US houseguests (female and male respectively) to be evicted during a Triple Eviction.
Celebrity Big Brother 2 (US) has the only Triple Eviction in which the latter two evictees are of the same gender.
All four people in Big Brother Canada who were voted to be saved each had 3 votes to save.
The first ever civilian Triple Evictions on Big Brother Canada and Big Brother US both featured a man named Kevin being evicted, that being Kevin Martin and Kevin Campbell respectively.
While not confirmed, it is heavily rumored that Big Brother 23 (US) was set to have a Triple Eviction on Day 71, but production changed the schedule to have two Double Evictions back to back on Days 65 and 71, in order for The Cookout to have a chance at being in the house by themselves for a full week if they managed to become the Final 6. The rumor has credibility with the Day 71 eviction episode being scheduled for two hours, which was the same episode length as Season 22's Triple Eviction.
