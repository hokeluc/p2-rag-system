# Camp Director

Camp Director
is a twist on

Big Brother 21 (US)
.

Press Release[]

Retrieved from CBS.com

History[]

On Night One, the houseguests had to elect a "Camp Director" to rule over the house. The winner would have the power to banish four houseguests to the Hit The Road competition where the loser would be evicted from the house.

The majority of the house voted for Jackson Michie, making him the Camp Director. Jackson chose to banish Cliff Hogg III, David Alexander, Jessica Milagros, and Kemi Fakunle. David lost the competition and was the first houseguest evicted from the house.
