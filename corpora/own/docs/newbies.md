The Newbies was an alliance on Big Brother 13 (US).

On the first night in the Big Brother house, the "new" contestants of Big Brother came together to beat the Veterans.

The Newbies strategy was to band together and form a strong alliance and have the numbers to take out all the Veterans. Newbie, Dominic Briones was able to get Veteran, Daniele Briones to come to their side. But with nearly half the alliance playing both sides with the Veterans it started to fall apart.

After Dick Donato walked from the game, the newbies alliance was up in numbers 8 to 5. Unfortunately for them the veterans had HOH and Keith Henderson and Porsche Briggs were currently nominated. The majority of newbies decided that it would be better to keep Keith since Porsche was playing both sides and Keith was in The Regulators side alliance. The veterans however wanted to keep Porsche and they convinced Kalia Booker and Shelly Moore with promises of safety to evict Keith. To the shock of much of the alliance Keith was evicted by a vote of 6-4.

Following Keith’s eviction, 3 newbies were taken out in 4 weeks, losing Cassi Colvin, Dominic Briones and Lawon Exum. This just left Adam Poch, Kalia, Porsche and Shelly left. But they were split in the current house divisions with Kaila and Porsche aligning with Daniele and Adam and Shelly aligning with Jeff Schroeder and Jordan Lloyd.

During Week 6, Daniele was able to convince Shelly that Jeff and Jordan stood in her way of winning. This caused Shelly to defect from her alliance with them. She tried to save Daniele that week but was unsuccessful. However this meant going into next week, Kalia, Porsche and Shelly were aligned and ready to take a shot at Jeff and Jordan. Thankfully for them Kalia won the next HOH and with Shelly officially defecting from that alliance and voting to evict Jeff. Kalia could cast the tie-breaking vote to evict him. Unfortunately due to Pandora's Box and Adam favoring aligning with the remainder of Jeff’s alliance Jordan and Rachel Reilly. Shelly and Kalia were evicted the next two weeks.

Due to Adam winning HOH and Porsche winning POV, Jordan was evicted. Following Jordan’s eviction, Rachel won the final HOH and evicted Adam. Although Porsche came close to winning, Rachel won by a vote of 4-3.

Coincidentally, the highest ranking member of the alliance, Porsche and the lowest ranking member: Keith were a duo.
The only two Houseguests to receive a Golden Key in the alliance were Shelly and Porsche.
