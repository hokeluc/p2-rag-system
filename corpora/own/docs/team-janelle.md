Team Janelle was one of the four teams formed as part of the Coach Twist introduced on Big Brother 14 (US). This team was led by returning veteran coach Janelle Pierzina, known for her strong competitive record in previous seasons. The new players on Team Janelle were Joe Arvin, Ashley Iocco, and Wil Heuser.

Team Janelle struggled strategically during the season despite Janelle’s experience as player. Unlike other teams that secured HOH and POV wins, this team did not succeed in winning any competitions as a collective unit throughout the regular game, making it vulnerable to nominations and evictions.

Team Janelle was the only team in Big Brother 14 not to win a single Head of Household or Power of Veto competition during the week-by-week gameplay.
Janelle's only wins in the season were in separate Coaches Competitions, not in the traditional HOH or POV roles.
Team Janelle was the first team to have all members evicted over the course of the season.
Janelle Pierzina is the only member of this team to return to compete in subsequent seasons.
Despite no team HOH or POV wins, Joe and Ashley leveraged strong social gameplay to advance further than some other players who won multiple competitions.
