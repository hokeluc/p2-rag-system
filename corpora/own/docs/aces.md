Aces was one of the four teams on Big Brother 23 (US).

Whitney won the first competition of the season, granting her the team captain of the Aces. She chose Brent Champagne, Derek Xiao and Hannah Chaddha.

The Aces managed to remain intact during the first two weeks of the game. However due to Brent’s cockiness annoying the other houseguests and Whitney openly going after the Kings team. Brent and Whitney became house targets and after failing to win HOH or a Wildcard competition. Brent and Whitney were evicted unanimously.

Derek and Hannah remained safe for two weeks after Whitney’s eviction, due to Derek winning HOH and Kyland Young keeping both of them safe. However after Sarah Beth Steagall rose to power she decided to target Derek for eviction, due to his competition threat and him nominating her. Sarah Beth successfully backdoored and evicted Derek after he opted not to play in the BB High Roller room.

Hannah had longevity in the game due to The Cookout alliance's dominance protecting her. However, once Azah Awasum won HOH at the final 5, she targeted Hannah and after Hannah failed to win the POV, she was evicted by a vote of 2-0, thus officially marking the team's extinction.
