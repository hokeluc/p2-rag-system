The Chillers is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

Despite initially being on opposite sides of the house, Cameron Hardin and Red Utley quickly became close and soon became each other’s closest ally.

In Week 4, Cameron would win the Pressure Cooker Competition to become Head of Household, and later on, Red would win the Power of Veto. Cameron's HOH reign would come to an unsuccessful end, however, as Matt Klotz would use the BB Power of Invincibility on Cameron's target Jag Bains, leading to the latter's immediate re-entry into the game.

After several scuffles, Cameron and Red stayed loyal to each other, but lost a substantial amount of trust.

Adversary Jared Fields would nominate both of them as the Week 5 HOH, and although Cameron would win the POV, a vote flip initiated by Izzy Gleicher would send Red home over Jag in an 8-2 vote, ending The Chillers.
