Team Boogie was one of the four teams apart of the coach twist introduced on Big Brother 14. The four members were Mike Malin, Ian Terry, Jenn Arroyo, and Frank Eudy.

Mike Malin is the only member to not make it to the jury.
Ironically, he was the coach of the team.
This is the only team to have 3 males and 1 female.
Jenn Arroyo is the only member to not be a returning player at one point.
This is the only team that did not have two members who were evicted back to back.
