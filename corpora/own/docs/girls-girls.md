Girls Girls was an alliance on Big Brother 24 (US).

On the first night of Big Brother 24 (US), Alyssa Snider and Paloma Aguilar gathered the girls in the bedroom and they all formed the "Girls Girls".

Despite Alyssa, Brittany Hoopes, and Paloma being backstage, chosen by Pooch Pucciarelli on the first night, the alliance controlled the first week. With affiliate Daniel Durston as the first HOH, the alliance sought to target Taylor Hale. With the help of Monte Taylor, the alliance successfully got her put up as a replacement nominee at the veto meeting.

Due to mental health issues, Paloma walked from the game on Day 8. This proved to be troublesome for the alliance, not only because they lost a member, but it also meant the eviction would be cancelled and their target, Taylor, stayed in the house.

Despite Taylor staying in the house, the alliance proved to have more success as Jasmine Davis won the following HOH. Originally, Jasmine decided to target Taylor again. However after Ameerah Jones and Nicole Layog realized that Pooch was a bigger threat and him volunteering to go up for nomination, the alliance decided to shift their target over to him; even though some of the boys wanted to keep Pooch. Ultimately the alliance got their wish as Pooch was evicted by a vote of 12-0. After the new Festie Bestie twist was revealed, the alliance had to adapt. Following this Matt Turner won HOH. Originally his plan was to backdoor Taylor.

On the night before the veto meeting Joseph Abdin, Kyle, Monte and Turner realized that the girls didn’t have their backs, so they decided to form a new alliance with themselves, Brittany, Michael, and Taylor. After the alliance was formed they decided to turn their target to Ameerah, who they saw as the leader of the other side. At the veto meeting, Turner named Ameerah and Terrance Higgins as his replacement nominees, surprising many in the house, namely the Girls Girls alliance.

Surprised at Turner's decision, the alliance started to scramble for votes to keep Ameerah. Despite being reassured by the house that Ameerah would be staying, the vote came out 7-4, with Ameerah being evicted. This, combined with Brittany defecting, meant that there were only three official members of the alliance left.

Following Ameerah’s eviction, The Leftovers continued to stay in power as they won the next 4 HOHs. Using that power, they took out Nicole, Daniel, Indy, and Jasmine. Alyssa was able to survive, thanks to fellow outsider Terrance winning HOH and keeping her safe during the Split House Twist. This still left Alyssa as the sole remaining member of the alliance who hadn’t defected.

Following the Split House Twist, Alyssa and Brittany decided to re-align together and brought in Taylor to form an alliance. This alliance proved to be a success as for the next few rounds they were able to take out Kyle, Terrance, and Michael. However after Monte won HOH at the final five the girls seemed to be in trouble.

Monte nominated Alyssa and Brittany together, but after Brittany won veto and saved herself, Monte named Taylor as the replacement nominee. Despite Brittany campaigning to save Alyssa, they couldn’t get the votes as Monte cast the tie-breaking voting to evict on her. Following Alyssa’s eviction, Brittany failed to win the HOH and POV next week, and Monte cast the sole vote to evict on her.

Every member who made it to jury voted for Taylor to win.
Taylor was the only woman from the season that was not part of the alliance.
