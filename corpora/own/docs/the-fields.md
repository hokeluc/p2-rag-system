The Fields is an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US).

During the first few moments in the house after Cirie Fields was revealed to be a player, Izzy Gleicher went up to Jared Fields and told him she knew that Cirie was his mom. However, Izzy wanted to work with them, instead of working against them. Despite their alliance not being named till a few weeks later, the alliance was working with each other since nearly the beginning.

The trio took power early in the game, after figuring out a strategy of having the group play both sides, the trio was able to gain a lot of control over the game through their multitude of alliances. This power stretch lasted for nearly six weeks before their games were blown up.

The trio’s control over the game did not go unnoticed by a few other houseguests, specifically America Lopez. After Izzy and Felicia Cannon were placed on the block in week six, America tried to convince her showmance Cory Wurtenberger that now was the time to take the shot at the trio. Despite being wary of the idea at first, Cory decided to go with it. Cory was able to use information leaked from Izzy and Jared to expose their multitude of alliance to the majority of the house. This led to the majority of the house coming together and deciding to vote out Izzy. After catching wind of this plan, Jared attempted to confront a few houseguests, most notably Matt Klotz, in which he threw Jag Bains under the bus to him. After Matt told Cory and they asked Jag about it, they all decided to meet with Jared in the Have-Not room for an open discussion. This open discussion turned into an argument between Cory and Jared as Jared had now gotten the confirmation that there was a vote flip. Later that evening, Izzy was evicted by a vote of 8-1, with only Cirie voting to keep her. Despite being the two house targets, Cirie and Jared were able to stay safe once Jared won Head of Household. Seeking revenge for Izzy’s eviction and looking to take out a major competitor, Jared set a plan to backdoor Cameron Hardin. Jared’s plan was successful as Cameron was evicted. However, it was revealed to be a Double Eviction and after Cory won HOH, he quickly nominated Blue Kim and Jared. After Matt won the Power of Veto and discarded it, Jared was evicted by a vote of 6-1, and despite becoming a BB Zombie, Jared was unable to return to the game.

Despite being heavily affiliated with Izzy and Jared, Cirie was able to fade into the background as her low competition prowess and her no longer having control over the game, led to her being able to stay under the radar as the group that turned the game against the trio started to go after each other.
