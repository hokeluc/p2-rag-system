The Vixens was an alliance on Big Brother 20 (US)

Angela Rummans and Rachel Swindler quickly bonded as both members of the Level Six alliance. This led them to form “The Vixens.”

During Bayleigh Dayton’s reign as HOH in Week 5. Bayleigh used Rachel as a pawn. To gain trust with Rachel, Bayleigh told her that Tyler Crispen came and asked who the replacement nominee would be if the POV was used and Bayleigh told him Angela. Rachel misheard this as Tyler trying to get Angela nominated. Rachel then scrambled and told the Level Six alliance believing Tyler had defected. After Level Six realized it was a misunderstanding, they decided Rachel was too much of a loose cannon to keep in the house. They along with JC Mounduix and Sam Bledsoe decided to blindside Rachel. Much to the shock of Rachel and the other side, Rachel was evicted by a vote of 5-4; receiving Angela’s vote to evict.
