Nicorey is an alliance & showmance on Big Brother 18 (US) that consists of Nicole Franzel and Corey Brooks.

Throughout their run in the house, they proved to be a powerful force by dodging the block together and playing under the radar. When the time came later in the game, they both began to play more aggressively winning several HOHs and POVs. For three weeks, they were able to control their targets and get out of being outsiders. In week 12, when Paul Abrahamian won HOH and the final POV, he had no choice but to nominate them together. Ultimately, Corey Brooks was targeted and evicted by the sole vote of James Huling.
