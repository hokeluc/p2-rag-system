The Weirdos was an alliance on Big Brother 16 (US).

Early in the game Nicole Franzel bonded with Christine Brecht and Hayden Voss and created an alliance between the three of them

Despite Nicole being the Head of Household in Week 6. Christine flipped on her and Hayden and voted to evict Jocasta Odom. This came as a shock to both Hayden and Nicole. Their night continued to grow worse as it was a Double Eviction and Hayden and Nicole were the final nominees. Ultimately, Hayden got evicted by a vote of 5-2.

Following the double eviction, Christine and Nicole won HOH. Despite it looking like an easy week with both in power. Nicole ended up being dethroned and after the POV was used, Christine betrayed Nicole and nominated her as a replacement nominee and Nicole was soon evicted.

Despite Nicole re-entering the house, There was no trust between Christine and Nicole and in another double eviction. Christine and Nicole were evicted in the same night.

Hayden Voss was the only male member in the alliance.
