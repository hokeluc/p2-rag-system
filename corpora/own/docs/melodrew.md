Melodrew were an alliance and showmance on Big Brother 28 (US).

The pair of Melody Morris and Drew Campbell connected in week one, despite being on two opposing sides of the house, as Melody was loyal to players such as Jason De Puy and Giggle Girls, whilst Drew was a member of The Toolshed. The pair were constantly in danger due to Drew's own allies paranoia about his loyalty to her, which led to own tension in their dynamic as Drew chose allies such as Barrett Pfeiffer and Rick Devens over Melody. In week 7, Barrett betrayed Drew and convinced Melody to evict him on the block no matter what. Drew won safety, but voted out Melody's closest ally Mallory Aurichio in the process. However, the pair ultimately reconciled and recemented their alliance during Drew's Head of Household reign, where he refused to nominate Melody despite pressure from his allies. Despite this, Melody continued to work against Drew by attempting to evict both Drew's close ally Devens and Drew himself in the following weeks. However, when Melody won the Double Eviction Head of Household, she refused to nominate Drew despite pressure from other players, instead taking out Yash Patel. This led to the pair finally becoming completely loyal to one another, but this harmony was short lived when Devens won the following Head of Household and, not wanting to go to the end with a showmance, nominated the duo, leading to Melody's eviction.

Melody initially found Drew annoying in the house, but over the first week the pair became closer after joining the alliances 4 Seasons and The Court Jesters together. Drew was aligned with The Icons and Barrett Pfeiffer, whilst Melody was aligned with Mallory Aurichio and Lyric Medeiros. They created an unofficial and unnamed final 2 alliance together at the end of week 1.

House target Ashley Trail came to Melody and revealed that an alliance including Drew offered her safety, however Drew lied and claimed that was not true. This lead to the flipped vote the Icons planned to be aborted and Ashley was evicted unanimously.

In week 2, Rick Devens nominated Melody as a pawn to evict one of her friends Rome Seymour and Jason De Puy. When Devens won the veto, he initially promised to save Melody before changing his mind to save Lyric instead. Drew's attempts to prevent this and save Melody lead to suspicions about his loyalties. This was furthered by the pair becoming increasingly flirtatious. Melody ultimately lost the blockbuster, but remained a pawn as Rome was evicted near unanimously.

In week 3, when Mallory was nominated, both Drew and Melody attempted to campaign on her behalf, leading to further suspicions from Drew's allies. Melody also attempted to campaign on Jason's behalf, but Drew wanted Jason to remain the target.

In week 4, the suspicions surrounding Drew and Melody's relationship as well as Drew's loyalties caught up with the pair as Drew's ally Haley Thogmartin nominated them together. Despite Haley intending for him to be a pawn in order to evict Taylor Brown, the icons convinced her and other allies that he should be the primary target, with Melody as the secondary in order to break up their duo. Both Melody and Drew believed the other to be the primary target, leading to many emotions and further cementing of them as a duo. However, near the end of the week, Dee Valladares began to realize that Drew's blind loyalty to her was more beneficial then his possible loyalty to Melody, so began working to make Melody the primary target. Once Lyric Medeiros was named the replacement nominee Drew had began planting seeds against Lyric hoping that the icons would see her as a bigger threat than Melody. On eviction day, further chaos happened when third nominee Lyric Medeiros was exposed for throwing both Melody and Mallory under the bus, leading to Dee and Rick Devens deciding to make her the primary target instead. Drew won the blockbuster, and several members of the house were blindsided as Lyric was evicted over Melody.

Due to LaTrice Verrett winning HoH in week 5, the duo felt safe. They created the four-person alliance F.A.P. with Barrett and Mallory, although Drew remained loyal to the Icons, unaware of their prior betrayal. In week 6, Yash Patel won the HoH and began targeting the icons, which they countered by merging with F.A.P. and creating the Mosh Pit. However, this move made Melody realize that Drew was likely not loyal to her and was already working with the Icons, leading to distrust between the pair.

Devens used his Diamond Power of Veto to save Dee and nominate Kamu Kirk, leading to Melody and Mallory to worry the Mosh Pit was just to protect them from this risky move. They decided to flip the vote to keep Kamu, and informed Drew, who spent all night with the pair to ensure this did not happen. Ultimately, Melody did not flip and Kamu was evicted near unanimously. She publicly blamed this on Drew, reluctant to trust him anymore.

In week 7, Dee won HoH and her continued paranoia about Drew's allegiances lead her to target him once again. Yash lied to Melody to sow further doubt about Drew, hoping to get him evicted. However, at the nomination ceremony, Drew successfully diverted the target off him regaining both the Icons and Melody's trust. In the days following the nomination ceremony Drew became increasingly frustrated with the icons and even suggested to his ally Barrett that they should prioritize the F.A.P. alliance moving forward and suggests that they should reveal the Crossovers alliance to Melody and Mallory. Drew also suggested that they stop telling the icons everything but Barrett proceeded to talk him out of this and encouraged Drew to continue giving the icons information and that it would be a mistake to reveal the Crossovers. Dee makes Mallory the renomination and subsequent target, upsetting Melody. Drew was very annoyed by Dee's decision but does not try hard to prevent this out of fear for his own safety, causing further distrust between the pair. Angered by Mallory's nomination, Barrett exposed all of his alliances with Drew to Mallory and Melody despite knowing that Drew was prepared to flip on the icons days earlier. Barrett decided to paint Drew in a negative light by falsely suggesting that Drew was unwilling to flip on the Icons leading to Melody to decide to evict him if he did not win blockbuster. However, Drew did win blockbuster and Mallory was subsequently evicted with only Melody and Barrett voting to evict Taylor.

Melody was infuriated with Drew for evicting Mallory, and refused to speak to him even though she herself had planned on voting him out without even confronting him with the information that Barrett had told her. However, during the Medieval twist, the pair partially reconciled personally although their game duo was still broken. Following Haley's eviction via twist, Drew continued to insist upon his loyalty to his final 2 with Melody. Following Drew's HoH win, he and Melody spent most of the night drinking wine together in his room, where he promised to never nominate her. However, in her DR's, Melody stated she was now only flirting as a game move.

After Zingbot implied that Melody was using Drew, many of the houseguests were surprised that the pair had not kissed. The Icons also attempted to use this to convince Drew to nominate Melody, but he refused. After Yash won the veto, Drew heavily considered nominating Barrett, but Melody pushed back on it. Ultimately, despite Drew wanting to renominate Dee, Devens volunteered leading Drew to feel like he had no choice but to renominate him, which he did. Melody planned with Barrett to blindside Drew by evicting Devens if he lost the Blockbuster, although Drew had minor suspicions of this happening.

Throughout his HoH week, Melody and Drew continued to spend most of the nights talking and flirting in his room, leading to speculation around the house about them becoming an official showmance. LaTrice in particular implied that Melody was sleeping in Drew's bed, despite Melody always coming downstairs to sleep, leading to the pair becoming worried about their perception. In the final two nights, Melody did want to sleep up there, but Drew insisted she slept downstairs, leading to minor tension.

Ultimately, Devens won the Blockbuster, meaning Melody's blindside of Drew was unsuccessful and LaTrice was evicted instead.

In week 9, Barrett won HoH and promised both Melody and Drew safety, planning on making a big move by getting one of the Survivors evicted. He followed through on this plan, nominating the Icons trio. The three also joined with the other two remaining new houseguests, Yash and Taylor, to create The Ceasefire, but whilst Melody believed in this alliance, Drew correctly assumed it was fake and that Yash and Taylor would target them at their next opportunity.

Melody and Drew became more physically affectionate over the week, leading to them becoming the subject of much house gossip, particularly from Devens and Angela. Angela notably confronted them about this, much to their embarrassment. Their loyalty was also tested when Barrett confessed he and Melody were planning on voting Drew out in week 7 and Devens in week 8, and Melody also confessed she was telling the DR she was manipulating him. Despite this tension, the pair ultimately continued to work as a duo. Despite both members of Melodrew evicting Dee, Yash and Taylor betrayed the Ceasefire and kept Dee with the intention of evicting Drew.

In week 10, Yash won HoH and whilst Drew assumed he was the target, Melody held out hope that the bribe was fake and she could escape nomination. However, Yash did eventually nominate the three remaining members of F.A.P, with Drew as the primary target. Yash and Taylor fabricated a story to blame Drew for their blindside the previous week, successfully convincing Barrett and Melody to evict him should he remain on the block. However, in the pressure cooker, Drew successfully convinced Yash to throw the competition after thirteen hours, granting him the veto.

Melody openly rooted against Drew during the veto, as if he won she would become the secondary target behind Barrett, annoying many of the houseguests, particularly Devens. Devens told Drew that Melody was just using him for game purposes, and did not like him romantically. Melody attempted to convince Drew to use the veto on her, but he refused and used it on himself. Ultimately, Melody won the blockbuster, her first ever competition, and secured her own safety, leading to Barrett's eviction.

Melody also won the Double Eviction Blockbuster, and targeted the Survivor duo of Dee and Devens. When Devens won the veto, Yash and Taylor attempted to convince her to nominate Drew, but she refused to put him in danger. She ultimately nominated Yash, and despite initially wanting to target Dee, Melodrew ultimately came to the conclusion that Yash was the bigger threat and he was evicted.

When Devens won the HoH in week 11, he became determined to evict Melody so that he would be Drew's closest remaining ally. He intended to nominate the showmance together, despite Drew spending hours begging him to nominate Melody alongside Taylor. Devens and Dee correctly suspected that Drew would use the veto on Melody if he won, automatically forcing Dee to be nominated, so both members of Melodrew were put on the block. Drew did win the veto, leaving Melody on the block as the primary target. The pair were very emotional, knowing it was unlikely they could convince Dee or Devens to keep her, with her ultimately being evicted by Devens after Drew cast his vote to evict Taylor instead.

Drew continuously spoke about Melody in the following days, often retreating to her pod and sleeping in there for the first few days. When he moved back to his own bed, he brought her pillow and blanket with him. In the Jury House, Melody also got into an argument with Angela when she refused to stop questioning Melody about her relationship with Drew.
