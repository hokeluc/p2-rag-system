Chrenchie is an alliance on Big Brother 23 (US), that was created on the first week of the season. The alliance consisted of Christian Birkenberger and Frenchie French.

Frenchie won the first Head of Household competition to become HOH for the week. Christian being a strong competitor made him immediately a target to Frenchie, who expressed his desire to take out the "meatheads" early on. However, Christian ended up winning the Wildcard Competition that week, earning himself safety and ruining Frenchie's plans to nominate him. Instead, Frenchie decided to nominate and target Alyssa Lopez because he believed that Christian and Alyssa were a budding showmance. The night before the first eviction, Frenchie ended up deciding to work with Christian and Alyssa instead of targeting them, inviting them both to The Slaughterhouse. Christian and Frenchie made a Final 2 deal together and Frenchie got the Slaughterhouse to keep Alyssa that week and vote out Travis Long.

In week 2, Frenchie became the house target after his chaotic HOH reign in week 1. Along with the rest of the Slaughterhouse, Christian abandoned Frenchie in order to save his game and contributed to successfully voting him out that week.
