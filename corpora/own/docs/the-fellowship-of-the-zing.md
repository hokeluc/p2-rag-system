The Fellowship of the Zing is an alliance on Big Brother 21 (US) that consists of Cliff Hogg III, Nicole Anthony, and Ovi Kabir.

Ovi is the only member of the alliance to not make the jury stage.
Ovi is also the only member of the the alliance to not make the Top 3 of America's Favorite Houseguest.
All alliance members competed in the Nightmare Whacktivity Competition called "This Stinks".
Coincidentally, "The Fellowship of The Zing" appeared as a subtitle in the final round of the BBFlix & Chill Head of Household competition in Big Brother 20 (US).
