The Board is an alliance in Big Brother 18 (US), it is a side alliance formed within The Executives alliance. It's members are Paulie Calafiore, Paul Abrahamian, and Victor Arroyo.

As Paulie was showing signs that he had neither Paul or Victor's back, they decided to distance themselves away from him. As they stuck into the middle of different sides in the house.
