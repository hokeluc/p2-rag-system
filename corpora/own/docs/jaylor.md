Jaylor was an alliance and showmance on Big Brother 24 (US).

Quickly upon entering the house, Joseph Abdin and Taylor Hale connected and bonded with Joseph being one of the few people who never believed in Paloma Aguilar's lies about Taylor and never mistreated her during the first few weeks. Their relationship didn’t turn game related however until the creation of The Leftovers.

Main article: The Leftovers

After Joseph and Taylor joined The Leftovers in week three, their alliance and friendship continued to grow as they formed a final 2 deal. Joseph and Taylor were able to remain safe for the next few weeks thanks to the dominance of The Leftovers.

Taylor and Joseph’s position in the house began to be put at risk after Taylor won Head of Household in Week 6. After Taylor won HOH, her reign proved to be chaotic, as Taylor changed targets multiple times throughout the weeks which caused the duo to lose trust with members their alliance like Kyle Capener and Matt Turner. Along with other events such as trying to convince Turner to go up as a pawn, Taylor and Joseph began to become targets for many houseguests. At the Split House Twist, Taylor and Joseph were separated. While Taylor was able to remain safe under Michael Bruner's Head of Household reign due to her final 3 deal with him and Brittnay Hoopes, Joseph wasn’t so lucky as Terrance Higgins, an outsider, was the Head of Household on his side. After Kyle Capener leaked The Leftovers to Terrance and threw Joseph and Turner under the bus, Terrance nominated Joseph next to Turner. Despite a wall yeller attempting to paint the target on Kyle's back and Terrance putting up Kyle as a replacement nominee, Joseph was evicted by a vote of 2-0 later that week.

Despite Joseph's eviction, Taylor was able to make her way to the final two through her relationships with Alyssa Snider, Brittany Hoopes, Matt Turner, and Monte Taylor. In the end, Taylor won the game in an 8-1 vote, including getting Joseph's vote, making her the winner of Big Brother 24 (US).

Taylor and Joseph both received 2 votes to evict over the course of the game.
Joseph was the lowest-placing member while Taylor was the highest-placing member of The Leftovers.
The pair officially started dating following the end of the season in September 2022, but separated amicably in April 2023.
