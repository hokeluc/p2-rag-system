Team America was a twist featured in Big Brother 16 (US), where viewers were given the power to select three houseguests to form a secret alliance controlled by America.

Joey Van Pelt was the initial member chosen, followed by Donny Thompson. After Joey's early eviction, Derrick Levasseur and Frankie Grande were both added to complete the alliance. Because Joey was evicted before the alliance was fully established, an additional member was brought in to fill her spot. It remains uncertain if Donny was ever made aware that Joey had originally been part of Team America.

Shortly before Frankie's eviction, both he and Derrick were summoned to the Diary Room and informed that if either of them won the game, that winner would receive a $50,000 bonus in addition to the standard $500,000 grand prize.
