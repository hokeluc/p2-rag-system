The Professors was an alliance on Big Brother 25 (US) that formed before the first eviction. The alliance included Bowie Jane Ball, Cirie Fields, Felicia Cannon, Hisam Goueli, Izzy Gleicher, Mecole Hayes and Red Utley. The seven person alliance was formed after the creation of the Bye Bye Bitches, which is a sub alliance with five members.

The Professors were formed following Reilly Smedley's reign as HOH. The Professors consisted of the older houseguests and had one goal in mind, to take out the alliance that dominated Week 1, The Handful/Family Style. Felicia brought Bowie, Cirie, Izzy, and Mecole together to form the Bye Bye Bitches alliance, and they later recruited Hisam and Red, and they formed The Professors.

After Hisam won the Power of Veto in Weeks 1 & 2 and won the HOH in Week 2, The Professors, specifically Izzy, Cirie, and Felicia, felt like Hisam was too much of a dictator and competition threat. After Felicia won HOH in Week 3, all but Hisam defected from The Professors, and Felicia backdoored him in a 11-0 vote, marking the end of the alliance.
