The F.A.P., also known as Four American People, or For the American People, and previously F**k-ass People, was an alliance on Big Brother 28 (US).

Whilst the alliance did not work together initially, all members were connected through their friendships or other alliances. Barrett and Drew were members of the large alliance The Toolshed and the sub-alliance within called The Crossovers as well as having a final two together. Melody and Mallory also created the Giggle Girls together alongside Lyric Medeiros. Melody and Drew were in the alliances 4 Seasons and The Court Jesters together, although the latter was not loyal to either. The pair also had a perceived "flirtmance", a final 2, Melodrew, and would stay up together talking after everyone else had gone to bed.

In week 4, Drew and Melody were nominated by HOH Haley Thogmartin due to their close bond, and Drew was targeted by his own alliance, including Barrett, with Melody as backup target. However, on day 30, the Icons ultimately flipped the vote to save Drew, and then again on day 31, to save Melody by sending Lyric home and blindsiding several members of the house.

The quartet then began working together under the nickname "F**k-ass People" which they had been using as a nickname to insult the Red Corner alliance. At the end of week 5, they changed the name to "For the American People" during a rock ceremony. The group, pushed by Drew and Barrett, aligned with The Icons during week 5, promising protection in the coming weeks. The group also hoped to target Kamu Kirk.

When Yash won HoH in week 6, F.A.P. worried that Melody was in danger. Drew spent several hours in the HOH room with Kamu and Yash, helping keep the nominations off F.A.P. although Melody and Barrett were the two possible replacement nominations. The quartet also organized a system to attempt to keep Haley on the block so they could vote her off instead of Yash's intended targets of Dee and Angela. Mallory spoke to Yash first, then Drew and Melody together, keeping them in good faith with Yash and his alliance.

Before the week 6 veto ceremony, F.A.P. officially merged with the Crossovers to create the Mosh Pit alliance. Devens used the DPoV on Dee and replaced her with Kamu/

During the Mosh Pit creation, Angela mentioned that she had previously been working with Barrett and Drew, worrying Mallory and Melody that they were not loyal to F.A.P.. This was exacerbated by the pair spending time with other members of the Mosh Pit. Yash Patel begins campaigning to the members of F.A.P. on Kamu's behalf, focusing on Mallory and Melody. Melody worries about drawing a line in the sand with Yash and his alliance, and begins to suggest saving Kamu, which Mallory ultimately agrees to. They questioned Drew about his loyalty to the icons, but he continued to deny that The Crossovers existed. The pair also agreed that Barrett was untrustworthy due to his close relationship with Dee.

Ultimately, the pair decided to betray both F.A.P. and the Mosh Pit and vote for Kamu to stay, but Drew successfully convinced them to remain with the original plan. However, the following day, they changed their minds again and decided to keep Kamu. Devens and Dee threatened both Drew and Barrett, promising to expose F.A.P. to the house if they did not convince them to evict Kamu. After further campaigning from both sides, Melody and Mallory decided to split their votes so that nobody would know which member evicted Kamu. However, they ultimately decided to just evict Kamu and blame it on Drew for convincing them. After Angela won the blockbuster, the pair approached Taylor and told her they had to evict Kamu, saying they could not convince Drew to keep him. Ultimately, Kamu was evicted with only LaTrice voting to evict Haley. Melody and Mallory publicly blamed their decision to evict Kamu on Drew.

Despite F.A.P. still being an alliance, it remained only Drew's priority as all members were suspicious of one another. Mallory began openly speaking about wanting to target Dee, drawing further ire from the Icons. Prior to the HoH competition, Drew pulled the other members of F.A.P. aside and begged them to win the HoH competition, believing they would be targeted for being an obvious quartet.

Dee won the next HoH after Barrett and Mallory threw it to her. She decided to target Drew, but decided to confront him so she would have support for turning on their alliances. She gathered the Mosh Pit and confronted Drew, but he did not admit to betraying the icons, as he did not. Yash joined the meeting and lied about Drew being the one to convince him to nominate Dee and Angela. After the others had left, Barrett claimed that Drew never warned him about being the replacement nomination, and encouraged Dee to target him. Melody and Mallory also spoke to Yash, who continued to lie and blame Drew for his nominations the prior week. Drew won the Time Capsule of the week, but was unsuccessful in attempting to leverage it for safety. Barrett grew worried about himself due to being the only member of the alliance to not receive America's vote.

However, at the nomination ceremony, Drew successfully diverted the target off him regaining both the Icons and F.A.P.'s trust, instead making the target Taylor. Drew, however, became frustrated by the Icons dismissal of him and approached Barrett with his desire to make F.A.P. their number one priority, which Barrett reluctantly agreed to consider. Devens pretended to overhear the pair, prompting them to try damage control and backtracking.

After LaTrice won veto, the Icons decided to turn on the Mosh Pit and target Mallory to weaken F.A.P. They told Drew and Barrett of this, but insisted she was just a pawn. Barrett was annoyed, but ultimately agreed to this plan, and whilst Drew pushed back more he felt like he could not defend her without endangering himself. Mallory and Melody were infuriated when informed of this, but also accepted it, as Taylor was still the target and was unlikely to win blockbuster.

Eventually, Drew was informed that Mallory was the main target and he insisted that Barrett be informed as well. Barrett was infuriated when told and proceeded to tell Mallory and Melody about every other alliance that he and Drew were in, with the goal of convincing them to target Drew. He also told Drew that they could not turn on the Icons as Drew had suggested, encouraging him to remain with the Crossovers. Mallory was enraged upon learning she was the target to the point she taped Dee's name tag to a pillow and punched it repeatedly as well as hysterically screaming in the diary room. However, F.A.P.'s plan to evict Drew even next to Taylor failed as he won blockbuster, leading to Mallory's eviction.

Drew remained unaware that F.A.P. had turned on him, whilst Barrett and Melody still plotted to get him evicted. During the medieval twist, Drew chose Barrett to keep him safe, leading to Barrett picking Yash and ensuring Haley's eviction. Drew won the next HoH and made his intentions to target the trio of LaTrice, Taylor, and Yash clear, annoying Melody and Barrett who believed he was a coward and they denounced him to that trio. However, when Yash won veto and Drew refused to consider Melody as a renomination, it became clear he would like nominate an Icon. Drew wanted to nominate Dee, but nobody including Barrett supported the idea, so Drew nominated Devens instead, as he volunteered. Barrett and Melody planned to betray Drew by evicting Devens, and lied to him about this despite Drew suspecting something was happening. However, Devens won the blockbuster so LaTrice was evicted instead.

In week 9, Barrett won HoH and immediately promised F.A.P. safety with the intention of nominating all three Icons with one of Dee and Devens being evicted. Barrett told Drew about the Icons targeting Drew since week 2, about him being the initial target in week 4, about F.A.P. intending to vote Drew out no matter what, and about intending to evict Devens on Drew's HoH. Drew was stunned by this information, but Barrett pledged loyalty to Drew and agreed to pick Drew if he was chosen in the veto picks, so they could take down Angela and renominate Yash. However, when Barrett was chosen, he instead betrayed Drew and chose Yash. Drew was upset by this, feeling completely betrayed by Barrett, and confronted him about this in the backyard, asking why Barrett did not just tell him he wanted the nominations to stay the same, Barrett stated he believed Drew would choose the Icons over him and Melody, frustrating Drew further.

Barrett and Melody antagonised Drew by cuddling with each other on Barrett's HoH bed whilst Drew awkwardly watched from the chair. This happened on multiple nights, but Melody ultimately stopped when Drew spoke to her privately and expressed how he felt uncomfortable. Barrett, Melody, and Drew created The Ceasefire alongside Yash and Taylor with the goal of evicting the Icons, but Drew immediately understood that it was fake and Yash and Taylor would target him at the first opportunity, whilst Barrett and Melody thought it was real. During the veto ceremony, Yash did not use it and the Icons took their speeches to instead threaten Barrett.

This situation led to Barrett retreating to his HoH room and rarely coming downstairs for the rest of the week. Meanwhile, Melody and Drew continued to reconcile and became more physically affectionate, leading to gossiping from the other houseguests. Ultimately, Yash and Taylor betrayed the Ceasefire by evicting Angela over Dee.

Yash won the HoH and due to the BB Bribe had to nominate F.A.P., which he justified by fabricating a story that Drew was the reason he betrayed them. Despite Drew voting to evict Dee, Barrett and Melody believed Yash over him and agreed to evict Drew if he remained on the block. Yash's lies about the bribe were not believed by Drew, but left Barrett and Melody with the hope Yash would nominate Devens over them, which he did not.

During the pressure cooker veto, Melody dropped second and spent her time in the house openly rooting against Drew, which annoyed Devens and Dee. Eventually, only Drew and Yash remained, and Drew convinced Yash to throw him the veto with the agreement that Drew evict Barrett if he remained on the block and Melody if he won the blockbuster, as well as not nominate Yash on his next HoH. Barrett and Melody were upset that Drew had won safety and openly expressed it to everyone including him. Devens told Drew his belief that Melody was using him and only pretending to be romantically interested. Drew also told Yash that he would not be evicting Melody if Barrett won the blockbuster, but that it should not matter as Yash could just break the tie.

Ultimately, Melody won her first ever competition with the blockbuster, leaving Barrett on the block where she was the only vote to save him, ending F.A.P. as only the duo of Melodrew remained.

Every member of F.A.P. was evicted when Taylor Brown was the other nominee and Rick Devens was a vote to evict them.
